Update Aquatic – Dive in! is a trailer promoting the release of Update Aquatic in Bedrock Edition.


== Summary ==
Riding boats, Alex and Steve drink their potions of water breathing as they are surrounded by dolphins. They swim across some warm ocean ruins, surrounded by various aquatic mobs, before moving to a colder ocean and toward a shipwreck. There, Alex opens a chest and gets a treasure map which both use to get to the buried chest. Along the way, they swim along a canyon full of whirlpools and drowned. While following Alex, Steve gets trapped in a whirlpool, and their friend comes to rescue them with their trident. Once they saved Steve from the drowned, the duo get to the shore of a taiga biome where they find the buried chest with a heart of the sea inside. With it, they construct a conduit as part of a larger build beneath the ocean.
