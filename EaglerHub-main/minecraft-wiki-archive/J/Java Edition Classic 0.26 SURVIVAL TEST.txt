0.26 SURVIVAL TEST is a version of Survival Test released on October 24, 2009, at roughly 00:13 UTC.


== Additions ==


=== Blocks ===
  Bookshelf

Currently impossible to obtain without a pre-edited map.
Can be broken by explosions.
Drops nothing when broken.
  Bricks

Currently unobtainable.
  Block of iron
  Moss stone
  TNT

10 are given to the player upon world generation.
When activated, a TNT block is replaced with an entity called primed TNT that creates an explosion after two seconds.
Primed TNT is affected by gravity.
Players have to hit TNT to prime the block.
Explosions would not ignite TNT, instead they blew it up like any other block.[verify for Java Edition]
  Stone slabs

 Two can be stacked in the space of one block.
 This stacked form has a hotbar equivalent, though it can never be obtained through normal gameplay.


=== Mobs ===
 Spiders

Hostile mob.
 Initially had a different texture during development.
Drop 0-2 brown mushrooms upon death.
Reward the player with 10 points upon death.[verify for Java Edition]


=== General ===
Options

Frame rate limiter.
Disabled by default.
Before this update, the frame rate limiter was automatically enabled by default, and it was not set as an option.
Particles

Added splash, rain and smoke.
Rain

Created by pressing F5.
Textures

 Added a cobweb texture to terrain.png. The cobweb block itself does not exist, and the texture is unused.


== Changes ==


=== Blocks ===
 Block of gold

Changed texture from .
Inventory sprite changed from  to .
Coal ore

Now drops 1–3 stone slabs when broken.
 Gold ore

Changed texture from .
Inventory sprite changed from  to .
Now drops 1–3 blocks of gold when broken.
Iron ore

Now drops 1–3 blocks of iron when broken.
Stone

Now drops cobblestone when broken.


=== Mobs ===
 Creepers

Changed texture from .
Now show explosion particles upon death.
Cannot destroy dirt-type blocks.
Can no longer destroy stone blocks.


=== Gameplay ===
Controls

Pressing B no longer places a sign.
Damage

The player can now deal damage faster.


=== General ===
Light

Blocks immediately on top of water no longer cause a shadow on its surface.
Hitboxes

Shrunk the hitbox of all plants.
Menu screens

Gradient for backgrounds was disabled.
This means that the death screen is no longer tinted red, and the menu screens are no longer tinted gray.


== Removals ==


=== Non-mob entities ===
Signs

Removed.


== Bugs ==
5 bugs reported

Old saved worlds from previous versions might not work.[dubious]
Slabs can't be placed under slabs without them collapsing into a full block.
The top slab texture is invisible if there is a block directly above.
Placing a block on a slab causes it to become a double-slab.
Killing a spider during rain crashes the game.
