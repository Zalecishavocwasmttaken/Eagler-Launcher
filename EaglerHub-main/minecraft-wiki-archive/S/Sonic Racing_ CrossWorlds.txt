Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds (Japanese: ソニックレーシング クロスワールド) is a kart racing video game published by Sega.
Minecraft content was added to the game on October 8, 2025  as part of its Season Pass, which is also included in the Digital Deluxe Edition.


== Background ==
Sonic Racing is a series part of the larger Sonic the Hedgehog franchise that features various Sega and third party characters racing against one another in karts on various differently themed courses. Its first entry, Sonic and Sega All-Stars Racing, was released in 2010. Announced at the tail end of the Fearless: Year of Shadow, Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds is the fourth installment of the series and was released on September 25, 2025. The game features Minecraft content as paid downloadable content, as well as other guest characters such as Ichiban Kasuga and Goro Majima from Like a Dragon, Joker from Persona, and NiGHTS from NiGHTS into Dreams, among others.

==== Red Rings locations ====
Red Rings first appeared in Sonic Colors as a collectible objects. A total of 5 per stage can be collected.
In Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds, they can only be found exclusively in the Grand Prix Mode and can only be seen during in Lap 1, and the Final Lap section. The Grand Prix Final Round only disables these collectibles due to having all three tracks being linked to each others.

At the Boost Pad on the left side after the last Boost Ramp on Lap 1
Inside the Nether section at the first ramp on Lap 1
During the Final Lap where creepers destroyed the road to a new path
Inside the mineshafts at the right side of the second railroad on Final Lap
Inside the End Section near at the end of the Flight on Final Lap

A Perfect Score only can be obtained once. Getting a Clear Score only gives the player 25 tickets.


=== Music ===
A total of 12 Minecraft Soundtracks are included in this game, with 7 being remixes of the 5 original Soundtracks, while the original versions are also included in the game.
These tracks can also be heard in the game jukebox.
