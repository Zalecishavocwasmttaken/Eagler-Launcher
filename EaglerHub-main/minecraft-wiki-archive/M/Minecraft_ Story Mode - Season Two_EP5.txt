
=== Chapter 1 ===
The group (Jesse, Petra, Jack, Nurm, Lluna, Radar/Binta, Cam and Kent) make their way to Beacontown underground through the Cave of Wonders, in which they observe the town in its "twisted" form.


=== Chapter 2 ===
Upon arriving, the group attempt to sneak to the map shop, but have to hide as they are nearly caught by "Jesse" (Romeo). "Jesse" then blows up Champion City or kills Xara, depending on player choices from the previous episode. Jesse then takes Olivia and Axel with the group to the map shop.
In the map shop, Olivia and Axel are relieved that the Admin is not the actual Jesse, but are concerned that people would turn on Jesse. Nonetheless, the group is hopeful in stopping the Admin. Jesse looks around the map shop and finds a lever, pulling it to reveal a pair of elytron. Suddenly, the group hears some clinking noises, and when Jesse investigates, it is revealed to be Nurm/Lluna (whoever was left behind at the Sunshine Institute). Ivor also shows up, and him and Jack have an awkward conversation. The group then make a plan to stop the Admin, which involves disguising as a member of the Fireworks Union, Axel and Olivia distracting the Admin, Binta/Radar giving Jesse a water bucket, and Petra and Jack waiting in the Order Hall. Jesse then removes his armor to disguise himself as Romeo and heads outside to begin the plan.


=== Chapter 3 ===
Outside, Jesse tries his best to blend into town, but is nearly caught twice as Romeo checks on the townspeople. Nonetheless, Jesse is able to obtain the Fireworks Union suits. That night, Jesse, Jack, Petra, and Binta/Radar enter the JesseCon backstage, and use the Fireworks Union materials to craft some firework rockets, allowing Jesse to use the elytra to fly toward the floating tower.


=== Chapter 4 ===
At the top of the floating tower, Jesse uses the water bucket from Binta/Radar to make a waterfall, allowing Jack and Petra to come along.
Inside the tower, the three are met by the new Ocelots (consisting of Lukas, Stampy Cat, Stacy Plays, and other Beacontown townspeople). Lukas mistakes the real Jesse for the Admin at first, but Jesse is able to convince Lukas into believing otherwise.


=== Chapter 5 ===
The group then make it up to the Admin's room, where Romeo greets them. After Jesse has a chitchat with him, Romeo thinks that he can do better "next time", so he decides to cover Jesse's world in bedrock. The group barely make it through the portal to the Terminal Space.
At the Terminal Space, the group find numerous artifacts from their past adventures and use the word of passage to obtain a gold gauntlet, which is meant to strip the Admin of its powers.


=== Chapter 6 ===
After an intense battle with Romeo, Jesse gets the upper hand and strips him of his powers, subsequently dissolving the Terminal Space and stopping the world from being covered in bedrock. Jesse can choose whether to take Romeo with them, or leave him behind, and the group make it back to Beacontown through the portal.
Back in Beacontown, Jesse and the others do everything they can to rebuild Beacontown. The player then has the choice of staying in Beacontown or hitting the road.
Should the player decide to stay in Beacontown, Radar will inform Jesse that they received a letter from Aiden about "redeeming himself" and a note from "W.P." telling them to "look out".
Likewise, if the player decides to hit the road, the player can choose whether Radar can run Beacontown or not. Jesse then decides to have a race with Petra.
