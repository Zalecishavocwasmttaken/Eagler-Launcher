Minecraft: Stonesword Saga is a series of children's books written by Nick Eliopulos and illustrated by Alan Batson and Chris Hill. The series is a sequel to Minecraft: Woodsword Chronicles, following Theo Grayson as he joins the heroes of the prior books.
