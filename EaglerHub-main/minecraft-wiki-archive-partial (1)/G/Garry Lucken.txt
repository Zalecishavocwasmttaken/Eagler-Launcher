Garry Lucken, also known as Army of Trolls, is a Pixel artist notable for his work on the Skin packs in Minecraft. Lucken's work includes the City Folk and Town Folk skin packs in Pocket Edition and has assisted with various Skin packs in Legacy Console Edition.
