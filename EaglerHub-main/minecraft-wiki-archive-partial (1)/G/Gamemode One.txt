Gamemode One is a content creator for Bedrock Edition and an official Minecraft partner, responsible for developing many add-ons and maps for the Marketplace. They have jointly made the 2023 Mob Vote Event and the Minecraft Legends Live Event with Mojang, alongside several other official collaborations. They currently have 17 packs on the marketplace.
