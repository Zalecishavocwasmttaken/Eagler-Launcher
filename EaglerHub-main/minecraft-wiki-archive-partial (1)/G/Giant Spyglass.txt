The Giant Spyglass (巨大望遠鏡, Kyodai Bōenkyō?) is a massive spyglass, created using the Imagination Mod Power in Minecraft: The Manga.

Appearance
The Giant Spyglass resembles a regular spyglass, but significantly larger.

Functionality
Using a technique called "Solar Beam" (ソーラービーム, Sōrā Bīmu?), which reflects and focuses sunlight, the Giant Spyglass can be used to burn undead mobs that are underwater.

Crafting recipe

IngredientsCrafting recipe

Amethyst Shard + Copper Ingot

History
While exploring a cave, Nico White and Gray fell into an underground lake. They were swarmed by a group of Drowned, but managed to escape due to the help of a nearby axolotl; to pay the axolotl back, Nico had Platinum punch a hole to the surface and crafted the Giant Spyglass, using it to focus the sunlight and burn the Drowned.
