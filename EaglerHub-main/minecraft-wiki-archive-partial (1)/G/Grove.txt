A grove is a snowy forest biome that often generates on the sides of mountains. Similar to the snowy taiga, it is filled with spruce trees but the ground is covered with snow blocks and occasional powder snow patches.

Generation
Groves generate as mountain slope biomes, similar to meadows and cherry groves, but at higher altitudes and not as plateaus. They can also generate standalone on smaller peaks, sometimes measuring only a few blocks in size.
They can generate in the coldest to medium temperature zones with higher humidity. They often border jagged peaks and frozen peaks as mountain biomes and meadows in valleys, and they are replaced by snowy slopes in lower humidity. Groves commonly border medium forested biomes like forests, birch forests, taigas, dark forests, and old growth taigas.
In the coldest temperature zones, groves extend to entire valleys and hills around mountains, replacing snowy taigas and taigas, and also bordering frozen rivers and frozen oceans with tall cliffs or stony shores. Snowy taigas and regular taigas can sometimes generate as thin strips replacing rivers in highland snowy slopes.
Because groves are mountain biomes, they are almost guaranteed to have deep dark underneath, and commonly also dripstone caves or lush caves.

Description
A grove creates a forest of spruce trees on the sides of a mountain, reminiscent of a snowy taiga but the surface is covered snow blocks above a dirt ground, with snow layers on top. All spruce trees grow on a single block of dirt replacing snow blocks, with a slightly lower density than in snowy taigas.
Strips of powder snow occasionally replace snow blocks and the dirt below, where trees do not generate. Fallen trees and dying trees do not generate here.
Rabbits, snowy wolves, and snow foxes can spawn in this biome. Like other mountain biomes, emerald ore and infested stone can generate in stone under the ground. Pillager outposts are the only structures that generate here.
With Vibrant Visuals, groves use default environmental settings with a coolish color grading, making the biome appear colder blue. They use cold sunlight, which is slightly brighter at dusk and dawn.
