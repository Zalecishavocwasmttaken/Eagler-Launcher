Gray is a human adventurer from Minecraft: The Manga who has a Cursed Mod that makes him look like a Zombie. He travels the world trying to find a cure to his ability, joining Nico on his journey. Unlike the rest of his village, he kept his intelligence even after being transformed.

Personality
Gray is calm and cool-headed the majority of the time, preferring to pause and make a plan instead of jumping into something. When he is in danger, he quickly becomes frightened and runs, while tears fly off his face. When he sees treasure and other valuables, however, he grows greedy and wants to do whatever it takes to get it.
