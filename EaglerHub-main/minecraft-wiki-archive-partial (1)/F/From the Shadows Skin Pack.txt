Key art
From the Shadows Skin Pack is a skin pack created by 4J Studios, launching in celebration of the 2018 Halloween season. It contains 16 detailed skins (referred to as "HD Skins") themed around creatures of horror.

Name

Render

Description

Notes

Gothica

An eerie monochromatic woman with glowing eyes

Free to use without purchasing the pack.

Bulbo

Appears to be some kind of hideously mutated humanoid rat

Splinter

A rotting piece of wood, hence the name splinter

Free to use without purchasing the pack.

See No Evil

A devil in a man's clothes, with holes in the eyes so he can't see

Mush

A body-horrific creature, somewhat reminiscent of John Carpenter’s The Thing

Yokai

resembles a Nukekubi, a variant on the more popular rokurokubi with a completely detachable head; also bears a resemblance to the stereotypical Japanese ghost women found in works like The Ring and The Grudge

Harbinger

A skeletal bird-like entity

Patchwork

A creature made of multiple creatures fused together

Poppy

Resembles a ball-jointed doll missing part of her head, revealing her to be filled with some kind of green substance

Speak No Evil

Another devil wearing man's clothes, with a messed-up mouth that doesn't let him speak

Wick

Resembles an anthropomorphic candle

Torn

Either a torn bloody human, or a torn creature eating a human skull

Vein

Resembles an animate cardiovascular system with some skeletal fragments

Parasite

A lab worker with a parasite infecting him

Hear No Evil

Another devil wearing man's clothes, this time with holes in his ears so he can't hear

Lurk

Resembles a swamp monster combined with elements of Cthulhu
