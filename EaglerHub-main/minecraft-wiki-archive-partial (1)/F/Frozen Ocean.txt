The frozen ocean is an icy variant of ocean covered by icebergs. The water's surface is partially covered with ice, where snowfall may occur and polar bears can spawn.

Generation
Frozen oceans generate as shallow ocean biomes in the frozen temperature zones, near snowy plains, snowy taigas, regular taigas, and ice spikes. They may be separated from land biomes by snowy beaches, non-frozen stony shores, and frozen rivers, though they may also border land biomes directly. They can generate close to mountains with groves and snowy slopes forming tall oceanic cliffs.
Frozen oceans border their deep variant far from land, and cold oceans near higher temperatures.

Description
The separate colors of each ocean variant in Java Edition
Unlike other oceans, frozen oceans do not have a gravel seabed and directly expose stone and variants.‌[Bedrock Edition  only] Neither seagrass nor kelp generates on the seafloor, leaving it entirely barren. The surface is widely covered with ice, while large icebergs made of snow blocks, packed ice, and a bit of blue ice frequently generate.
Shipwrecks and cold ocean ruins generate here, often inside icebergs. Occasional islands in frozen oceans are also covered with stone, however, some patches of grass blocks generate in between.
Fewer mobs spawn here than in any other ocean variant, only cod and salmon.‌[BE  only] On the surface, however, polar bears and rabbits may also spawn in Bedrock Edition, while some skeletons are replaced by strays.
Frozen oceans have varying temperatures across their landscapes. Colder patches generate snowfall and freeze water on any altitude, while some patches only allow snowfall above Y=120±8, creating rainfall and unfrozen water on the ocean's surface.
With Vibrant Visuals, frozen oceans use default environmental settings with a strong cold color grading, making the biome appear colder blue. They use cold sunlight, which is slightly stronger at dusk and dawn.
