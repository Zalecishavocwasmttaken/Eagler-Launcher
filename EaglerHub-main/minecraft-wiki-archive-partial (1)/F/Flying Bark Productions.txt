Flying Bark Productions is an Australian entertainment and animation studio based in Sydney, that has been a subsidiary of the Belgian children's entertainment production company Studio 100 since 2008. They are currently in the process of animating the upcoming Minecraft animated series with WildBrain Studios for Netflix.
