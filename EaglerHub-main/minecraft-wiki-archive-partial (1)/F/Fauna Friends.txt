Key art
Fauna Friends is a skin pack that includes 36 skins themed around animals. It is available through a promo code obtained by purchasing an eligible, Minecraft themed product at Walmart between June 24, 2024 and August 24, 2024. The promo codes were redeemable until February 28, 2025.
