Bartering
When given a gold ingot, piglins have a ~8.53% (40⁄469) chance to barter a single fire charge.

Natural occurrence
Main article: Ominous Trial Spawner § Projectile spawning
During ominous trials, each ominous trial spawner has a 1⁄5 chance to dispense a fire charge on top of either a player or a mob it spawns. The fire charge is directly dispensed into the world and cannot be obtained in the inventory.

Usage
Dispenser
Dispensed small fireball
Fire charges can be used as ammunition in dispensers. When a fire charge is fired from a dispenser, it shoots a small fireball. However, a player cannot simply use a fire charge while holding it to shoot a small fireball.

Fire
When used, a fire charge instantly places a fire, similar to flint and steel. But, unlike flint and steel, the fire charge is consumed in the process. It can prime TNT and light nether portals, campfires, candles, cakes with candles, and creepers‌[Java Edition  only].
