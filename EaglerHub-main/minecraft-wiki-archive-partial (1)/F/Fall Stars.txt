Fall Stars is an upcoming special MC Championship event in collaboration with Mojang Studios that reveals the name of the third game drop of 2026. It is hosted on twitch.tv/minecraft by Couriway and jojosolos on 2026-09-05T19:00Z. The competition includes previous MC Championship winners.
