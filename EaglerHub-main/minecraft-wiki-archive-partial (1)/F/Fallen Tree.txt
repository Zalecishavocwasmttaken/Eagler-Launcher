Generation
A fallen birch with mushrooms
Regular oak, spruce, birch, super birch, jungle, and poplar​[upcoming: Third Drop 2026] trees can generate as fallen trees. Any variant trees such as fancy oaks, 2×2 variants, or trees with bee nests are not able to be replaced by fallen trees.
Therefore, fallen trees can be found in all biomes where their standing tree variants grow, except the following:

Meadow
Grove
Bamboo Jungle
Flower Forest
Has fallen birch trees but not fallen oak trees.
Fallen trees consist of a single upright log, making the stump, and a log (composed of a row of log blocks) lying on its side, making the trunk. The trunk is often found 1–2 blocks from the stump, but does not always generate. Fallen trees have the following trunk lengths:

 Oak: 4–7 logs
 Birch: 5–8 logs
 Super Birch: 5–15 logs
 Spruce: 6–10 logs
 Jungle: 4–11 logs
 Poplar: 4–7 logs
Fallen spruce and birch trees can generate with 1–2 red mushrooms on top of it; fallen poplar and birch trees can generate with 1–2 brown mushrooms on top of it.[verify] The stump has a 75% chance to generate with vines growing on its sides, except fallen spruce and birch trees. Fallen poplar trees can also generate with shelf mushrooms on their trunk.
