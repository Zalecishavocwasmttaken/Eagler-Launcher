The Follower's Cape is a cape that was given out as part of the 15 Days of Minecraft celebrations alongside the Purple Heart Cape and the 15th Anniversary Cape.

Appearance
The cape accompanies a white creeper face on a dark background which emulates a design similar to the TikTok logo, an electronic wave effect. On either side of the cape are two blue and red strips, acting as the border.

Distribution
Players could receive this cape by redeeming a code, which can be obtained by watching any Minecraft livestream on TikTok. This event started on May 18, 2024 and ended on June 18, 2024.
Depending on the user's region, there were additional requirements, such as watching for some time, or having to type "minecraft" in the chat.
This task could not be completed normally in regions where either TikTok or its rewards program were unavailable. Players in these regions could obtain the cape by using a proxy/VPN, traveling to regions where they were available, or receiving a code from another player.

Trivia
Despite being announced earlier, this cape and the Purple Heart Cape were the focus of the fourth day of the 15 Days of Minecraft celebrations.
The original article and the redemption page stated that codes for this cape and the Purple Heart Cape would be disabled on July 30, 2024. However, as of May 2026, codes for these capes are known to still work.
