This article is about the list of families.   For the target selector argument family, see Target selectors § Selecting targets by family.



This feature is exclusive to Bedrock Edition. 

A family, or entity family, is a unique technical label for Bedrock Edition, which specifies the family to which an entity belongs. Families are defined by the minecraft:type_family component in the entities' behavior files. This component is necessary to give the mob the ability to ride. These labels are used for target selection in commands and entity behaviors.
