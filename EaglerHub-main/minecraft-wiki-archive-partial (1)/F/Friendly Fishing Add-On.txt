Friendly Fishing Add-On is an add-on developed by 50 different Marketplace Partners in collaboration with Conservation International. 100% of net proceeds from each sale along with $100,000 will be donated to Conservation International to support global conservation efforts.

Trivia
The blobfish depicted in the add-on is based on a viral image of a specific blobfish, Mr Blobby, that had a distorted appearance from being forcefully taken out of its native environment.
In the credits for the add-on on Minecraft.net, Aria Creations and Architechts Edge are mistakenly labeled as Architechts and EdgeAriaCreations.
