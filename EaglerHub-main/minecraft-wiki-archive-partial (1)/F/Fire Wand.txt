A fire wand is an item only present in 25w14craftmine. It uses the blaze rod texture with an enchantment glint. It is the counterpart of the wind wand.

Obtaining
The fire wand is only obtainable (without commands) by getting the upgrade "Leaf", but by default this upgrade is obfuscated. Killing a blaze makes this upgrade readable.

Usage
By default, the fire wand shoots out a ghast fireball when used.
