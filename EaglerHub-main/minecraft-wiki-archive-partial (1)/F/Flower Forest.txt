A flower forest is a variant of the forest filled with a much larger variation and frequency of flowers. Rabbits spawn here instead of wolves and bee nests generate much more often.

Generation
Flower forests generate in medium temperature ranges and in the lowest humidity. This means that they are rarer than regular forests and never border them.
They are most commonly bordered by plains and meadow biomes or separated from sunflower plains, which generate in place of a flower forest in high weirdness, by rivers. Generating in all types of terrain, they can either border windswept hills or mountains.

Description
Flower forests have both oak and birch trees. They generate with a wide array of flowers, along with bee nests on 5% of trees.
Abandoned camps sometimes generate in flower forests.​[upcoming: Third Drop 2026]

Differences to regular forests
There are some key differences between regular forests and flower forests.

Flower forests have fewer trees and less grass. Instead, flower forests include a diverse variety of flowers. Leaf litter and bushes are completely absent.
The vast number of flowers makes this biome variant an excellent source of dyes. The following types of flowers can be generated in flower forests:

There is a 10× greater chance of trees generating a bee nest.
There is also a slight difference in mob spawning:
Wolves cannot spawn in flower forests.
Rabbits can spawn in flower forests.
Bees also occur 10× more often, as there is 10× higher chance of spawning a bee nest with a tree.
