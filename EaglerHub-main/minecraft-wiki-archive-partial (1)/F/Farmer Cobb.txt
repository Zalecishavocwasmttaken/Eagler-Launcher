Appearance
Cobb is a stylized, humanoid figure with peach skin, thick eyebrows, and spiky red hair, usually in three square points. His wardrobe consists of a green hat, sandals, a belt satchel, brown finger-less gloves, and turquoise overalls on top of a white muscle shirt. His body appears muscular, with noticeable abs and hair on his chest and legs.

Saved by the Bell
One morning while eating a carrot, Cobb started up a conversation with Flint, who asked if he had seen Rochelle. He says he thought she went with him to gather gunpowder, and Flint infers he forgot, before Rochelle turns up herself and thanks Flint for the help. Trouble brews when a creeper follows her into town and destroys the bell, with Cobb trying to survey the damage. He voices his concern for the town's safety, motivating Rochelle to go out and find a replacement. She returns with two, and Cobb shows his gratitude.
