The penguin was a proposed mob featured in the Mob Vote during Minecraft Live 2023, alongside the crab and armadillo, having been the first mob to be voted out. It would have spawned in stony shores, and would have been amphibious, living both in the water and on land. Although they were clumsy when walking and fell down occasionally, the penguins were excellent swimmers and could swim as fast as the dolphins. They gathered in groups.
The penguin could increase the speed of the player’s boat on water. It was never confirmed that this ability applied outside of water.
The penguin lost the vote with only 25.2% of the votes.

History
December 22, 2009
Penguins are suggested to Markus Persson on the TIGSource Forums. He declines, but considers adding a "penguin hat" as a "pretty nice easter egg."October 7, 2015
Penguins are mentioned in a developer Q&A for Pocket Edition.March 8, 2018
Jens Bergensten shoots down adding penguins in the Update Aquatic, as "not this time."October 5, 2023
Penguins are announced to be a candidate in the 2023 Mob Vote at Minecraft Live 2023.October 15, 2023
The penguin loses the vote to the armadillo.Bedrock EditionOctober 13, 2023
The 2023 Mob Vote Event begins, where players could vote for the penguin.December 9, 2024
The Eerie Mojang Office Party event server begins, featuring a hologram of a penguin in the Cloud Race minigame.
Trivia
The penguin's design is heavily based off of the crested penguins, known for having a pair of yellow crests.
