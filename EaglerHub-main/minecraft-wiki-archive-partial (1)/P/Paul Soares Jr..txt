Paul Soares Jr. is a YouTube creator who has made a lot of Minecraft videos starting from Java Edition Alpha in July 2010, becoming one of the first Minecraft YouTubers to achieve widespread popularity and pioneering the survival let's-play series genre.

In the Minecraft franchise
He contributed to several official Minecraft books such as Minecraft: Essential Handbook, Minecraft: Combat Handbook and Minecraft: The Official Annual 2014.
