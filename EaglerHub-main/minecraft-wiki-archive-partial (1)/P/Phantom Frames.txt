Phantom Frames is a tie-in DLC to The Garden Awakens, released under the Expect the unexpected campaign, as both an add-on for Bedrock Edition and a data pack for Java Edition.

Additions
Items
 Phantom Frames Add-On
Appears in the inventory upon starting the world or activating the add-on.
Gives an explanation of the add-on's features.
Mobs
  Merchant‌[BE  only]
Spawns during night.
Can be traded with, offering only magical paintings.
Entities
 Magical Painting
Crafted from a painting and an ender pearl‌[JE  only]
Requires 3×3 wall (similar to a painting) to be placed.
When interacted with, it teleports the player to a random place in the Overworld next to another magical painting, which teleports the player back
When first placed, it is purple, but after entered, it will show the biome it leads to
It drops itself when broken if it wasn't entered yet, but it drops nothing if it was already entered
Has a chance to show a mysterious, white-eyed figure appear for a short time before disappearing.
