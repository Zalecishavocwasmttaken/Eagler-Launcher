Spawning
A pet fox is spawned next to the player on mine start when the "Pet Fox" Player Unlock is active.

Drops
Pet foxes drop nothing when they are killed.

Behavior
Unlike normal foxes, it cannot pick up items; however, it will follow the player like a tamed wolf, teleporting if needed.
