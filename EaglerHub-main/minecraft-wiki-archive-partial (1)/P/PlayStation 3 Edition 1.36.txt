1.36  is a version of PlayStation 3 Edition released on October 5, 2016.  It is also known as the October 2016 Update.

Additions
1.36 block and item additions
Blocks
Banners
Have an automatic crafting option.
Uses default when Classic Crafting is on.
Beetroots
Bone block
Dirt path
Can be found in the Creative inventory.
End stone bricks
Frosted ice
Can be picked up with Silk Touch.
Magma block
Nether wart block
Red nether bricks
Items
Beetroot
Beetroot seeds
Beetroot soup
Mobs
Polar bear
World generation
Fossil
Igloo
Only generate in new worlds.
Gameplay
Enchantments

Added Frost Walker and Mending enchantments.
General
Sounds

Added sounds for witch, squid, snowman, armor stand, cow, horse, item frames, leash knots, and paintings.
Added a sound for milking cows.
DLC

Added the Chinese Mythology Mash-Up Pack.

Mini games

Added bone blocks, Nether wart blocks, red Nether brick, magma blocks and End stone bricks to Tumble.
Changes
Blocks
End portal frames

Added a particle effect when placing an eye of ender inside.
Huge mushrooms

Now have a chance of growing twice as tall as normal.
Stained clay

Are now named "Hardened Clay" (e.g., "White Hardened Clay").
Gold ore

Now generates in mesa biomes between Y=32 and Y=79.
Items
Shears

Are no longer able to be enchanted with Silk Touch.
World generation
Villages

Now spawn with grass paths.
Are no longer restricted by biome boundaries.
Now have bridges when generated over water.
There's now a small chance for villages to spawn as zombie villages
Farms now include beetroots.
Mineshafts

Now generate in mesa biomes - previously they did not spawn at all, even underground.[verify]
Loot chests

Updated loot for loot chests in dungeons, temples, mineshafts etc.
Plains and sunflower plains

Now have a chance to generate trees.
Mobs
Endermen

Now spawn in the Nether.
Elder guardians

Now drop dry sponges instead of wet sponges.
Pigs

Can be led and bred with potatoes and beetroots, in addition to carrots.
Skeletons

Now use the old sound for dying.
Gameplay
Health

Health regenerates faster when saturation is not empty and the player is not hungry: 1HP per half-second versus 1HP per 4 seconds.
Health regeneration increases exhaustion faster: now 4 exhaustion points (1 food point) per 1HP instead of 3 (3⁄4 food points).
Enchantments

Nerfed the Sharpness enchantment.
Sleeping

Changed the sleeping animation.
General
Updated all Texture and Mash-up Packs with new items & blocks.
Explosions no longer deal knockback to players, which are flying in Creative mode.
Replaced the "Show Description", "Show Ingredients" and "Show Inventory" tooltips in the crafting menu with "Info".

MCCE-2413 – Skin select issue

MCCE-2424 – Cannot unlock the time passing trophy
MCCE-2464 – Minecarts can get stuck on activated activator rails
