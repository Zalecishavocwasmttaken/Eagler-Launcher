A pet axolotl is the pet variant of an axolotl, only present in 25w14craftmine.

Spawning
A pet axolotl is spawned next to the player on mine start when the "Pet Axolotl" Player Unlock is active.

Drops
On death
Pet axolotls have no drops.

Behavior
Pet axolotls have the same AI behavior as axolotls.
