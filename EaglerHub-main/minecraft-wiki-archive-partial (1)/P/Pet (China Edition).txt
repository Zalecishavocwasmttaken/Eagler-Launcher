Behavior



This section needs expansion. 
You can help by expanding it.

A pet will come to the player when called upon with the "summon" button. It will not run away from the player and will follow them around like other tamed animals.
Players can interact with their pet by pressing the "play" button. This causes the pet to run away to chase a tennis ball before returning to the player. The player can also command the pet to "stay" like other tamed animals. A pet cannot be fed.
