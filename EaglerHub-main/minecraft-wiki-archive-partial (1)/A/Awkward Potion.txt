An awkward potion is a potion that provides no effects. It is used as the basis for brewing most other potions.

Lingering potion
In Bedrock Edition, a lingering awkward potion acts similar to the lingering water bottle, being able to extinguish burning entities and damage endermen, blazes, and snow golems, but without the ability to put out fires.
