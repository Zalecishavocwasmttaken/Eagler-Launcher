Marketplace releases
Key art for the map
Angry Birds
Main article: Angry Birds (map)
Released on April 26, 2022, Angry Birds is a DLC map based on the franchise of the same name. The map concerns a conflict between the Angry Birds and Bad Piggies, with several different birds being playable over the course of the story, each with unique abilities. Included in the map are ten skins based off the Angry Birds: Summer Madness television series, featuring both existing characters and new ones made for the program.

Angry Birds Pack
Main article: Content bundles
Continuing off the release of the DLC world, the Angry Birds Pack is a content bundle featuring it, eight character creator items, and  1,720 Minecoins.

Trivia
Prior to the announcement of The Angry Birds Movie 3, an article on Minecraft.net would allude to its existence, claiming it was releasing in Summer 2022. It would later be corrected.
During an official video on the Angry Birds TikTok where Red plays the game smash or pass, Steve was singled out as one of the characters he would "smash."
According to the Angry Birds official website, the events of the collaborations are a separate continuity from the films and considered part of the franchise's multiverse.
