A Minecraft Movie DLC Trailer (stylized as A MINECRAFT MOVIE DLC Trailer) is a trailer promoting the release of A Minecraft Movie DLC, showcasing three of the gang competing in Garrett's arcade games about their lives.

Summary
The camera pans through Game Over World, as Garrett walks over and proceeds to punch an arcade game, inviting the camera to play it. After a title card, the video cuts to Dawn, Henry, and Natalie in a canyon, gliding with elytra. As they gain altitude, Henry purposefully hits his sister, gaining the lead. However, he is quick to discover a troop of piglins and their ghast, facing his comeuppance as he halts in place. The scene cuts to another race, now in the badlands, before returning to the canyon, and then finally the real world. Natalie claps, before they decide to play another game.
Now in the First Night minigame, the three are surrounded by a mob of mobs. One of the heroes uses the buck-chuckets to defeat a zombie, before demoing turrets. Natalie blasts a saxophone on several zombies and vexes, while Henry places a mannequin of Steve, which successfully fools a zombie and pays the price. The three decide to play one final game, Henry hiding inside a plant in Woodland Mansion. He's quickly spotted by a vindicator, and ducks inside of a storage room, grabbing another saxophone to disarm an enderman. The siblings reach the end, claiming the Orb of Dominance.
Back in the real world, Dawn and Henry decide to return to their first game, trying to beat Garrett's high score.
