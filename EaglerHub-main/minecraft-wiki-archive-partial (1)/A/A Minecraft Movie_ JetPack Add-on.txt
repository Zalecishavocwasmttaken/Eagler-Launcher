Key art
A Minecraft Movie: JetPack Add-on is an official add-on made in promotion of A Minecraft Movie, which can be obtained by purchasing tickets to see the movie on its opening weekend (April 3 - April 7, 2025) through Fandango and select movie theater chains internationally. Redemption codes must be redeemed before December 31, 2025 at 11:59pm PT.

Additions
  JetPack
The jetpack is automatically given upon entering a world.
Crafted from 7 iron ingots, 1 block of redstone, and 1 gunpowder.
Has a battery life of 8 bars. It consumes 1 bar of battery every 2 seconds. When not in use, it automatically recharges every 0.5 tick. If the battery runs out while in use the jetpack will emit smoke and slowly descends the player back to the ground.
Provides an infinite amount of slow falling.

Ingredients
Crafting recipe

Iron Ingot +Block of Redstone +Gunpowder
