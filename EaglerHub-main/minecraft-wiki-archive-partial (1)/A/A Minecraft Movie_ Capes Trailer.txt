Summary
Within Midport Village, three heroes each take on a different activity. After a flash of Steve, he enters into a mine wearing the yearn cape. After harvesting A Minecraft Movie he leaps over a hole in a platform, meanwhile Dawn is wearing the home cape in a neighborhood. She buys from a weaponsmith, befriends a cat, and plays saxophone surrounded by llamas. Elsewhere in the region, Natalie has the menace cape and eats lava chicken, setting herself on fire to run over a bridge faster. She proceeds to fight against a piglin invasion at the town wall, before leaping in to battle them herself using the heat generated from her body. The three converge back at Steve's Lava Chicken and show off their capes.

Trivia
Instead of the NPCs from the original server, villagers, cats and llamas in the trailer use the original mobs, and thus their current textures at the time. Presumably this was done to make them easier to script.
