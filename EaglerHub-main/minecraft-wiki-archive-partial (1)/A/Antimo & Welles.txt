Antimo & Welles are a composing duo consisting of Skyler Barto (Antimo) and Andrew Arcadi (Welles). They are known for composing the music for Telltale Games' Minecraft: Story Mode and its Season Two.

Biography
Skyler Barto and Andrew Arcadi worked on music for Telltale Games' titles Minecraft: Story Mode and the cancelled Stranger Things project. Andrew Arcadi also composed music for The Walking Dead and The Wolf Among Us. The two later released Story Mode Archives on December 21, 2018, containing music created for Minecraft: Story Mode but not included in the game.
Andrew Arcadi later worked on AdHoc Studio's game Dispatch, with additional music by Skyler Barto. The game's music credits include both of them as composers, and it received a nomination for Music at the British Academy of Film and Television Arts Games Awards.
