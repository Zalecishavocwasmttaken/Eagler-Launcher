An azalea tree is a rare tree that can generate above a lush cave biome, marking the biome's presence aboveground. They generate with a root system underground into the cave beneath.

Appearance
Azalea trees are made of oak logs and (sometimes flowering) azalea leaves on the surface. Their trunks grow straight up then on a diagonal halfway up the tree. Naturally generated azalea trees come with a root system feature. Below the tree, rooted dirt replaces random blocks going all the way down to the lush caves where some hanging roots generate.

Generation
An azalea tree in a jungle
Azalea trees generate on any empty space above a lush cave exposed to the sky, if there is dirt or a supported block where they can grow on. This can sometimes produce jarring scenery, such as when the trees generate in a snowy taiga or mushroom fields.
Azalea trees can generate in water up to 2 blocks deep, similarly to swamp oaks. Unlike all other trees, however, the maximum depth is limited by the azalea's unique root system feature config rather than a surface_water_depth_filter placement modifier.

Planting

Azalea trees can be grown by using bone meal on azalea or flowering azalea. They do not automatically grow into an azalea tree, so bone meal must be used. Application of bone meal gives an azalea or flowering azalea a 45% chance to grow into an azalea tree, provided there is a 3×5×3 area above it that is clear of all blocks that are not leaves or logs.

Azalea trees requires a 3×3 column of unobstructed space at least 5 blocks above the sapling (6 blocks including the sapling itself). No horizontal clearance is needed at the base of the tree (an azalea planted in a hole 1 block deep can still grow).
Growing an azalea tree replaces the block below its trunk with rooted dirt. This only generates one block of rooted dirt and no hanging roots. Azalea trees do not need to be grown over a lush caves biome; they can grow anywhere, like saplings.
Azaleas and flowering azaleas produce identical trees with the same proportion of flowering leaves.
Azalea trees can grow underwater, just like mangrove trees.‌[Bedrock Edition  only]
Azalea trees always grow in one of the four cardinal directions with the extra branches offset by one to two blocks to the middle at the third to fifth log layer. They have two branches at the fifth block layer unless grown in a 5×5 cuboid.
