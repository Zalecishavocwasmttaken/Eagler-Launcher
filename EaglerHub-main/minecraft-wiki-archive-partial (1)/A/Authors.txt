Ian Flynn

Minecraft: Stories from the Overworld

An American-Canadian comic book writer, best known for his work on the Sonic the Hedgehog franchise. He served as the chief writer for the Archie Comics series starting with #160, and later returned to lead its successor at IDW Publishing. In 2022, he became the head writer of the games, beginning with Sonic Frontiers.
In 2025, he authored the Archie story Servers You Right!, an unofficial Minecraft parody.

Kazuyoshi Seto(瀬戸カズヨシ)

Minecraft: The Manga

A Japanese mangaka, best known for their work on Minecraft: The Manga. They debuted with the five-volume manga Namennayo! Nameneko Matakichi Saikyo Densetsu (2006-2009), before authoring Plamo Senki Aratame (2012-2013) and Game Senshi Bit! (2017-2020).
