Henrik Pettersson is the third artist hired to work on Caller’s Bane, and was hired in August 2011. He developed Crown and Council, a surprise game released on Steam for free on April 22, 2016.
On June 28, 2018, he announced his departure from Mojang to join Resolution Games.
