Summary
At night, Agent gets lost inside a huge maze with mysterious red lights, to represent the narrator's description of how frightening getting stuck is. Frightened, they almost notice an unknown figure with purple legs walking far, who always hides out of sight. Then, Agent finds a dark tunnel, deciding to ignore it out of fear. While searching for the exit, they find a candle, using more of them to mark the paths they used. Agent then finds themselves stuck in a path splitting into three, including two they recently used as marked by candles. As they walk along the third path, they find the exit along with a chest. The scene shifts to a demonstration of the map, which includes a scene in which Agent opens the chest and lots of emeralds overflow from it.
