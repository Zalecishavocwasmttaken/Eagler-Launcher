How to: Play in Survival or Creative Mode
Ready for a thrilling adventure or eager to express your artistic side? We have you covered, no matter what mood you are in. So, let's dive into the differences between survival and creative mode and see how each shapes your Minecraft world.
Teaches gamemode in Minecraft.

How to: Use Bedrock Controls
Every great journey begins with a single step. But how does one take the first step? That is the question. In this video, we'll go through the basic controls and keybindings of Minecraft: Bedrock Edition to teach you how to take that first step on your endless adventures into the Overworld.
Teaches player movements, sprinting, navigating inventory, dropping an item, changing controls, and more.

YouTube

How to: Craft and Use Different Items
It might not come as such a surprise for some that in a game called Minecraft, there is a whole bunch of stuff you can craft... maybe even an overwhelming amount of stuff. We are here to show you how to make the essentials and how they will help you on your blocky adventure.
Teaches the player crafting mechanics, using tools, and smelting. At first, get wood and craft the crafting table. Get a pickaxe, axe, and shovel. Use furnaces to smelt, and torches to provide light.

YouTube

How to: Prepare For the Night
In Minecraft, it's not just the stars that come out at night. Think less twinkle-twinkle and more bitey-bitey. So, it might be better to take a raincheck on that midnight hike and find some shelter. This video will show you how to prepare for your first spooky night in the Overworld.
Teaches the player to prepare for the nighttime by crafting a wooden sword and pickaxe, scavenging for food, and building a shelter.

YouTube

How to: Survive the First Night
Slumber parties might be really fun, but not if you bunk with zombies and creepers. So, when night comes, make sure to prevent any unexpected visitors. Let's take a look at how to secure your newly built home and how you can survive the first night.
Teaches the player to survive the night. Block entrances using doors, craft a bed to sleep in the night, and torches to lighten up the interior. Eat food to regenerate health. In the nighttime, you can craft items, organize your inventory, and plan for tomorrow.

How to: Build the Basics
Rome wasn't built in a day, and neither will your Minecraft house if you don't know how. So put on your hardhats, because today we will learn the basics of Minecraft construction — the art of turning simple blocks into architectural masterpieces.
You can plan what you build or just let your creativity begin. There are a lot of blocks, all of them have different properties.

YouTube

How to: Tame and Breed Animals
Animal lover? The Overworld is filled with furry friends to tame and call your own. Best of all, they won't chew up your shoes as their real-life counterparts! In this video, we will show you how to obtain a little feathered or four-legged companion and how to take care of them.
You can tame wolves, ocelots, horses, and parrots. To tame wolves use bones, for ocelotss use fish, keep mounting horses to tame them. Wheat can be fed to cows and sheep, seeds to chicken, and carrots to pigs to breed them.

YouTube

How to: Build a Simple Farm
The person who said, "Your soul is a garden," definitely never knew how frustrating gardening can be if you don't have the know-how... or maybe they did? Nevertheless, it is time to paint those thumbs green because, in this video, we will go through the basics of Minecraft farming.
Farms are essentials to get food. Use a hoe to create farmland nearby water sources. Get seeds by breaking grass or find other crops in different places. Plant them in the farmland to grow them.

YouTube

How to: Discover Different Biomes Part 1
The world of Minecraft is mind-bogglingly large — like really, really, really big. In this video, we will do a quick run-through of the different variations of climates and environments that the Overworld has to offer – let's explore the biomes of Minecraft.
There are a lot of biomes in the Minecraft world. In every biome there are a lot of different resources, as well as different dimensions (The End and the Nether which are accessible through portals.).

YouTube

How to: Discover Different Biomes Part 2
Ready to go beyond green forests and sandy beaches? Well, pack your bags explorers, it is time to discover the more uncharted biomes of the Overworld. From the barren yet colorful badlands to the brutally cold but still breathtakingly beautiful ice spikes, today we will go through the less common biomes you might encounter in your travels.
Mushroom fields contain a lot of mushrooms and there are no hostile mobs. In the Badlands there is terracotta and coal. In Ice Spikes, large amounts of ice spikes will generate.

How to: Use Redstone and Command Blocks Part 1
