Minecraft x Herschel or Herschel x Minecraft is a collaboration between Minecraft and Herschel Supply Co. that includes the Herschel Backpack Trials add-on, various Minecraft collections on Herschel's store, as well as a giveaway for the Minecraft x Herschel Little America™ Cube Backpack.

Collections
Creeper, pink sheep, and enderman collections
These collections each have 9 products including size variances (2 backpacks, 2 caps, 2 beanies, a lunchbox, a pencil case, and a hip pack). The items correspond with each other across collections, and are represented as different color variations of eachother on the website.
