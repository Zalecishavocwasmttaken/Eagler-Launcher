Summary

You can help by expanding it.

As the narrator describes AI, the video starts in a white-colored room with Agent cleaning the floor with their robot cleaner. They repeatedly get frustrated as Chicken keeps trailing dirt and pecking the camera. The narrator then insists that AI requires thoughtful and responsible usage, represented by Agent improving their robot cleaner through their PC. The scene shifts to a demonstration of the map, which ends by Agent building a staircase out of purple wool.
