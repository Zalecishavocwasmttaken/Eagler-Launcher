The Hero Cape is an upcoming cape given to players for playing both Minecraft Dungeons and Minecraft Dungeons II.

Appearance
The cape's design depicts a redstone monstrosity face.

Distribution
Players can automatically receive this cape by logging in to Minecraft Dungeons then Minecraft Dungeons II before December 31, 2026. Owning the cape in Minecraft Dungeons is not required to receive it in Minecraft.

History
August 27, 2026
The cape is announced for both Minecraft and Minecraft Dungeons II.September 29, 2026
Minecraft Dungeons II releases, making the cape available.December 31, 2026
The cape distribution concludes.
