Redstone Monstrosity Hot Wheels car.
From 2017 to 2022, the toy brand Hot Wheels released a special line of cars based on Minecraft characters as part of their Character Cars series, namely the Creeper, Enderman, Skeleton, Iron Golem, Spider and Zombie. In 2020, a new car was added to the roster featuring the Ocelot, and, in 2021, another new car was released, featuring the Redstone Monstrosity from Minecraft Dungeons.
In 2025 new cars based on Minecraft mobs and characters from A Minecraft Movie were produced. Between 2016 and 2018 there were also several Minecraft-themed Hot Wheels track sets featuring cars resembling Minecraft minecarts containing mobs and players. Additionally, a series of Minecart cars has been regularly released with various colors and patterns.

Minecarts
A series of Minecart cars also exists, designed to be compatible with Mini figures. Several models with different colors and designs have been produced.














































































RacerVerse series
Hot Wheels cars depicting actual Minecraft mobs riding on Minecraft entities were also made: Steve riding a Pig, a Creeper riding a Minecart, a Spider Jockey and a Chicken Jockey.


































A Minecraft Movie
Hot Wheels cars based on characters from A Minecraft Movie riding in minecarts were also made: Steve, Garret, a Creeper and a Piglin.
