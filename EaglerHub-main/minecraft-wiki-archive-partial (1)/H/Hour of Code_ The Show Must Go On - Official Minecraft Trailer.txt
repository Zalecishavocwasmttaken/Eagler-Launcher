Summary
In the backstage of a show, Agent refuses to show up to the audience due to their stage fright. As the show starts, they peek out but come back quickly out of fear. Aware of Agent's situation, Chicken advises them to take a deep breath, then hangs them on a hook which picks them up onto the stage. Having regained courage, Agent pulls the curtains, revealing the mechanics of the map behind them.
