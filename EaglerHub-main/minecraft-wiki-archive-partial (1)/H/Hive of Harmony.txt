Additions
Items
 Hive of Harmony Guide

Contains an introduction to the add-on, information on bee and flower pairings, farming mechanics, and crafting recipes.
Allows for setting either 8-bit (default)[verify] or 16-bit textures for bees
Blocks
Harmony hive
A pride bee going into a bee hive tranforms it into a harmony hive (also known as a hive of harmony). There are three levels of maturity a harmony hive can have depending on how many pride bees have entered it.

Entities
Bees
A regular bee has a 99% chance to turn into a pride bee if fed a corresponding flower while crouching. It has a 1% chance of turning into an ally queen instead.
