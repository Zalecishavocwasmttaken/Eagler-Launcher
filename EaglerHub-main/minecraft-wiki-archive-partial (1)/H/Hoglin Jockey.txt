Spawning
Post-generation
Baby piglins run near and ride baby hoglins. If there are more baby piglins than baby hoglins, the baby piglins occasionally jump on other baby piglins already riding a baby hoglin, with a maximum of 3 stacked on a single baby hoglin.

Dismounting
The baby piglins can dismount the hoglin after getting on it, making hoglin jockeys a temporary occurrence.[verify for Bedrock Edition]

Drops
When killed by a player or tamed wolf, hoglins (including babies) drop 5XP, and baby piglins drop 1XP.
Baby piglins will not drop any items they have picked up, regardless of jockey status.

Behavior
Baby piglins
Main article: Baby Piglin
Baby piglins are passive and play with baby hoglins, running around and riding upon them. Up to 3 baby piglins may stack on top of each other while riding a baby hoglin.
Baby piglins never grow up.

This feature is exclusive to Java Edition. 

In Java Edition, if a hoglin jockey is brought to the Overworld or The End, the baby hoglin converts to a baby zoglin, and the baby piglin riders convert into baby zombified piglins. The newly created baby zombified piglins continue to ride the newly created baby zoglin.
