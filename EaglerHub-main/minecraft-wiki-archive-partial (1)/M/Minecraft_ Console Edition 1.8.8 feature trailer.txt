Summary
Steve rides on horseback across a forest then toward a mountaintop. In order to get to the other side of the valley underneath, they throw an ender pearl to the cliff, spawning two endermites in the process. They then walk across a taiga, then a badlands biome alongside two horses. Meanwhile in a dark forest, Alex comes out of their prismarine house toward a savanna, then up a mountain and while jumping on slime blocks across an ice spikes biome. They and Steve finally meet on a frozen beach, drinking potions of water breathing to conquer an ocean monument underwater.
