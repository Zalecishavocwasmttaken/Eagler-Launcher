Villagers converting to witches is an example of mob conversion.
Mob conversion is a game mechanic where under specific circumstances certain mobs can convert to mobs of a different type, or to a different variant of the same mob.

Notes

 The initial version of this list was provided by Mojang in the 24w37a changelog.

 There are currently several bugs where data is not retained.

 The health of the converted mob is set to the same percentage of max health as the original mob had before conversion.
