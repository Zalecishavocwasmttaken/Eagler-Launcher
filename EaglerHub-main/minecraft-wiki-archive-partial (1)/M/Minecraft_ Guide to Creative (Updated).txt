Minecraft: Guide to Creative is a Minecraft book authored by Thomas McBrien, meant to help introduce players to creative mode. Alongside many diagrams and explanations are several special passages from YouTubers, including Jeracraft, Wattles, Team Visionary, and Varuna.

Synopsis
“
Ignite your imagination and embrace your creativity with the Minecraft Guide to Creative—the only book you'll need to take your creative skills to the next level.

Follow step-by-step instructions to complete your first builds, discover top tips on lighting, build themes and so much more. Includes expert advice from pro builders to inspire you to get creative!„
Contents
