Vu Bui in the first episode of Minecraft Monthly, featuring the old logo.Minecraft Monthly is a series of normally monthly news videos hosted by Vu Bui, aimed at giving a comedic rundown of recent and upcoming features regarding the Minecraft franchise. It is the successor of the livestream series Minecraft Now. The show usually features developers discussing upcoming features, explaining how certain ideas are developed, and previewing content that may appear in future updates. It also sometimes highlights creations from players and provides announcements related to events like Minecraft Live.

Announced that instead of a single annual update, multiple game drops will now be released throughout the year, with two Minecraft Live events planned for 2025.
Mentioned the release of the James Webb Space Telescope Challenge DLC with NASA and Planet Earth III in Minecraft Education and Marketplace, teaching about the cosmos and the natural world.
Introduced the Bundles of Bravery drop, which added bundles for easier item storage and gifting, and brought Hardcore Mode to Minecraft Bedrock Edition.
Announced the first-ever immersive experience, Villager Rescue, currently in Dallas, Texas, with plans to expand to other cities.
Introduced The Garden Awakens drop featuring the pale garden biome, pale oak trees, pale hanging moss, eyeblossoms, and the hostile mob creaking.
Celebrated Minecraft's 15th birthday, thanking the community for their support and creativity.
