Minecraft: Pixel Genesis is a remix album featuring compositions by Lena Raine, Kumi Tanioka, and Aaron Cherof with remixes by Hyper Potions, Synthion, AIKA, leon chang, Snail's House, Elliot Hsu, and Anamanaguchi. It remixes tracks from Minecraft: Tricky Trials (Original Game Soundtrack) and "Infinite Amethyst," from Minecraft: Caves & Cliffs (Original Game Soundtrack). It was released on September 13, 2024. The album's cover art and the accompanying animated videos were created by Martin Wörister.
