Summary
In an windswept hills biome, Steve overlooks from the summit before going downhill, while in wooded badlands, Alex mounting a chestnut horse overlooks the regular badlands. The two then build a treehouse in a jungle before the scene cuts to a savanna with some llamas. Meanwhile in a village, Steve buys a woodland explorer map from a cartographer and goes in search for their destination, accompanied by four leashed llamas. Meeting Alex, they find the woodland mansion and the two go together inside. There, Steve encounters a vindicator and flees to the map room where they face an evoker. After a short fight, Steve manages to get a totem of undying and goes to an end city with Alex, who kills a shulker and makes a box from its shells. Steve, pushed down by an enderman from above, manages to survive with their totem. Back in the Overworld, Alex places a dragon head on a frame while Steve places the shulker box, and the two come out of their base in the forest as the camera zooms out from above.
