Minecraft: The Hero's Ballad is the upcoming 25th Minecraft novel. It is authored by Kersten Hamilton and is slated to be published by Random House Worlds on February 2, 2027.

Synopsis
“Stone Haven used to be a peaceful, prosperous place — until Raska and her army came. They promised to keep the people safe, but in exchange they took over everything. And everyone.
Raska’s control is all that Wat and Sevyn have ever known. So when a traveling bard wanders into town singing tales of crafting and fighting, they start to think maybe such wonders could be possible again for Stone Haven. Seeking help to restore their home’s freedom, Wat and Sevyn sneak off in search of the real-life hero from the bard’s songs.

Outside Stone Haven’s restrictions, the Overworld is grander than they ever could have imagined. Enchanted weapons, secret hideouts, portals to new dimensions—and there’s still so much they’ve yet to explore! With the help of new friends, Wat and Sevyn learn to mine, craft, and fight for survival as they journey across the land. But can they find their hero and return to Stone Haven in time to reclaim their home from Raska?„ — Book description
