Summary
Steve comes out of their base, going downhill and tilling the blocks around them to plant wheat in one. Back in their base, they bring out of their chest a raw chicken, an apple, and a block of white wool. Outside, they fall from such a high cliff they take damage and have to eat cooked chicken to recover. At night, Steve returns to their base to sleep, before the camera zooms out to reveal the footage was taken on a phone. The next scenes include Steve entering through a trapdoor, smelting a red mushroom to red dye, turning on peaceful mode, cooking raw beef into steak, and building in Creative mode using mossy cobblestone and redstone ore. Back in-game, Steve brings four sands and five gunpowder items out of their chest to craft TNT, which they use against a group of three zombies. As it slowly turns daytime, they walk downhill as the camera zooms out to the phone, and a creeper detonates the phone itself into falling blocks before the background transitions to black.
