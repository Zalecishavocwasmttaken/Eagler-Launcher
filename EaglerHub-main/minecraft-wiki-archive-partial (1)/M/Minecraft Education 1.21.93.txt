1.21.93 (Windows, ChromeOS, Android) or 1.21.93.1 (macOS, iOS) is a minor update to Minecraft Education, released on October 30, 2025, which fixes some bugs.

Contents

1 Changes
2 Fixes
3 References
4 Navigation

Changes
Updates to MakeCode:
Added more events.
Code does not run automatically anymore when closing.
Fixes
Fixed switching touch controls on Android.
Behavior and resource packs are correctly included in the world again when opened from My Templates.
Fixed a login error.
