Summary
The video opens on Bianca awakening in a taiga, confused, before stumbling upon a mansion. After passing by what appears to be a tollbooth, she enters the building, lit by redstone lamps, and discovers her friends have been turned into endermen. Seeing them contained inside of pods, she elects to cure them of the "curse of the endermen." To do this, she places several items in an oversized cauldron, pushes a minecart, discovers a secret door in a bookshelf, and finally places four keys into item frames. As she enters into a hedge maze, the camera pans to an overhead view of the area, ending the video.
