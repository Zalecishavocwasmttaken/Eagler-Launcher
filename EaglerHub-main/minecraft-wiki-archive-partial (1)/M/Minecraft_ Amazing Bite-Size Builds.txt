Synopsis
“Learn how to design, build and customize 20 mini-projects in Minecraft, from space rockets and mineshaft trains to jungle shrines and lighthouses. There's even a marine tugboat!  With detailed instructions, block hacks and build tips, this book will teach you new tricks and help you practice old skills as you create bite-size builds to impress your friends in Minecraft.„
