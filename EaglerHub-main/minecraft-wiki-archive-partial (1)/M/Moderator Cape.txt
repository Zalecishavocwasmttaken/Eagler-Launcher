The Moderator Cape is a cape given to moderators on Mojira, the Mojang Bug Tracker.

Distribution
This cape is given to current and past moderators on Mojira. To become a moderator, a community member needs to be consistently active in assisting in the bug tracker's moderation and become a helper first.

History
December 25, 2014
The cape is given to current Mojira moderators.May 9, 2016
The cape is now given to all Mojira moderators from now.
