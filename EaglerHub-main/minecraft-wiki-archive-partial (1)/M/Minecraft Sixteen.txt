Minecraft Sixteen is a font. It is used on a few Minecraft.net articles,and the footer of the Minecraft Experience: Villager Rescue website. It has also been seen in pages which introduce Minecraft Dungeons II and its screenshots.

Definition
MinecraftSixteen.ttf
A TrueType font, named "Minecraft Sixteen". It is a less pixelated version of the Mojangles font.
