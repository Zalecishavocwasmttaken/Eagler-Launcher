The Moonlight Trail Cape is a cape given to Minecraft Experience: Moonlight Trail attendees.

Appearance
The cape's design depicts a diamond beacon pyramid, with a beam appearing from a beacon. It is displayed on a dark blue background with a full moon in a starry night sky.

Distribution
Players can receive this cape by redeeming a code sent by e-mail within 72 hours after completing the experience. The codes must be redeemed no later than 30 days after the event closes in the tour city where they were obtained.

History
March 21, 2026
Minecraft Experience: Moonlight Trail was announced during Minecraft Live – March 2026.May 15, 2026
The event begins, with cape code distribution starting within 90 hours of this point.

The cape initially only displays in Bedrock Edition, with the FAQ stating that it will later be added to Java Edition.May 26, 2026[verify]
The cape was given to Java Edition player Battix, but is not publicly available on the edition yet.May 27, 2026
The cape is now available to all eligible Java Edition players.
