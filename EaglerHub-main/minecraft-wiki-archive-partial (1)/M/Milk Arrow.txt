A Milk Arrow (Japanese: 牛乳矢, Hepburn: Gyūnyū Ya?) is a tipped arrow covered in milk. It can be created using the Imagination Mod Power in Minecraft: The Manga.

Appearance
Milk Arrows resemble other tipped arrows, with their head covered in milk instead of a potion effect.

Functionality
When shot from an arrow, milk arrows have a poison-countering effect. Gray was able to use one to puncture Dr. Liquid's poison wall.

Crafting recipe

IngredientsCrafting recipe

Arrow + Milk Bucket

History
While fighting Dr. Liquid, Nico White created a milk arrow at Gray's request. Using the arrow, Gray shot through Dr. Liquid's defensive poison wall, defeating him.
