Mounts of Mayhem (stylized in all caps) is an official trailer for Mounts of Mayhem. It revolves around Alex and others battling mounted foes.

Summary
In a badlands biome, Alex is seen riding a horse, scaring an armadillo as they rush by it. They are pursued by a group of five camel husk jockeys, and a parched attempts shooting an arrow at Alex only to miss. Alex then blows their goat horn and stops near a wooded biome, seemingly waiting for something as the group of jockeys rushes towards them. They soon hear the blaring of another horn as an answer, leading them to unsheathe their diamond sword and charge toward the incoming jockeys. As they ride towards the jockeys, their friends Efe, Kai, Makena, and Sunny appear by their side, all the four wielding iron spears and swords.
Both sides prepare to engage, and a pig in the center becomes panicked as the forces collide. Efe manages to take down both riders of a camel husk with a single jab of their spear. As they celebrate their feat, Makena subsequently takes down two sets of camel husk riders with two swift jabs; effectively one-upping Efe. Efe shakes their head in disapproval, until they notice the sun setting as five zombie horsemen arrive as reinforcements and attack the heroes. Kai is shot off their horse and begins running away from a zombie horseman chasing them. As the horseman closes in and prepares to attack, a gliding Noor swoops down and instantly takes down the zombie rider.
As Kai breathes a sigh of relief, Alex passing by on their horse causes them to fall into a nearby lake. Kai sinks into the water, and then finds themself faced with a group of five zombie nautilus jockeys preparing to attack them. Kai then finds a nautilus and quickly rides it back towards safety, petting it before climbing back onto land only to again fall into the lake due to a running camel husk. The scene then shifts to the other heroes continuing the fight before Kai spots a lone zombie horse. They offer the zombie horse a potato, only for it to refuse and the pig from earlier to accept it instead.
With the hostile mobs defeated, all the heroes, including Kai now on the pig, meet together and celebrate their victory. Before long, Alex senses a rumbling as an army of camel husk jockeys, zombie horsemen, spider jockeys, and chicken jockeys emerges from the woods. The heroes look to each other for reassurance, and Alex blows their goat horn before the group charges towards the army of mounted monsters.
