Minecraft Idea a Day is a Minecraft flip book, giving daily suggestions and techniques for building.
Synopsis
“
Flip the page and discover a new Minecraft build idea to inspire you each day!
Get creative with 150 awesome Minecraft builds, from secret bases, cute animals, and dream homes to supersized landmarks, mob builds and much more!
The stand-up, spiral-bound format is perfect for displaying next to your screen and enjoying with family and friends, as you find inspiration for your next brilliant build.
Whether it’s a bouncy castle, creeper maze, swamp monster, rainbow bridge, axolotl house, or giant hotdog, each day brings a new and totally random, super-fun surprise. Plus, get top tips on how to level-up your Minecraft building skills.

„ — Book description
