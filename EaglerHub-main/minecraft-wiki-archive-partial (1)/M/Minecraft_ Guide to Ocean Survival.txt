Minecraft: Guide to Ocean Survival is a guide that contains various tips on how to explore survive in different ocean biomes.

Synopsis
“Minecraft's oceans are teeming with colorful life and rare treasures, but new dangers lurk beneath the water and survival can prove difficult.
The official Minecraft: Guide to Ocean Survival will teach you how to breath underwater, find valuable sunken loot and fight off guardians and other menacing mobs of the deep.

With insider info and tips from the experts at Mojang, this is the definitive guide to underwater survival.„
