Track description
Memories is a calm track and its melody slowly builds up starting with a piano, then gaining a chime-like instrument. Like in "Ancestry", noises sounding like wind can be heard at the end.Previews of tracks in Minecraft have been shortened to 30 seconds on this wiki in accordance with fair use rationale. "11" and "Magnetic Circuit" are exempt from this.

Windows: Content\data\resource_packs\vanilla_music\sounds\music\game
All music files are in Ogg Vorbis.
