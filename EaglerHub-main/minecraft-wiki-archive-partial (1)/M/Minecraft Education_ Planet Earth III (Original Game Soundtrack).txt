Minecraft Education: Planet Earth III (Original Game Soundtrack) is the first soundtrack released for Minecraft by Jacob Shea and Sara Barone, which consists of the songs that were released as part of the Planet Earth III DLC. It was released on January 16, 2024, the same day the DLC was released on the Marketplace.
