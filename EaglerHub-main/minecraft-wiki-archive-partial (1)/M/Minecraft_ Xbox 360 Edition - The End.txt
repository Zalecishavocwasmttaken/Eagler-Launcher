Summary
In a plains assembly building, Altair instructs Claptrap, a grizzly bear, and a mermaid on how to get to the End. As a first step, the team has to gather ender pearls by killing endermen from a forest and a swamp alongside a chef, Barry Steakfries, and a moose. For the second step, the team is tasked to collect blaze powder. To do so, they travel to a Nether fortress to defeat blazes. For the third step, the original members of the assembly locate a stronghold with their eyes of ender. Once they found the location, the mermaid digs down while Claptrap places ladders. Down below, the four activate the End portal and approach the ender dragon to fight it.
