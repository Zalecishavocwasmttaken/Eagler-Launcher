The MCC 15th Year Cape is a cape which was obtainable by completing all 15 challenges in the MCC x Minecraft 15th Anniversary Party.

Appearance
The cape's design include a golden crown and large, white lettering of the number fifteen. It's placed on a red background with additional golden frills at the bottom and top.

Distribution
Main article: MCC x Minecraft 15th Anniversary PartyThe MCC 15th Year cape was obtained by completing all 15 challenges in the MCC x Minecraft 15th Anniversary Party event server.
History
July 22, 2024
The Minecraft 15th Anniversary Party event server has started.July 28, 2024
The final mystery cave challenge is revealed, making the cape obtainable.August 5, 2024
The Minecraft 15th Anniversary Party event server has ended.
Trivia
