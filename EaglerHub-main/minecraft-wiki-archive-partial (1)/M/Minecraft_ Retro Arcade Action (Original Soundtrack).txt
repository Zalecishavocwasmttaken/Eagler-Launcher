Minecraft: Retro Arcade Action (Original Soundtrack) is the second soundtrack released for Minecraft by Kyle Martin Cora, known professionally as "Kylydian", containing songs that are included as part of the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles DLC. The soundtrack was released on February 20, 2024.
