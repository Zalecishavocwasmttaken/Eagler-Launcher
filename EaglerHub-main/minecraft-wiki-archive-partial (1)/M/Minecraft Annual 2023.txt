Synopsis
“Find out what’s new in the world of Minecraft with this new official Minecraft Annual 2023, perfect for gamers. Check out this year’s Minecraft Annual, full of exciting activities, insider tips and features on new things that have been added to the game in the past year. This annual is a must-have for all Minecraft fans.Find out more about the latest Wild Update, check out what the people at Mojang love most about the game and take part in survival challenges. Plus find out more about command blocks and how to use them. If that wasn’t enough, the book is also filled with super-fun activities for you to complete.You’ll not want to miss this!„ — Book description
