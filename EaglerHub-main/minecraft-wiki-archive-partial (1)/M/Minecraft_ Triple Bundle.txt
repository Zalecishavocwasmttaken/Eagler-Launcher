The Minecraft: Triple Bundle was a bundle allowing you to buy Minecraft, Minecraft Dungeons, and Minecraft Legends for US$59.99 (Can$69.99, €49.99, £44.99, A$84.95, NZ$94.95, S$71.90). The bundle also came with 1,020 Minecoins.
