Summary
The video opens in the End showing an end city, before entering through an end gateway and teleporting to the Overworld where Alex and Steve throw an eye of ender. The eye leads them to the badlands across a construction site and toward what looks like a temple in Petra. Inside, they use a moving platform across a large gap as two other players build a room in the temple. They then come across a wide hall where Alex finds signs reading altogether "Only those who seek the light will find the way". In accordance to the order, they step on pressure plates, turning on several redstone lamps and causing a TNT to fall on the floor, revealing a stronghold underneath. There, the two activate the End portal and enter the outer End islands where they find an end city. Inside, they try avoiding shulker bullets and make it to the rooftop where a mooshroom brewer and a tundra engineer give them elytra. The video soon darkens to black as an ender dragon swoops toward the camera.
