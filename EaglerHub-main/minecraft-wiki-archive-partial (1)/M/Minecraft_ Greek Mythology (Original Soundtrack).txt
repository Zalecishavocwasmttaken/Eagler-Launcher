Minecraft: Greek Mythology (Original Soundtrack) is the first soundtrack released for Minecraft by Gareth Coker, which features music from the Greek Mythology Mash-up pack. Originally released as part of the July 24, 2015 update to Legacy Console Edition, the soundtrack was later released on December 21, 2016.
