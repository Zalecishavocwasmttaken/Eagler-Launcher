Minecraft: Triple Bundle is a remix album featuring compositions by Aaron Cherof, Crispin Hands, Johan Johnson, Peter Hont, Lena Raine, and Kumi Tanioka remixed by Hyper Potions. It was released on March 4, 2025 alongside the release of the Minecraft Triple Bundle offer.

Trivia
"Memories of Dalarna" is a future bass remix of "Comforting Memories", "Dalarna", "Relic", "The Well of Fate" and "A Time of Legends".
"Legends from the Otherside" is a happy hardcore remix of "Otherside", "Halland" and "A Time of Legends".
"Arch-Illager's Pigstep" is a drum and bass and dubstep remix of "Pigstep", "The Arch-Illager"  and "Burly and Surly".
