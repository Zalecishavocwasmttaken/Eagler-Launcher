Cover for Minecraft x Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds (Original Soundtrack).
Minecraft × Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds (Original Soundtrack) is an extended play and the original soundtrack for the Minecraft pack in Sonic Racing: CrossWorlds. It features compositions by Lena Raine, Kumi Tanioka, Aaron Cherof, and Peter Hont remixed by Takahiro Kai. It was released on December 18, 2025.

Trivia
"Overworld Track" is a remix of "Comforting Memories" and "Infinite Amethyst".
"Overworld Track (Final Lap)" is a remix of "Relic".
"The Nether Track" is a remix of "Pigstep".
"The End Track" is a remix of "Broken Heart of Ender".
