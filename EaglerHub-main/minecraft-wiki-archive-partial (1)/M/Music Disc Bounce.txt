"Bounce" refers to both a song composed by fingerspit and a music disc that plays the song when inserted in a jukebox. The music disc can be found in mineshafts within sulfur caves.

Previews of tracks in Minecraft have been shortened to 30 seconds on this wiki in accordance with fair use rationale. "11" and "Magnetic Circuit" are exempt from this.

"Bounce" is an energetic electronic track driven by bright, punchy synths that carry the main melody and bass harmony throughout. It features crisp synthetic percussion, with the drum pattern arranged in breakbeat style. The piece has a lively, rhythmic feel with a upbeat, optimistic tone.

Usage
Like all music discs, using the "Bounce" music disc on a jukebox causes the jukebox to play the song.
If a redstone comparator is reading a jukebox playing "Bounce", it emits a redstone signal of 8.
