Minecraft Experience refers to various in-person interactive events run by Experience Mod and Supply+Demand, in conjunction with Mojang Studios.
Both events combined (Villager Rescue and Moonlight Trail at the time) were visited by one million people as of July 2026.

Events
Villager Rescue
Main article: Minecraft Experience: Villager Rescue
Minecraft Experience: Villager Rescue is an in-person interactive event where participants in groups of up to 25 go through a mission and scavenger hunt through multiple Minecraft biomes and dimensions to rescue villagers. The first event took place in Dallas, Texas, from October 18, 2024, to January 20, 2025, with more events taking place in various locations all around the world afterwards.

Moonlight Trail
Main article: Minecraft Experience: Moonlight Trail
Minecraft Experience: Moonlight Trail is a nightly in-person interactive event held outdoors, where participants in groups hike through various Minecraft biomes in a trail that goes up a kilometer in length, leading to a final area where participants battle mobs to restore a lighthouse. The first event is currently taking place in Buenos Aires, Argentina. Additional locations are planned in the future.
