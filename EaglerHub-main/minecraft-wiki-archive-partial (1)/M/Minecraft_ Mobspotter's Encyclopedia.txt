Cover for Minecraft: Mobspotter's Encyclopedia
Minecraft: Mobspotter's Encyclopedia is a Minecraft book authored by Mojang Studios and published by Random House World that gives information and commentary on the 79 mobs that existed as of Trails & Tales.

Synopsis
“You’ll meet seven expert guides, who are full of stories of their fun experiences, and be taken on a tour to explore every corner of the Overworld. You’ll even navigate to the Nether and experience the End dimension, on a thrilling journey to witness all seventy-nine mobs in existence.
Discover how to survive a shoot-out with skeletons, dive for treasure with dolphins or risk a run-in with the Ender Dragon—and then start a whole new adventure of your own!„“It’s time for adventure! Discover all there is to know about every mob in Minecraft in the official Mobspotter’s Encyclopedia: The Ultimate Guide to the Mobs of Minecraft.„ — Product details
Contents
Table of contents, featuring an allay, vex, and striders.
Each page provides information and commentary on at least one mob and features relating to it. The book covers all mobs added until the Trails & Tales update across seven sections. Each section has a different character original to the book providing commentary on the mobs.
Numbers shown after each mob represent the page that the mob's information is found on.

Trivia
Errors
On page 148, an arrow referring to a nether fortress goes in the opposite direction, pointing to a warped forest instead.
On page 156, while discussing the habitat of a wither, a basalt delta and bastion remnant are referred to as a nether wastes and nether fortress respectively.
