Summary
A peasant and a postman walk together to a desert village made of various colorful blocks. There, the peasant buys a map from a cartographer while the postman equips a diamond helmet they brought from a blacksmith's workshop chest. The two walk across a river with frost walker enchanted boots to bring their three llamas with them. At dusk, they arrive at a woodland mansion as a thunderstorm strikes, encountering a vindicator and defeating it. They then find an evoker at the ritual room and get a totem of undying from it. In the End, the duo attempt to go up the tower of an end city: the peasant shoots at shulker bullets with their bow while the postman gets hit by one, levitating in the air. After they reach the bridge, they try to find a way to reach the faraway end ship, the postman falling and surviving with their totem in the process. The two then come together and launch ender pearls toward the ship. Once there, the postman retrieves a pair of elytra and launches themselves far from the city.
