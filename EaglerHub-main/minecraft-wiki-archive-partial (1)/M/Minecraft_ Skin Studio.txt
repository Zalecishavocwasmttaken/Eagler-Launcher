Minecraft: Skin Studio was an app for iOS developed by 57Digital which was initially released in October 14, 2012, allowing for the creation of skins for Minecraft: Java and Bedrock editions.

Functionality
There are several buttons in the application:

Begin – provides various tools for editing skins. Finished skins can be ported to various editions of the Minecraft, and the skin can also be posted in the Community Skins section.
My Skins – provides a catalog of skins created by the user.
Community Skins – provides a catalog of skins, which has 3 categories: Featured, Popular and Latest, created by other users.
My Profile – provides editable information of the user's account.
