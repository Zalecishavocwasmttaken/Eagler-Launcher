A mossy cobblestone wall is a decorative wall variant of mossy cobblestone that generates as part of pillager outposts and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Mossy cobblestone walls can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.
