Minecraft: Chinese Mythology (Original Soundtrack) is the third soundtrack for Minecraft released by Gareth Coker, which contains music from the Chinese Mythology mash-up pack. Originally released as part of the October 4, 2016 update to Legacy Console Edition, the soundtrack was later released on December 21, 2016.
