Minecraft Annual 2025 is a Minecraft book authored by Mojang AB and Farshore Books. The book covers a wide range of topics from the past year and games for readers to enjoy. In structure and aesthetic it is virtually identical to its precursor, however with occasional subtle changes, such as Ari and Zuri receiving different categories.

Synopsis
“
Discover all there is to know about the past year of Minecraft in this exciting annual. Stuffed to the brim with updates, activities, step-by-step builds and tips and tricks for the game, this book is a must-have for any minecrafter.

This year’s annual includes an awesome mining boardgame, loads of fun activities and tips on smithing templates, archaeology and how to survive a pillager raid. Plus discover a blocky brownie recipe, awesome buillds and survival challenges for you to complete in-game!„ — Book description

Trivia
Unlike the rest of the heroes, Ari receives two categories, Write and Bake. This brings the total up to ten, one more than there are characters in Minecraft.
Errors
Several outdated textures can be seen in Live in the Nether. On page 12, Efe can be seen holding the old bread and cooked porkchop textures. On page 13, a piglin can be seen bartering several outdated sprites of nether quartz.
The then-current enderman design and the prior one, distinguishable by their eye colors, can be seen pages apart during Which Mob Are You?
On the same page, an allay can be seen holding an outdated diamond.
