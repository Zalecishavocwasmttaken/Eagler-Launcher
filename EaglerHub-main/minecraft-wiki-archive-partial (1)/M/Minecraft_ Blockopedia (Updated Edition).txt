Minecraft: Blockopedia (Updated Edition) is a guide book that contains information on every block in Minecraft up until Caves & Cliffs, including blocks found in Minecraft Education. A foreword is present, written by Agnes Larsson.

Synopsis
“Discover everything there is to know about more than 600 blocks in MINECRAFT BLOCKPEDIA: UPDATED EDITION, a comprehensive guide to every single block you can find in Minecraft!
Ever wanted to know how to make obsidian or find out where you can mine for gilded blackstone? Maybe you've wondered how to use ancient debris or copper ore? You can find the answers to all of these questions and so much more in more than 300 fully illustrated pages!„

Trivia
Certain blocks that don't normally have textures, like the end portal block and end gateway, have been given one.
Errors
The page for block of lapis lazuli says it is crafted with nine of itself.
The recipe for scaffolding has 3D renders of bamboo instead of the item form.
The page for cut copper slabs incorrectly show the existence of regular copper slabs, and the cut copper stairs page shows only the false existence of oxidized copper stairs, while the background shows copper stairs and weathered copper stairs.
On the page for wooden slabs, the dark oak slab uses an outdated texture.
On the page for stone stairs, the cobbled deepslate stairs use the deepslate side texture.
Many blocks that use item models for their inventory icon use their block models in recipes.
Many items are flipped.
