Minecraft: Papercraft Studio was an app for iOS developed by 57Digital which was initially released on February 13, 2013. This app allowed the creation of paper figures of Minecraft mobs and characters.

Functionality
The application allows the user to generate templates for printing and assembling paper figures of Minecraft mobs and characters. Templates can be customized in height, armor, and cape. The character skin can then be selected from the catalog or found by name.
