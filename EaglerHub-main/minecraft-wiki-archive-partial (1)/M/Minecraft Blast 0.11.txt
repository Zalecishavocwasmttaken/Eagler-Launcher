Features
Gameplay
 Levels

Levels are the main gameplay features within Minecraft Blast and is placed within the home screen. Levels 1-230 were added.
 Tasks

Tasks are one of the main gameplay features that the player must do in Minecraft Blast to progress.
 Blocks

Blocks are the main components of each level of Minecraft Blast. They are used to destroy, clear or collect blockers by tapping on matching blocks that are next to the blockers.
 Boosters

Boosters are power-ups the player can create when matching a high amount of blocks to help them beat levels.
 Blockers

Blockers are objectives the player must complete to successfully beat levels.
 Companions

Companions are virtual avatars the player controls within the game. 6 options were added.
