Minecraft Game Design is a book written by Jens Bergensten and published by Mojang AB in 2019 which outlines the core design principles of the Minecraft franchise. Being intended for internal use at Mojang Studios, the book was never officially distributed to the broader public. However, due to Swedish legal deposit regulations, physical copies of the publication are available at the National Library of Sweden.
