The Minecraft Experience Cape‌[JE  only] or Villager Rescue Cape‌[BE  only] is a cape given to Minecraft Experience: Villager Rescue attendees.

Appearance
The cape's design includes a face of a villager with a zombie villager on one side and a normal villager on the other. It is displayed on a dark purple background.

Distribution
Players can receive this cape by redeeming a code rewarded after completing the experience. The codes must be redeemed no later than 30 days after the event closes in the tour city where they were obtained.
The code is distributed using one of two methods; which method is used varies by tour city. In some cities, codes are sent by email after visiting. In other cities, codes are distributed on printed cards when the Orb of Interaction is returned at the end of the experience. Guests will not receive code emails from cities that use the printed card method; in those cities, the code must be collected from the experience.

History
September 20, 2024
It is announced that Minecraft Experience attendees would receive a cape.

It is originally only announced as a Bedrock Edition cape, although Minecraft Experience employees stated Mojang Studios was working on adding it to Java Edition.October 18, 2024
The event begins, making the cape available in Bedrock Edition.October 23, 2024
The cape is made available in Java Edition.

The elytra texture uses an unfinished monochrome dark purple design.October 25, 2024
The cape in Java Edition is updated to match Bedrock Edition.
Trivia
The cape was designed by Craig Leigh, a Mojang employee. He took inspiration from Two-Face, a villain appearing in Batman comics.

Notes

 This deadline is very inconsistent, with multiple official sources providing different redemption periods. The terms and conditions mention that codes can be used within 30 days after the end of the experience, but the redemption page mentions 60 days, and the Toronto FAQ mentioned 6 months. Because of this, it is highly recommended to assume that codes expire within 30 days and use them as soon as possible.
