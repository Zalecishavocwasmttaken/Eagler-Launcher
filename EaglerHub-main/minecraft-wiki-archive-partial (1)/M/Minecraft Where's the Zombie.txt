Minecraft Where's the Zombie is a children's search and find book, the second of its kind after Minecraft Catch the Creeper and Other Mobs, centered around the search for a zombie.

Synopsis
“"Spot the zombies and their variations lurking in these official Minecraft search-and-find scenes!
Did you hear that? A groan. There it goes again! But where is that pesky zombie?
Journey across the dimensions, spotting zombies and other mobs in ten incredible scenes and patterns.

Will you be able to spot them all before they find you?"„
“Look carefully! Zombies could be hiding anywhere! This full-color activity book is perfect for Minecraft fans of all ages to find hidden mobs and more throughout its intricately illustrated pages!Find the hidden creepers, llamas, villagers—and ZOMBIES!!! This hardcover activity book will entertain Minecraft fans ages 6 to 8 as they search for hidden mobs and game characters in the highly-detailed scenes.„ — Book description
