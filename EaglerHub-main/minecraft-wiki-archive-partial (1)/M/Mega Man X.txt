Background
Mega Man X is a series of action-platform games released by Capcom. It is a sub-series of the Mega Man franchise. The first game was released on December 17, 1993 in Japan on the Super Famicom and the following month on the Super NES in North America. Set 100 years after the original series, the story follows X, the last creation of Dr. Thomas Light, whose free-willed programming and intelligence allows for countless other free-willed androids called reploids to be produced. When these reploids begin to go "Maverick" and start to rebel against humans under the command of former Maverick Hunter Sigma, X decides to make up for his creations by joining the Hunters.
