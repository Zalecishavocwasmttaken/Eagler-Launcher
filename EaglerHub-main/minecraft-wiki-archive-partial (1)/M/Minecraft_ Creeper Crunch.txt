Minecraft: Creeper Crunch is a breakfast cereal that is produced by Kellogg's in the United States, consisting of pulverized oats flavored with cinnamon and "Creeper Bit" marshmallows molded into cubes.
The box also contains a code which is able to be redeemed for an exclusive character creator item, 22 of which have been released.

Trivia
Only ten character creation items were available through in-box codes originally
The other twelve items were added in groups of three at later dates.
This cereal was originally planned to be discontinued on August 31, 2021, but was later pushed back to June 30, 2022.
