1.21.132 (Windows, ChromeOS, Android) or 1.21.132.1 (macOS, iOS), dubbed Copper, Collaborate & Compete, is a minor update to Minecraft Education, released on February  17, 2026. It adds features from both The Copper Age and Mounts of Mayhem game drops.
