Appearance
During Minecraft: Woodsword Chronicles, Morgan resembles a typical child for his age, with light skin, black hair, a blue jacket, yellow shirt, black pants, and red boots. His avatar closely resembles his true self, though with noticeably different hair spiking upwards, and a teal jacket. During gym class, he wears a white T-shirt featuring an X and two sets of two lines.
As of Minecraft: Stonesword Saga, he grows slightly older and changes to reflect that, now wearing a characteristic pair of sunglasses and a darker jacket. This change would be reflected into Minecraft: Ironsword Academy, where he now wears a leather jacket and voxel glasses.
