Not to be confused with Mob.

A mob (short for mobile, mobile entity, or mobile object) is an AI-driven game entity appearing in add-ons and certain worlds. All mobs are made using an API, typically designed around the Marketplace, and often with traits taken from existing mobs in the main game.
