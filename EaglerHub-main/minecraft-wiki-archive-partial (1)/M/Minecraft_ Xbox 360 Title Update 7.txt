Summary
The video opens on a Minecraft logo with the title update name made of wool built above snowy biomes during snowfall. Later at night, Parka Steve walks in a town and enters a house where they find Santa brewing and a Christmas present sleeping on a bed. Deeper inside is a kitchen where a zombie turkey, a Christmas pudding, and a Christmas tree disguised as their respective namesakes. Steve then goes outside building a snow golem next to a snowman as two robins watch them, before the scene returns to the wool logo and time of day.
