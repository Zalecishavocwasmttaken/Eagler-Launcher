Minecraft Puzzle Adventure: A Warden's Warning is a puzzle book that was released on July 7, 2026. The book consists of several pages of simple puzzles, the goal being to complete them all in sequence to conclude a story. On certain pages are clues, contained inside of apples.

“Complete the activities to find the clues and solve the mysteries in this engaging Minecraft activity book! Perfect for puzzle enthusiasts and Minecraft fans alike. The book begins with a form asking for information on the player, ranging from their name, motivation for playing Minecraft, favorite build, favorite mob, and favorite place to explore. From there, Efe asks them to draw their skin on the book. The storyline begins with Zuri and Efe going down to investigate the deep dark, unaware of the threat the warden poses.
