Synopsis
“The only official Minecraft Annual out in 2023, packed with activities, build ideas and survival challenges to try out in-game.„
“
Discover all there is to know about the past year of Minecraft in this exciting annual. Stuffed to the brim with updates, activities, step-by-step builds and tips and tricks for the game, this book is a must-have for any minecrafter!

This year’s annual includes updates such as the new skins, Legends, the 1.20 update and Dungeons Fauna Faire, as well as a desert build and survival challenge, a treasure hunt, fun games to play in Survival mode and fun new builds to create.„ — Book description
