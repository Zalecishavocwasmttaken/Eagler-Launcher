M.A.R.I.L.L.A. or M.A.R.I.L.L.A, backronym for Minecraft Antiquated Robot Inadequate Loves Lying Audaciously, is a character in the official Minecraft YouTube channel voiced by Rill Askey, formerly known as M.A.R.I.L.L.A. Wex.
She hosted the series Ten Things You Probably Didn't Know About Minecraft and then co-hosted The Secrets of Minecraft, Around the Minecraft World in 80 Biomes, and How We Make Minecraft with Narrator.

Appearance
M.A.R.I.L.L.A. is an old box computer with a creamy tan colored base, dark brown casing, a green and white screen, a gray keyboard and a "Team Player Mode" switch on the back. A ROM cartridge with Minecraft on it can be seen sticking out and a joystick sits on the right side connected by a cable. She is able to run programs such as Minecraft on herself.

Personality
M.A.R.I.L.L.A. is lighthearted, sarcastic, confidant, and audacious. She is quick to give false trivia and can be a little superstitious but always does so with positive boldness. She likes endermen (presumably romantically), but doesn't like Narrator, in spite of constantly working with him on various projects.

Abilities
Being a computer, M.A.R.I.L.L.A. is able to compute large amounts of information, possessing full access to the internet and several applications. These include some that augment abilities not typically possible by computers, such as a program for crying. In spite of having a backlit screen, it does not constitute as light in Minecraft, due to her soul possessing great darkness. At one point she even claims to have the power to access every computer in the world, able to see any person's "other tabs" and folders, later explained as her being a virus.
During the first season of her show, she reveals that she has a "Facts Meter" that goes up every time she shares a piece of trivia. Once this is filled, she ends up "fulfilling [her] purpose," causing her to be permanently deactivated. However, this is instead shown to cause her to spontaneously teleport to some sort of Heaven-like landfill full of other computers, which she manages to return from. She has also been shown to receive software update from an unknown source, with one adding features such as "M.A.X.I.M.U.M. M.A.R.I.L.L.A. mode."

Pre-series



The content of this section has uncertain continuity. 
The following  section  might not be in the intended timeline order.

Supposedly, in the distant past M.A.R.I.L.L.A. was a human, who came into contact with a witch, potentially Baba Yaga. She was offered three magic beans, but ended up being tricked, her soul being trapped inside of a computer inside Mojang Studios. At one point, she claimed to have been discovered in the Nether, but later claimed to have been found in the End. Another time, she claimed to have been created in Britain. She also claimed she gained sentience by being struck by lightning. She would end up being used for many projects prior to the company's official founding, apparently as far back as the 1980s, and was their first experiment in vocalization. At some point, she ended up being deemed obsolete and left to rot inside of a broom closet by Steve.
Ten Things You Probably Didn't Know About Minecraft
Desperate for a purpose, she would begin to make videos from inside, reciting various pieces of trivia from her own twisted perspective.

The Secrets of Minecraft
At an unknown point in time, M.A.R.I.L.L.A. began collaborating with Narrator on a new series where they explained the secrets of Minecraft.
After filming 2,893,000 episodes, the two agreed to be interviewed by Tom Stone. During this, she appeared to suffer bouts of amnesia, not remembering she filmed the show until it turned out they would only be releasing nine episodes, 0.00031109574835811% of what was filmed. Through the interview she frequently mocked Narrator, and suffered multiple hardware failings, before lying about one to get out of the interview.
Before the penultimate episode of the series, M.A.R.I.L.L.A. went on a vacation to a swamp. In spite of telling him about this several times, Narrator forgot, and believed she had been kidnapped by Mobbo. While investigating, he had become enveloped in a conspiracy by Mobbo and Jonathan Minecraft, while M.A.R.I.L.L.A. remained blissfully unaware. In spite of this, the two proceeded to film their final episode together. During this, she received an update known as "M.A.X.I.M.U.M. M.A.R.I.L.L.A. mode," creating a digital avatar of a real human being, before discovering of the plans to add Mobbo. Wanting to prevent this at all costs, the two banded together to confront the developer trying to implement them, Jonathan Minecraft. They discover the conspiracy goes deeper than realized, with Jonathan weaponizing a machine to add Mobbo, successfully merging with the code before they could be stopped. With no other choice, the two heroes jumped into the machine themselves.

Around the Minecraft World in 80 Biomes
In the final episode of The Secrets of Minecraft, M.A.R.I.L.L.A. and Narrator learn that Jonathan Minecraft (Narrator's ex-husband) has been planning to add Mobbo into Minecraft using a machine that puts things inside Minecraft. Learning this, Mojang first sends Tiny Jens and Tiny Agnes as two incredible heroes. However, unfortunately, they both spawn in the middle of the ocean, so Mojang decides to send the less incredible M.A.R.I.L.L.A. and Narrator, as they're cheaper and agreed to accept Minecoins as payment. So they go on an adventure around the world through many biomes to kick Mobbo out and save the Overworld, while also making a show that gets good ratings again.
This becomes the series known as Around the Minecraft World in 80 Biomes.

Relationships
Enderman
A frequent running gag is her infatuation with endermen. M.A.R.I.L.L.A. often shows affection and adoration for the enderman. She calls him a "total cutie!" and crashes from excitement when the enderman comments on her video. She even has a personal ship folder about her and enderman being together. However, in a later episode, it turns out that the enderman and M.A.R.I.L.L.A. went out on a date. However, according to M.A.R.I.L.L.A., the enderman teleported immediately after the bill arrived and never responded to any of her texts again. Later in that same episode, M.A.R.I.L.L.A. encourages a bunch of hostile foxes wearing diamond armor to visit that enderman for revenge.

Trivia
The introductory sentences has minor differences depending on the video, some being in the greetings: "Hello!", "Hello there!", or "Hello again!" along with the glitching. The modem sound is also occasionally "[MODEM SOUND VARIATION 3582]" instead of the usual "[OLD MODEM SOUND]".
M.A.R.I.L.L.A. is the second computer character in the Minecraft franchise after PAMA.
The description card on Minecraft's YouTube channel has her as "Artificial Untelligence", [sic] "Sentient computer/worryingly immortal", "Excellent liar", etc. This changes each episode.
Narrator believes that "M.A.R.I.L.L.A. was created a few thousand years before rocks were invented."
Her name is likely based on her actress M.A.R.I.L.L.A. "Rill" Askey, as seen by the acronym having random words that spell out "M.A.R.I.L.L.A.."
The glitchy introductions feature M.A.R.I.L.L.A. displaying a mockup of the boot loader 'Kickstart' from the Commodore Amiga line of computers.
M.A.R.I.L.L.A. is apparently worth less than a non-fungible token.
M.A.R.I.L.L.A. has a deep disdain for blue wool and wishes to see it burn.
