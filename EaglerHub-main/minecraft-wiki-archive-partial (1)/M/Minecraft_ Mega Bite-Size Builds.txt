Synopsis
“Embark on another bite-size adventure! Build over 20 new mini projects in Minecraft, from duck party boats and houses on the moon to steam trains and fire engines! There's even a UFO that abducts cows!  With illustrated step-by-step instructions and tips throughout, this book will have you creating loads of fun builds to impress your friends!„
