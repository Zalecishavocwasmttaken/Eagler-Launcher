“
Go on an epic adventure across the Overworld, solving Minecraft puzzles as you go.
Embark on a wild journey through the Overworld, tackling mobs, hunting for treasure and striving to survive. Solve sudokus to fill your inventory, crack codes to translate villagers and follow iron golems through mazes to locate villages. Will you be able to muddle your way through this adventure and puzzle your way to the end of the story?
