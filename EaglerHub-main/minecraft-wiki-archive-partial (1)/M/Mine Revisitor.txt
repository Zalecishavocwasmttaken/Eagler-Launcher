The mine revisitor is a block present only in 25w14craftmine. It resembles a re-textured sculk shrieker.

Obtaining
The mine revisitor is unobtainable in Survival mode, however it generates at the hub upon creating a world and is in the Creative mode inventory.

Usage
The block can be used to re-visit mines the player has already generated.
Once used, the player is put in Adventure mode and gets transported to the mine desired to re-visit.
The player is also given an eye of exit once transported to the mine.
