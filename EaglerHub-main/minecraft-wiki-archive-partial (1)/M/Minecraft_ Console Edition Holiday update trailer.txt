Summary
Steve and Alex plan to get elytra for their Glide game to start. First, they sail across the ocean to reach a desert where they encounter a group of husks, which they avoided with ease. After reaching the village, they enter a temple-like structure with a staircase leading to a stronghold where the two go through the End portal. In the End, they defeat the ender dragon and manage to open an End gateway. Alex goes first, then Steve, to the outer End islands where they spawn in front of an end city. They avoid a shulker next to the door and go up the tower until they reach the bridge, finding an end ship farther away. They teleport using ender pearls to the ship where they retrieve their elytra, bringing them back to the Overworld and gliding across the map at night as fireworks shoot out.
