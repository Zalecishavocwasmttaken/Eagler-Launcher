Cover for Minecraft: Steve Rocks the Block (Original Soundtrack).
Minecraft: Steve Rocks the Block (Original Soundtrack) is an extended play featuring remixes of various music tracks of Minecraft music for Challenger Pack 7 in Super Smash Bros. Ultimate. It features compositions by Johan Johnson, Peter Hont, Gareth Coker, and Shauny Jang, with remixes by ACE (TOMOri Kudo / CHiCO), Yoko Shimomura, Mitsuhiro Kitadani, Yuzo Koshiro, Hiroki Hashimoto, and Motoi Sakuraba. It was released on February 10, 2026, but was originally made available from January 26 to January 31 on streaming services before being taken down silently.
The release marks the first and only official soundtrack release for Super Smash Bros. Ultimate, and the only one released outside of Nintendo.
