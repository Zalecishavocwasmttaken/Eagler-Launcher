Minecraft: Guide to Survival is a guide that contains various tips, tutorials, and guides to survive your first few days in a new world.

Synopsis
“The world of Minecraft is waiting to be explored. But danger lurks around every corner and survival can prove difficult for even the bravest adventurer.
The official Minecraft:Guide to Survival will help you stay alive. Learn how to find resources, craft equipment and protect yourself from hostile mobs.

With insider info and tips from the experts at Mojang, this is the definitive guide to survival for new Minecrafters.„
