Minecraft: Tetris (Original Soundtrack) is an extended play released for Minecraft featuring compositions by Charles Taylor, known professionally as "driftingaway", which consists of the songs that were released as part of the Tetris DLC. It was released on July 30, 2024, the same day that the DLC was released on the Marketplace.
