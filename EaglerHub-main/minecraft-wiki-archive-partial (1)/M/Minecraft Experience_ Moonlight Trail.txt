Minecraft Experience: Moonlight Trail is a in-person interactive events run by Experience Mod and Supply+Demand, in conjunction with Mojang Studios. This event held in outdoors at night, where participants in groups hike through various Minecraft biomes in a trail that goes up to a kilometer in length, leading to a final area where participants battle mobs to restore a beacon. The event is filled with special effects from flashing lights, to fog. The event was announced at Minecraft Live – March 2026. The first event took place in Buenos Aires, Argentina from May 15 to August 9, 2026. A second event is currently planned for Chile, with additional locations planned around the world. This was the second adventure for the Minecraft Experience after Minecraft Experience: Villager Rescue.

Summary



Spoiler warning! This section contains detailed information about Minecraft Experience: Moonlight Trail that may spoil your enjoyment of it. Read at your own risk!

Map of the experience
Entrance / registration
The experience begins with employees registering guests near "welcome tents". Guests enter the tents to prepare to enter the world of Minecraft.

Camp
Guests then hike to an area depicting a camp, where they gear up and learn about the legend of the fallen beacon.

The Ruins
Guests then hike to an area depicting ancient ruins, where they learn how to craft weapons and resources with the materials found around them.

The Cherry Grove
Guests then hike to an area depicting a cherry grove, where they learn to compose a melody that summons allays.

The Cave
Guests then hike to an area depicting a cave, where they dig for diamonds, fight off skeletons, and activate an amethyst geode.

The Waterfall
Guests then hike to an area depicting a waterfall, where they cast fishing rods to catch fish.

The Storm
Guests then hike through an area depicting a storm. Mobs invade the trail as lights flash and numerous sounds are heard.

The Battle
In the final area, guests use the weapons they have crafted and fight alongside each other to defeat the remaining mobs and restore the beacon.

Memorabilia
Minecraft Moonlight Trail Cape
Guests who have completed the experience will receive a code in the email (within 72 hours of the guest’s visit) which can be redeemed to obtain the Moonlight Trail Cape. The codes must be redeemed no later than 30 days after the event closes in the tour city where they were obtained.Cape promotional image

Times, locations, and pricing
Buenos Aires, Argentina
The first event took place from May 15 to August 9, 2026 in Buenos Aires, Argentina at Ciudad Universitaria de Buenos Aires. Parking was available at the venue at a cost of ARS$30,000 (~$22 USD).
Tickets for the event started at ARS$40,000 (~$36.50 USD), with VIP tickets costing ARS$70,000 (~$51 USD) and 4-pack tickets costing ARS$147,000 (~$106 USD). Children ages two and younger were allowed to enter if the payee payed a "compulsory insurance" fee of ARS$7,000 (~$5 USD), and anyone aged 15 or younger must have been accompanied by an adult.
