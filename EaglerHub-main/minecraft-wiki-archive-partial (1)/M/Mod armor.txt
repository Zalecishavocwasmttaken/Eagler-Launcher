Mod armor was an item exclusive to China Edition, previously used for add-on development. It has now become obsolete.

Obtaining
The item could be obtained through the use of addons and modifying NBT data, but it could not be obtained through the /give command.

Usage
In the early versions of China Edition, mod_armor was used for custom equipment and an item frame that allows customization and modification of equipment settings (only for the equipment slot area).
When worn, mod_armor provided 2 () armor points. The item was not worn on the head, but on the feet. It had 0 armor toughness by default.
The item used the old golden_helmet texture, and did not have a translated name; erroneously displaying the translation key ("item.mod_armor.name") in-game instead.
