Summary
As the narrator introduces the build challenge, a sunset occurs on a beach before the scene moves to the warehouse. To explain the gravity of the increase of plastic in the ocean, a lot of trash is shown underwater and on the beach around the bungalows. At the port, Axel builds a boat after the scene shifts to a jungle then to an ocean. After finishing the build, he uses the boat to remove the trash from the sea, before standing next to the warehouse keeper.

Transcript

Team Workbench presents Save The Ocean
an innovative challenge to express your creativity, while helping the ocean!
Nowadays, there is more plastic in the ocean than living things
This is concerning!
So we want you to help mother earth,
by coming up with creative solutions to eliminate plastics from the ocean
You are helping our planet, but also the creatures that live in these oceans
Here is your challenge
Use your imagination to build a vehicle, or something else
which gets the plastic out of the oceans
Good luck! We believe in you
