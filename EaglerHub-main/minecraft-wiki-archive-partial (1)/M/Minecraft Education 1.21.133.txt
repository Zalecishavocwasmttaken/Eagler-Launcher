1.21.133 (Windows, ChromeOS, Android) or 1.21.133.2 (macOS, iOS) is a minor update to Minecraft Education, released for macOS, ChromeOS, and Android on April 28, 2026, and for Windows and iOS on April 30, 2026, which fixes some bugs. This is the final version of any edition to use the old version formatting.
