Minecraft Quest Journal is a children's activity book described as "a must-have companion journal for playing Minecraft, featuring checklists, prompts, dares, and fun facts to enhance gameplay."

Summary
“The ultimate companion book to your Minecraft game, this journal includes pro tips, checklists, fun facts, and dangerous dares for Minecraft players who are looking for a guidebook and tracking log for their awesome achievements. Plus, drawing and writing prompts throughout the book allow the player to personalize and document their Minecraft journey. Perfect for every kind of Minecraft fan, this interactive journal will provide the reader with new information they might not know and encourage them to try out new things in the game.„ — Book Description
