Matt Berry is an English actor, known for his roles in comedy series.

In the Minecraft franchise
Main article: Movie:Nitwit
Berry entered talks to join the cast of A Minecraft Movie in May 2023 In the film, Berry was the primary voice of the Nitwit, a villager that falls in love with and later gets engaged to Vice Principal Marlene.
At Minecraft Live – May 2026, Matt Berry was announced to be playing an undisclosed role in the movie's sequel, A Minecraft Movie Squared.
