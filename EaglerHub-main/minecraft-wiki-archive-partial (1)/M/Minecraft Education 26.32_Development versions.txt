This is a list of Minecraft Education 26.32 development versions.

 Preview 26.30.0
26.30.0 (Windows, ChromeOS, Android, iOS) or 26.30.1 (macOS) is the first and only Preview version for Minecraft Education 26.32, released for macOS, ChromeOS, Android, and iOS on June 25, 2026, and for Windows on July 14, 2026, which adds features from the Tiny Takeover and Chaos Cubed game drops.

Additions
Main articles: Bedrock Edition 26.0, Bedrock Edition 26.10, Bedrock Edition 26.20, and Bedrock Edition 26.30
Added features from Bedrock Edition 26.0 to Bedrock Edition 26.30, Tiny Takeover and Chaos Cubed.
Closed captions are not available in the accessibility settings, and can only be toggled in options.txt. Even when toggled on, only sounds that play in animations can produce subtitles, such as wardens emerging, roaring, digging, tendrils clicking, armadillos rolling up, armadillos peeking, and breezes whirling. Also subtitles like "Spear hits" and "Bubble meter dropping".
