Key art
MINECON Earth 2017 Skin Pack is a celebratory skin pack for MINECON Earth 2017 that includes 8 skins themed around MINECON. In addition to the skin pack's release on the storefronts of Bedrock Edition and Legacy Console Edition, a ZIP archive containing the skins for Java Edition was available on Minecraft.net.

Skins

Name

Render

Description

Notes

Cake

A miniaturized version of Steve, holding a cake above his head.

The skin is an optical illusion, the head being a cake and Steve's torso making up both the legs and body.

Alex Globe

A miniaturized version of Alex, holding a globe above her head.

Similar to the aforementioned skin.

Blue Sheep

An unknown person in a costume of a humanoid sheep.

Although called blue, the shade of the sheep's wool more accurately resembles the palette used for light blue.

Cardboard Cosplayer

A person wearing armor made out of cardboard.

Tiny Jens would wear similar armor in the 2022 Mob Vote Event.

Party Alex

A stylized version of Alex wearing a green dress.

Party Steve

A stylized version of Steve with a gaping maw and white buttoned shirt.

Piñata

An unknown person wearing a pinata costume with the face of a creeper.

Creeper Cosplay

A miniaturized character dressed like a creeper, holding a block of TNT.
