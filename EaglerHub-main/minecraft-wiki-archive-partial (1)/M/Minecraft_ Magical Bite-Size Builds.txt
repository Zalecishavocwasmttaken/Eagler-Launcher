Synopsis
“Add a touch of magic to your Overworld with these 20 new mini-projects in Minecraft, from mermaid lagoons and genie lamps to dragons' lairs and enchanting towers. There's even a giant magician's hat that spawns rabbits!  With illustrated step-by-steo instructions and tips throughout, this book will have you creating loads of magical builds to impress your friends with!„
