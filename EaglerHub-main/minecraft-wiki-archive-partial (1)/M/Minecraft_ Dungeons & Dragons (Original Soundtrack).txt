Minecraft: Dungeons & Dragons (Original Soundtrack) is the first soundtrack released for Minecraft by Kyle Martin Cora, known professionally as "Kylydian", which consists of the songs that were released as part of the Dungeons & Dragons DLC. It was released on September 26, 2023, the same day that the DLC was released on the Marketplace.
