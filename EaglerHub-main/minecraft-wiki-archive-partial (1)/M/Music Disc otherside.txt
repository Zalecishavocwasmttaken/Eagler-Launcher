"otherside" refers to both a song composed by Lena Raine, and a music disc that plays the song when inserted in a jukebox. The music disc is obtained from various structures, but is most commonly found in ancient cities.

Previews of tracks in Minecraft have been shortened to 30 seconds on this wiki in accordance with fair use rationale. "11" and "Magnetic Circuit" are exempt from this.

"otherside" starts off as an uplifting and happy retro-style ostinato in a major key. Upon reaching the second half, the song changes to a minor key and progresses into a darker melody, eventually cutting off abruptly. A clock can be heard ticking quickly at the end.

Usage
Like all music discs, using the "otherside" music disc on a jukebox causes the jukebox to play the song.
If a redstone comparator is reading a jukebox playing "otherside", it will emit a redstone signal of 14.
