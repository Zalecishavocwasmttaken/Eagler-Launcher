Minecraft x Ben 10 DLC: Official Trailer is a trailer promoting the release of the Ben 10 DLC. The video serves as a preview of the sequence of events within the world, with screenshots from the release being used on the Marketplace store page.

Summary
The video opens on a shot of Ben, Gwen, and Max huddled together in front of the Rustbucket, before seemingly handing an Omnitrix to the player, who selects Heatblast and propels over an ocean. Later, Four Arms rushes into a Funfair, and takes on Zombozo. XLR8 rushes through the streets of Bellwood, while Rath runs on all fours on top of an office building and slashes wooden boards.
The next boss shown is Dr. Animo, before Slapback frees a pig from a containment cell. Kevin 11 makes himself known and taunts, before turning into Quad Smack. Cannonbolt rolls. Hex shifts through several pages of a spell book. At Billions Tower, Billy Billions shrugs, before Shock Rock shoots a projectile at him. In a separate location, two players wave at Shock Rock, before turning into Diamondhead and Jetray, running up to the Forever Knight's castle. In two separate locations, Cannonbolt and Rath run together, while Heatblast and Humungousuar fight on a rooftop. As they fire, Rath grabs a token. Shock Rock, Heatblast, and XLR8 converge on a road, before the player from the beginning meets back up with Ben and Gwen. Their final challenge is to take on Vilgax himself, but they're not alone, Ben turning into Four Arms himself to even the odds.
