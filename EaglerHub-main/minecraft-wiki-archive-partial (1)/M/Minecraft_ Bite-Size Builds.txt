Synopsis
“Learn how to design, build and customise 20 mini-projects in Minecraft, from firefighter planes and deep-sea submarines to hidden bunkers and arcade games.  There's even a superhero flying school! With detailed instructions, block hacks and build tips, this book will teach you new tricks and practise old skills as you create bite-size builds to impress your friends in Minecraft.„
