Summary
The video starts with the player collecting dirt, sand, and wood to craft planks in order to build a base. Then, the next day, the player joins two other players with the Steve skin who are busy mining sand. Then, the player shoots at a skeleton, then at a creeper in the water. Afterwards, they go down underground to mine diamonds alongside another player, before the video closes in front of the player's base.
