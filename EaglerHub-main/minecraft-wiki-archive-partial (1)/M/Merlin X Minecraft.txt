Summary
The video opens in the perspective of a rider on a mountaintop roller coaster where a creeper on the way gets rammed down by a rushing goat, freeing the way. The rider then descends rapidly down the mountain, through a forest alongside some bees and into a cave where they pass through a brightly lit opening. They find themselves riding a real life roller coaster as unseen people cheer on, before entering a Nether portal upside down and ending up in a dark void with fireworks and the logos.
