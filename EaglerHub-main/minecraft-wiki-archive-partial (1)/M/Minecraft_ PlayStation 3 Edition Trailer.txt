Minecraft: PlayStation 3 Edition Trailer is a trailer promoting the release of PlayStation 3 Edition produced by Hat Films.

Summary
Steve overlooks a coastal scenery from above a cliff, before heading to plains where they wield a wooden sword and meet a pig and Athlete Steve, who wields a stone sword. The two play together on splitscreen; they gather wood from a tree and build a house. As they fight zombies inside a monster room, Scottish Steve and Tuxedo Steve arrive to join the duo, and the four play on splitscreen together. One player gathers wood in a snowy taiga, another is busy building a wooden house, and the two others mine stone. Back in the plains, Steve finishes building a large stone house, and invites their friends, along with Boxer Steve, Cyclist Steve, Prisoner Steve, and Tennis Steve into the house. The team then runs across the plains at night when a creeper explodes in the background, then goes to the Nether where they fight zombie pigmen, light fires, look at the map, and run along. Back in the Overworld, the team continues their activities and visits the wooden house before running across the plains.
