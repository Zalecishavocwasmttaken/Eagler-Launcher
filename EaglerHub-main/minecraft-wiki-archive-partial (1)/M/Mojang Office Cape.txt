The Mojang Office Cape is a cape which was obtainable by completing all five daily challenges in the Eerie Mojang Office Party.

Appearance
The cape's design includes one large, opened eyeblossom as the centerpiece with additional, smaller eyeblossoms around it. It is displayed on a dark background, mimicking the night when eyeblossoms bloom.

Distribution
Main article: Eerie Mojang Office Party
The Mojang Office cape was obtained by completing all five daily challenges in the Eerie Mojang Office Party event server.

History
November 25, 2024
This cape was first mentioned on November 25, 2024 on the code redemption section of Minecraft's website. At the time, it was called the "Eyeblossom Cape". The text implied that by purchasing a Creeper Cape in any size from The Official Shop of Minecraft, an in-game Eyeblossom Cape would be received. It was removed a few hours later.November 28, 2024
Some players note that the store page for the Creeper Cape was updated to include the information that purchasing this cape would unlock an exclusive in-game cape on December 7th, 2024.December 2, 2024
The text about purchasing the cape granting an in-game one is removed from the store page. It is later replaced with a promotion including a Bedrock Character Creator item.December 9, 2024
The Eerie Mojang Office Party event server starts.December 13, 2024
The final daily challenge is revealed, making the cape obtainable.December 20, 2024
The Eerie Mojang Office Party event server ends.
Trivia
This cape has also been referred to as the Eyeblossom Cape in official media.​[more information needed]
