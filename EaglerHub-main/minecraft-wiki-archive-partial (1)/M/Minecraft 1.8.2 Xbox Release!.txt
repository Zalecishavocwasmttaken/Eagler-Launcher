Minecraft 1.8.2 Xbox Release! is a trailer promoting the release of the 1.8.2 Update (Title Update 5) for the Xbox 360 Edition.

Summary
In a desert village, a pig and a sheep come out of their houses and run across the farms, each taking their respectively colored wool path. Once they reach the end of both paths, they meet at a minecart station and ride along, activating a redstone mechanism that causes 1.8.2 to show on a cliffside. They travel across the desert and a forest, each taking different paths before they both fall into a cave, eating fish and steak to heal from the damage. After seeing a mineshaft above raised terrain, Pig breaks a block to make way, causing a silverfish to come out. In the mineshaft, both encounter an enderman on a wooden bridge, killing it after. Then, they encounter a cave spider in the spawner room. They then change their mode to Creative and move on to a cave where a group of players build the Xbox logo out of green wool and blocks of iron, helping them.
