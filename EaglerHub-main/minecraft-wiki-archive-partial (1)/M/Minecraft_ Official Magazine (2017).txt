Minecraft: Official Magazine was a series of Minecraft magazines published by Egmont in cooperation with Mojang Studios. Throughout the magazine's run, 14 issues were published starting in July 2017  and ending in 2019.
Each magazine was packed in a cardboard box containing the magazine itself with two sheets of stickers. The magazine had 60 pages full of news, build inspirations, guides,one comic, and a quiz at the end. Notably, these featured four new characters created by Trudi Castle.

Issue 11
The eleventh issue of the magazine, named "Treasure Awaits", has light lush cave theming, containing information on foliage of multiple kinds and information about certain aquatic mobs.

Issue 12
The twelfth issue of the magazine, named "Deep Sea Diving", has an underwater theme, containing guides on aquariums and potions and an interactive fight against the blaze.

Issue 13
The thirteenth issue of the magazine, named "Climbing Challenges", has a mountainous theme, with sections about goats and zombie villagers.
