Synopsis
“
Discover all there is to know about the past year of Minecraft in this exciting annual. Stuffed to the brim with updates, activities, step-by-step builds and tips and tricks for the game, this book is a must-have for any minecrafter.

This year’s annual includes a biome personality quiz, loads of fun activities and tips on surviving a trial chamber [sic], where to find all the wolf variants and how to loot an ancient city. Plus discover a mob board game, awesome builds and survival challenges for you to complete in-game!„ — Book description
