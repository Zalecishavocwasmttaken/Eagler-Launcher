Summary



This section needs expansion. 
You can help by expanding it.

The map takes place in a large generic mansion, with several paths and a hedge maze outside, in which the player must navigate and solve puzzles and challenges. The plot revolves around saving the protagonist's friends from Endermen, several of whom are seen in glass cages. At one point, the player must collect several keys to progress.
