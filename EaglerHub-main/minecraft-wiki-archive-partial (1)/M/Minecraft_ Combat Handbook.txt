Minecraft: Combat Handbook is a guide to Minecraft combat first published in 2014, and an updated version was published in 2015. Therein the handbook are several tips from Minecraft Youtubers of the time including Paul Soares Jr., FyreUK, and CNB MInecraft.

Synopsis
“In Minecraft, you're never alone and the threat of attack is constant. How can you survive?The official Combat Handbook will teach you everything you need to know to defend yourself from hostile monsters and enemy players.Learn how to build a fort, craft armor and weapons, set mob traps, defeat enemies in one-on-one combat, and battle your way out of the Nether and the End. With tips from many Minecraft experts, as well as the team behind the game Mojang, you'll be a formidable Minecraft warrior in no time! „ — Original & Updated description
