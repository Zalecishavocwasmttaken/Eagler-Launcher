Synopsis
“Here's everything you need to know to get connected to the electrifying possibilities of redstone.From basic mining and logic circuits to more advanced mechanics, this book will give you step-by-step guide instructions to get you fully wired to redstone. With builds including redstone traps and arrow launchers, you'll never fear creepers again!Plus, see some of the most amazing community creations from the very best redstone builders - they'll blow your mind!„ — Old description

Trivia
The book claims witches were added in Alpha 1.4.2, a version that does not exist. Witches were added in the full release version of 1.4.2.
The book claims you can mine redstone ore with a golden pickaxe to obtain redstone dust. Mining redstone ore with a golden pickaxe will not drop anything.
