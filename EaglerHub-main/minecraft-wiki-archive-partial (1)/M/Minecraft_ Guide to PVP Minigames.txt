Synopsis
“When it comes to making your own fun in Minecraft, the player-versus-player minigame options are endless, and the only limit is your imagination.
The official Minecraft: Guide to PVP Minigames contains some of the best games for you to re-create and play with friends in your own world.

With insider info and tips from the experts at Mojang, this is the definitive guide to building and playing PVP minigames in Minecraft.„

Description

Introduction

5

An introduction to the book, written by Marsh Davies.

Mob Waves

8-11

How to build and play a mini game about fighting off waves of mobs.

Arrow Golf

12-15

How to build a golf course, and instructions on how to play golf with a bow and arrows.

Hoop Shoot Match

16-21

How to build and play Hoop Shoot, a mini game about shooting buttons with arrows.

Elytra Ace

22-25

An elytra race reminiscent of Legacy Console Edition's Glide mini game, but with the added challenge of mid-air PvP.

Wool Race

26-29

How to build and play Wool Race, a game focused on shearing as many sheep as possible in a set time, with each color determing the points received.

Skywars

32-35

Loose instructions on how to build a skywars map, with a small overview of how the game works.

Spleef Tournament

36-39

How to build a spleef arena, with instructions on how to play.

Capture the Flag

40-45

How to build a capture the flag map, with tips on making it more intersection.

Gladiator Combat

46-49

How to build a gladiator arena, with ideas to make it more challenging.

Battle Parkour

50-53

How to build a simple parkour course, with a guide on how to add PvP elements.

Connecting With Other Players

56-57

An overview of Bedrock Edition's menu, specifically the multiplayer settings, the server list, and the join a friend tab.

Realms

58-61

A guide on how to customize your Bedrock Realms world.

The Character Class System

62-69

Six different inventory sets, used as examples of a class system.

Command Blocks

70-77

A guide on how to use a few commands with command blocks.

Final Words

78-79

A thanks and final goodbye written by Marsh Davies.

Reception
During a piece by the website Create in the Chaos, Guide to PVP Minigames was recommended by the writer as a personal favorite.
