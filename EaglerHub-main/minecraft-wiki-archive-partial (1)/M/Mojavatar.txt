A Mojavatar (a portmanteau of mojang and avatar) is an avatar created to represent a Mojang Studios employee. Almost all developers and employees at Mojang have their own Mojavatar. Mojavatars can usually be seen in news articles on Minecraft.net or in the respective social media profiles of Mojang staff.
The current Mojavatars are made with the Mojavatar Maker, an internal private program for creating 3D avatars in a style similar to the Minecraft look. While this current iteration is most common, the two previous styles are still in use by some developers to this day, and can still be seen in some news articles.

Mojavatar Maker
The Mojavatar Maker is the avatar creator program made by Mojang Studios to assist in creating new avatar images for the new or current staff. It features a Minecraft-related 3D "chibi" design with low-resolution pixel art for the avatars, and includes a large variety of cosmetics, hair styles, and other customizations.
It is intended solely for internal use by the Mojang Studios team, and there is no currently no public version available for use.

2021–present: Mojavatar Maker
As the number of Mojang employees grew, it became no longer viable to create avatars manually. Following the rebranding of Mojang Studios in 2021, a group of developers created an internal program, the Mojavatar Maker, to produce avatars more quickly and easily.
This is the current version of the Mojavatars. They are presented as low-resolution, "chibi"-style 3D models, currently used by almost all employees.

Contest winners
Two first-generation Mojavatars were awarded to non-developers through a contest held during a Minecraft Marathon event.
The first person outside of Mojang to use the Mojavatar Maker was a girl named Star, who won a Blue Peter competition in 2023 and created her own Mojavatar during a tour of Mojang Studios in Stockholm.
Another person granted access to the Mojavatar Maker was Lucie, a modder who won an auction and received the opportunity to visit Mojang’s office in Stockholm.
