The title "Pigstep" is a portmanteau of "piglin" and "dubstep".
The song is an intense, somewhat hip-hop-style beat beginning with a repeating cello-like tune and dubstep-style drop, along with more mellow parts reminiscent of a radio. The song was featured in A Minecraft Movie.
The album release of "Pigstep" includes two versions: the mono mix used in the game and a stereo mix with more usage of multi-directional sound.

Usage
Like all music discs, using the "Pigstep" music disc on a jukebox causes the jukebox to play the song.
If a redstone comparator is reading a jukebox playing "Pigstep", it emits a redstone signal of 13.

Trivia
Part of "Pigstep" can be heard in Minecraft Dungeons when equipping Golden Piglin Armor.
In A Minecraft Movie, Malgosha is seen dancing to Pigstep while doing an audition for Nether's Got Talent in a flashback.
A remix of "Pigstep" known as "Pigstep (Just Dance Version)" is a playable song in Just Dance +.
In Minecraft Legends, sometimes after killing a patrol of piglins, an part of "Pigstep" can be heard.
