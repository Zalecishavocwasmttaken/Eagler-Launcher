Minecraft: Tricky Trials (Original Game Soundtrack), also known as Minecraft: 1.21 Soundtrack on some platforms, is the fourth soundtrack for Minecraft featuring Lena Raine, as well as the second featuring Aaron Cherof and Kumi Tanioka. It has been released on various music platforms since April 26, 2024.
The album includes 12 songs for a total length of nearly an hour. It serves as the soundtrack for the Tricky Trials update and includes three new music disc tracks.

Trivia
The word komorebi (木漏[こも]れ日[び]) means "sunlight filtering through trees" in Japanese.
The word pokopoko (ポコポコ) is an onomatopoeia in Japanese, which simulates the sound of bubbles popping or a hollow object being knocked.
According to the artist, in this track she "wanted to express a bird's-eye view of the world of Minecraft while delving a little more freely into it".
The word yakusoku (約束, やくそく) means "promise" in Japanese.
Eld is an archaic English word, meaning "old".
Some tracks in this album feature melodies similar to previous soundtracks:
Part of melodies in Watcher resemble Bromeliad and Echo in the Wind.
Part of melodies in komorebi resemble Comforting Memories.
Part of melodies in Deeper and Endless resemble Infinite Amethyst.
Part of melodies in Puzzlebox resemble Aria Math.
Part of background synth accompaniments in the beginning of Precipice resemble Bromeliad.
Melodies near the end of Precipice resemble Moog City or Moog City 2.
