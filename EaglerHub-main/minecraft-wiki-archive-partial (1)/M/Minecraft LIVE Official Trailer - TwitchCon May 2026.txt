Summary
The plot begins with a sulfur cube absorbing an oak log. Alex beats it off with an iron shovel, which launches the sulfur cube, bouncing off a sheep and then a cat. The sulfur cube collides with other companions and a pack of wolves follows them. Five sulfur cubes are then seen flying at Steve. They then try to swing their axe at a sulfur cube, which is knocked by a goat instead. The sulfur cubes and goat slide on ice, and Kai and Alex follow them. One sulfur cube knocks down a chicken and enters a sulfur cave. A sulfur cube with ignited TNT bounces over a sulfur cube with ice and the cube with TNT explodes, launching several sulfur cubes along with Alex and Kai out of the cave. Finally, they camera pans to Agnes, Jens, and Steve on a stage with a live audience.
