Minecraft Live: September 2025 (Original Soundtrack) is the soundtrack released for Minecraft LIVE – September 2025, featuring compositions by Camilo Forero, and Niccolo Pacella , which feature all of the music included as part of the event. The soundtrack was released on September 28, 2025.
