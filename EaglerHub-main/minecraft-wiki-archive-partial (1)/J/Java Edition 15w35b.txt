15w35b is the fifteenth snapshot for Java Edition 1.9, released on August 24, 2015 to fix various bugs, including problems with zombie AI being disabled, block rendering in the End, and End crystals respawning.

MC-86984 – Newly placed blocks in the End are invisible in new chunks.
