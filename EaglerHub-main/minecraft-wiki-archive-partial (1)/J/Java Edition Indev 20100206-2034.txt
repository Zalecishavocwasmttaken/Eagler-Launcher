The initial version of Minecraft Indev was released on February 6, 2010, at 20:34 UTC.  It was announced on The Word of Notch at 20:43 UTC. It introduced farming, hoes, seeds, wheat crops and bread.

Additions
Minecraft Indev block and item additions
Blocks
  Farmland

Made by right-clicking a grass block or dirt block using a hoe.
Become moistened by water, infinite water sources and infinite lava sources.
Can turn back to dirt by walking on it.
Is unobtainable by normal means.
 Wheat crops

Grow in 8 stages.
Stages below 8 drop nothing.
 Stage 8 drops wheat and seeds.
Items
 Bread

New food item.
Restores 5HP health.
Crafted with wheat.

 Hoes

Can be used to create farmland blocks.
Crafted with stick and planks, cobblestone, iron ingot, diamond.

 Seeds

Can be grown into a wheat crop by planting it on farmland.
Obtainable by using a hoe on a grass block.
 Wheat

Collected by destroying stage 8 wheat crops.
Can be used to craft bread.

General
Performance

Huge performance improvements.
Debug screen

Added "C", "F", "O", "B", "I", and "T".
C is rendered/total chunks.
F is chunks outside the field of view.
O is chunks culled by occlusion culling.
E is rendered/total entities.
B is entities culled by being in a chunk that can't be seen.
I is individually culled entities.
T is "terrain and lighting data".
Removed "LT".
