13w10b is the fifteenth and final snapshot for Java Edition 1.5, released on March 6, 2013 to fix some of the bugs and crashes in the 13w10a snapshot.

Changes
Blocks
Dispensers and droppers

Now only fire once when they receive a redstone signal.
To fire again, the signal must be turned off and on again. (Previously they would fire whenever they had a signal and a nearby block changed)
Fixes
24 issues fixed
From released versions before 1.5

MC-151 – Pushing TNT with a piston and activating it at the same time duplicates it.
MC-266 – Placing a solid block near TNT and above redstone torch won't update the TNT.
MC-846 – Dispenser and dropper shooting out when updated.
MC-3259 – Long redstone wire does not update/behaves strange in newly loaded chunks.
MC-3458 – Server crashes if spaces are input on the console.
MC-6223 – Night Vision Potions that are edited to last longer create a potentially seizure-inducing flashing sky background.
MC-10956 – Animals can breed over distances up to almost 8 blocks when only separated by fences.
From the 1.5 development versions

MC-5961 – Droppers and dispensers aren't triggered by 1 or 2 tick pulses.
MC-6047 – Comparator strings fail to transmit 1-tick pulses.
MC-7654 – Items disappearing of droppers in some situations.
MC-8022 – Furnace interface occasionally closes when adding fuel, deleting all items in the furnace.
MC-8597 – Named mobs don't have named babies.
MC-8790 – Scoreboard teams function does not accept @p or @r arguments.
MC-9685 – Comparators are still very inconsistent in 13w06a.
MC-9818 – Non translatable error message "Could not clear the inventory of <player>, no items to remove".
MC-10357 – Getting into a minecart with a mob in it crashes minecraft.
MC-10881 – Comparator coming off of chests, droppers, hoppers ... behave strangely.
MC-11044 – Adding items to brewing stand using dropper does not change state of comparator.
From the previous development version

MC-10946 – 2 hoppers funneling unstackable items into a 3rd hopper at the same time cause the 3rd hopper to not funnel items into a container.
MC-10948 – Dispensers continually shoot/use certain items like water buckets and flint&steel.
MC-10950 – Comparators still giving off power when hopper inventory is empty.
MC-10995 – Message "THIS TOO" in console.
MC-10997 – Powered dispensers and droppers update when a normal chest is opened or closed within 2 blocks.
MC-11019 – Full hoppers feeding into full hoppers causes the forward hopper to stall.
