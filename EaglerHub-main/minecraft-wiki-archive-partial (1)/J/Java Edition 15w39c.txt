15w39c is the twenty-eighth snapshot for Java Edition 1.9, released on September 23, 2015 to fix several bugs and improve performance.

Changes
Blocks
Dispensers

Now equip pumpkins and mob heads onto the heads of players, mobs and armor stands.
General
Performance

Redstone and piston contraptions visibly update much faster.
Better performance on older computers.
Fixes
10 issues fixed
From released versions before 1.9

MC-8199 – Mob heads and pumpkins cannot be equipped by dispenser/through shift-click.
MC-44959 – Zombie and skeleton horses don't count as undead.
MC-54120 – Wrong creeper hitbox & eye Level.
MC-54615 – Villagers' and skeletons' hitbox needs to be adjusted.
From the 1.9 development versions

MC-82835 – Spamming ender pearls in creative temporarily loses them.
MC-83746 – Framerate drop in 1.9 snapshots.
MC-86289 – The statistics of shields will show "item.shield.name" instead of the "Shield".
MC-87411 – Zombie villager leg has transparent pixels.
MC-88315 – Consistent chunk updates after respawn.
MC-89302 – Block lag.
