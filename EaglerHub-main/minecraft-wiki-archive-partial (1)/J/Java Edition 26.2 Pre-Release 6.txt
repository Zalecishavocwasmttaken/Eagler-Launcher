26.2 Pre-Release 6 (known as 26.2-pre-6 in the launcher) is the sixth and final pre-release for Java Edition 26.2, released on June 10, 2026.

Changes
General
Game rules

Respawning as a spectator in a Hardcore world no longer turns off the spectators_generate_chunks game rule.
Any older worlds are "upgraded" to retroactively apply this change to them as well.
