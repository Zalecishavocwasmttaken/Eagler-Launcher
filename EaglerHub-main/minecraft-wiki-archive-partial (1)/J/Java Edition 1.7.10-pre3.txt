1.7.10-pre3 is the third pre-release for Java Edition 1.7.10, released on June 10, 2014, to fix some bugs and a crash in 1.7.10-pre2 related to the disconnect screen.

Other fixes

Trivia
This version was previously removed from the launcher for some time, but was added back in September 2018.
This version came out exactly six years before 1.16 Pre-release 3.
