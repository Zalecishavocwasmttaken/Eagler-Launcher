0.0.22a is a version of Java Edition Classic released on June 29, 2009, at 21:56 UTC, which added sounds and music by C418.
0.0.22a was reuploaded at 21:58 UTC (2 minutes after release) to fix players not being able to connect to servers.

Additions
Gameplay
Sounds

Grass, gravel, stone, and wood sounds. Used as footsteps and breaking sounds.
Grass sounds were used for dirt, leaves, sponge, cloth.
Gravel sounds were used for sand, gravel.
Stone sounds were used for stone, cobblestone, gold ore, iron ore, coal ore. High pitched stone sounds were used for glass, block of gold.
Wood sounds were used for planks, wood.
Added unused wood click.ogg sound.
Music

Added music by C418: calm1.ogg, calm2.ogg, calm3.ogg
Temporarily added music by Notch: calm4.ogg (originally was an unused test song, but was replaced by a duplicate of calm2.ogg in a reupload)
Changes
Blocks
 Lava

Texture changed from .
It now appears brighter overall.
Technical
General
Replaced the FPS meter with a frame-rate limiter.
FPS is now limited to 200.
Removals
FPS no longer displays on the screen.[verify for Java Edition]
Bugs
4 bugs reported

Pre-reupload: Players can no longer connect to servers.
Sounds are very laggy and choppy in this version for some users.
Walking sounds continue for a while after the player stops moving.
Breaking a plant in darkness no longer produces particles.[verify for Java Edition]
