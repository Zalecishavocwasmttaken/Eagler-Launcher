Alpha v1.0.13_01 is a version of Java Edition Alpha released on July 29, 2010, to fix a crash in Alpha v1.0.13.
Alpha v1.0.13_01 was reuploaded to change the logo to "1K IN 24h" to celebrate 1000 sales in the last 24 hours.
