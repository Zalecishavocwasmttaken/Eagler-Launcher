1.14.3 Pre-Release 1 (known as 1.14.3-pre1 in the launcher) is the first pre-release for Java Edition 1.14.3, released on June 3, 2019, which makes small changes to villagers and fixes some bugs left over from 1.14.2.

Changes
Mobs
Villagers

Panicking villagers now have a higher chance of spawning iron golems.
Farmers now spend more time farming when they are working.
Farmers now always give away food even if other villagers do not need it.
General
Performance

Improved performance.
