Generates when spawning a copper golem.

Ingredients

Pickaxe

Can be used by copper golems, it will visit up to 10 copper chests in sequence to try to take out up to 16 of the first item contained within.
The search area for copper chests from the copper golem's position is horizontally 32 blocks and vertically 8 blocks.

Copper golem statue

Copper golem statues are created when a copper golem fully oxidizes.

Any

Interacting with a copper golem statue changes its pose, cycling through four poses: standing, sitting, running, and star.
Unwaxed copper golem statues have four stages of oxidation. Lightning bolts and axes can remove the oxidation on copper golems statues. Using a honeycomb on a copper golems statues prevents it from oxidizing.
Using an axe on an unoxidized unwaxed copper golem statue will reanimate it into an unoxidized unwaxed copper golem.
When they become a statue, they will freeze in a random pose.

Shelf

Ingredients
Crafting recipe

Matching Stripped Log or Block of Stripped Bamboo or Matching Stripped Stem

6666666666666

Axe

The shelf can store up to 3 item stacks (slots).
While a shelf is powered by redstone, its front texture changes to reflect that it's powered; when placed next to each other, up to 3 powered shelves (of the same wood type) will connect together.
Shelves can be used as a fuel in furnaces, smelting 1.5 items per shelf. Crimson and warped shelves cannot be used as a fuel in furnaces.

Copper bars

Ingredients
Crafting recipe

Copper Ingot

16

Pickaxe

Functions like the existing iron bars.
Non-waxed copper bars have four stages of oxidation (including the initial normal state). Lightning bolts and axes can remove the oxidation on copper bars. They can be waxed with honeycomb to prevent oxidation from progressing.
Like other copper blocks, the waxed variant can be obtained by using a honeycomb item on the block.

Items
New

Name

Copper pickaxe

Ingredients
Crafting recipe

Copper Ingot +Stick

A copper pickaxe is used to break stone and metal-based materials faster. Copper pickaxes are the same tier as stone pickaxes, but have more durability.
Copper pickaxes can perform all the functions that other pickaxes can.

Copper sword

Ingredients
Crafting recipe

Copper Ingot +Stick

A copper sword is used to deal damage to mobs and entities by attacking them. Copper swords are the same tier as stone swords, dealing 5HP base damage in Java Edition, but have more durability.
Copper swords can perform all the functions that other swords can.

Copper axe

Ingredients
Crafting recipe

Copper Ingot +Stick

A copper axe is used to break wood-related blocks faster than other tools, and can also be used as a weapon. Copper axes are the same tier as stone axes, but have more durability.
Copper axes can perform all the functions that other axes can.

Copper shovel

Ingredients
Crafting recipe

Copper Ingot +Stick

A copper shovel can be used to break dirt and soil-type materials faster. Copper shovels are the same tier as stone shovels, but have more durability.
Copper shovels can perform all the functions that other shovels can.

Copper hoe

Ingredients
Crafting recipe

Copper Ingot +Stick

A copper hoe is used convert various dirt blocks into farmland and also to harvest various plant blocks, Nether fungi, and sculk blocks more quickly. Copper hoes are the same tier as stone hoes, but have more durability.
Copper hoes can perform all the actions that other hoes can.

Copper helmet

Ingredients
Crafting recipe

Copper Ingot

When worn, a copper helmet provides 2 () armor points.
A copper helmet can be repaired in an anvil by adding copper ingots, with each ingot restoring 25% the helmet's maximum durability, rounded down. Two copper helmets can also be combined in an anvil.
Two damaged copper helmets can be also combined through crafting or using the grindstone. The durability of the used copper helmets are added together, plus an extra 5% durability.

Copper chestplate

Ingredients
Crafting recipe

Copper Ingot

When worn, a copper chestplate provides 6 () armor points.
Two damaged copper chestplates can be combined through crafting or using the grindstone. The durability of the used copper chestplates are added together, plus an extra 5% durability.
A copper chestplate can also be repaired in an anvil by adding copper ingots, with each ingot restoring 25% the copper chestplates' maximum durability, rounded down. Two copper chestplates of the same tier can also be combined in an anvil.

Copper leggings

Ingredients
Crafting recipe

Copper Ingot

When worn, copper leggings provide 5 () armor points.
Two damaged pairs of copper leggings can be combined through crafting, using the grindstone or the anvil. The durability of the used copper leggings is added together, plus an extra 5% durability.
A copper leggings can also be repaired in an anvil by adding copper ingots, with each ingot restoring 25% the copper leggings' maximum durability, rounded down.

Copper boots

Ingredients
Crafting recipe

Copper Ingot

When worn, copper boots provide 2 () armor points.
Two damaged pairs of copper boots can be combined through crafting, using the grindstone or the anvil. The durability of the used copper boots is added together, plus an extra 5% durability.
A copper boots can be also repaired in an anvil by adding units of the tiers' repair material, with each repair material restoring 25% the boots' maximum durability, rounded down. Two copper boots of the same tier can also be combined in an anvil.

Copper horse armor

Can be found in the chests of monster rooms, weaponsmith's houses in villages, End cities, Nether fortresses, strongholds, jungle pyramids, and desert pyramids.

Adult horses can wear copper horse armor; baby horses, donkeys, mules, skeleton horses and zombie horses cannot be equipped with armor. When equipped, copper horse armor gives 4 () armor points.
