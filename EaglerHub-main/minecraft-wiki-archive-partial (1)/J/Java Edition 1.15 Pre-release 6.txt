1.15 Pre-release 6 (known as 1.15-pre6 in the launcher) is the sixth pre-release for Java Edition 1.15, released on December 6, 2019, which fixes some bugs.

4 issues fixed

From the 1.15 development versions

MC-163270 – Sign in sign editing GUI is too dark.
MC-163979 – Entities (including items) get slowed down by soul sand even when not sunken into the block.
MC-166756 – Plains village tree leaves are much bigger than normal.
