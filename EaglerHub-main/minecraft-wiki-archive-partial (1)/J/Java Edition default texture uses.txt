Block and item particles (breaking, broken, sprinting on, falling on, iron golem walking on, eating, ...)
For version 13w02a to 13w17a, the default position coincides with the missing texture. Only cases where the missing texture is used due to it being in the default position (verifiable through use of a texture pack with a block texture larger than 64x64) are listed here; cases where the missing texture was used due to a texture actually being missing are listed here.
