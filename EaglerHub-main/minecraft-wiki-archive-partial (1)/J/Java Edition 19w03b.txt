19w03b is the fifteenth snapshot for Java Edition 1.14, released on January 17, 2019, which modifies composters.

Contents

1 Changes
2 Fixes
3 Videos
4 Trivia
5 References
6 Navigation

Changes
Composters

Red and brown mushroom blocks can now be used in a composter.
Can now be moved by pistons.
Fixes
15 issues fixed
From released versions before 1.14

MC-113869 – Shulker boxes restore custom name after deleting it and placing them down
From the 1.14 development versions

MC-140950 – Armor on player or entities is misaligned.
MC-140969 – Villager/zombie villager clothes remain visible when villager is invisible.
MC-140974 – Z-fighting on invisible villager's clothes and end of sleeve, as well as bottom of main body and legs
MC-140991 – Breaking an observer with a bell attached does not break the bell.
MC-141101 – Bell turns into double_wall state when updated with a noteblock.
MC-142114 – Joining the world outside of the spawn chunks results in client side desync.
From the previous development version

MC-142368 – Arrows, trident and shulker bullets get stuck in air.
MC-142376 – Mobs do not move.
MC-142394 – Some entities don't show fire particles when they [are] burning.
MC-142401 – Some entities have an incorrect hitbox.
MC-142430 – Red and brown mushroom blocks cannot be used in the composter.
MC-142465 – Composters cannot be pushed via pistons.
MC-142487 – When a puffer fish shrink[s] down, it will teleport itself to the side.
MC-142520 – Mob physics break upon loading a world in 19w03a.
