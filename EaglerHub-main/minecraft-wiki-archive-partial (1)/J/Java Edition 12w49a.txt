12w49a is the first snapshot for Java Edition 1.4.6, released on December 7, 2012, which added nether brick slabs, enchanted books and fireworks.

Additions
Blocks
 Nether brick slabs

New kind of slab.
Made by using 3 nether bricks blocks in a horizontal line.
Came from a suggestion at Minecon 2012.
Items
 Firework rocket

New kind of shootable projectible.
Sound effects will come soon.
Fireworks are customizable by crafting.
 Firework star

Used for crafting firework rockets.
 Enchanted books

Used for enchanting items.
In this snapshot due to a bug, any item could be given any enchantment in Survival mode using an anvil.
Changes
Blocks
General

When holding sneak, the player can place blocks and items (like redstone, repeaters, and levers) directly onto blocks such as crafting tables, furnaces, and dispensers.
Cobwebs

Can be harvested utilizing Silk Touch shears.
Items
Shears

Can now have the Silk Touch enchantment by using the anvil.
Mobs
Ghasts

The blast radius of fireballs a specific ghast shoots can be customized with an editor.
Removals
General
Distance effects

Falling block behavior has changed - they are no longer capable of infinitely producing dropped items at high coordinate values.
Fixes
16 issues fixed
From released versions before 1.4.6

MC-96 – using the number keys to move an item out of a furnace does not give XP.
MC-256 – Mobs can still revert farmland to dirt even if mobGriefing gamerule is set on false.
MC-498 – Setting an enderman on fire with flint and steel, or lava, will not make it teleport away.
MC-721 – Animals on fire do not flee.
MC-1140 – Boats always get placed in the same direction and not based on how they get placed.
MC-1240 – Endermen cannot dodge flame arrow. When the arrow is shot the Enderman moves out of the way but is still set on fire.
MC-1311 – When entering the Nether, the game auto-selects the first item on the toolbar.
MC-1847 – Leaves have the incorrect tool tip.
MC-2145 – "Drowning" in Creative mode.
MC-2504 – Enchant command cannot enchant with ID 0 (Protection).
MC-3332 – The XP level-up sound, which appears every 5 levels, does not play.
MC-3490 – Minecarts and falling entities flicker.
MC-3501 – Passive mob spawn eggs are not consumed upon use.
MC-3565 – Endermen do not open their mouth when attacked by other mobs.
MC-4069 – Pressing F3 + B displays hitboxes rendering invisibility potions useless.
MC-4117 – The wither can sometimes destroy its own star due to the posthumous detonation of wither skulls.
