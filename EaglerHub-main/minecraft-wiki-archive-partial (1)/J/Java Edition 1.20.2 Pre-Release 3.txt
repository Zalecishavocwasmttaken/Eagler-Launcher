1.20.2 Pre-Release 3 (known as 1.20.2-pre3 in the launcher) is the third pre-release for Java Edition 1.20.2, released on September 12, 2023, which fixes bugs.

MC-264564 – Record attribute is stripped from records with no components.
MC-265335 – Hoglins can't be bred.

MC-265296 – Player sits lower in minecarts.
