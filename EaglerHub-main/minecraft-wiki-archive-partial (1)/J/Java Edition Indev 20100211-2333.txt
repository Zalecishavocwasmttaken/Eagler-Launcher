A version of Minecraft Indev was released on February 11, 2010, at 23:33 UTC. It was announced, bundled with the previous version, on The Word of Notch at 23:37 UTC.
This version attempted to fix the game crashing, but this fix is unsuccessful.
This version therefore has no changes at all.
