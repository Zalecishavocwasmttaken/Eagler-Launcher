Not to be confused with Classic 0.0.14a.



This page documents unreleased versions. 
While there is proof of these versions' existence, they were never released to the public.

Several unreleased development versions of the game labeled 0.0.14a were developed from May 23 to May 25, 2009. They show in-development features later included in 0.0.14a and later releases.

New blocks (May 23, 2009)
Word of Notch post,Rotab's IRC logs, May 25, 2009

General
Added clouds.
New textures for cobblestone and planks.
Blocks
Added:
Sand and gravel.
They fall to the ground when placed.
Coal, iron and gold ore.
Logs and leaves.
Terrain
New terrain generator.
Added trees, beaches of sand and gravel and naturally spawning ore veins.
Water flow test (May 24, 2009)
Word of Notch post, YouTube video (reupload), Rotab's IRC logs, May 26, 2009

General
Added fog when changing render distance, replacing the old empty chunk system.
Online saving temporarily disabled.
Grass side texture has been changed and is now one block shorter.
Levels
The player can now select world sizes: "Small", "Normal" and "Huge".
Blocks
Water
Now flows faster than before.
Now flows from world border.
The player can now select and build with water.
Was disabled in the final release.
Offset test (May 25, 2009)
Word of Notch post, YouTube video (reupload)

General
Online level saving re-enabled.
Reverted the grass side texture back to the original.
Blocks
Vertex and color offset tests.
Vertex offsets cause "wavy" blocks, making the world look more realistic.
Color offsets cause every block to be a slightly different color.
Was later removed from release as Notch didn't like the idea.
Trivia
Respawning when pressing R worked just like in 0.0.13a_03 and prior, as this was not changed until the public release of 0.0.14a.
The mouse features (auto breaking/building when  Left Mouse Button is held down,  Scroll Wheel block picking support and pick-block with  Middle Mouse Button) were all added on May 27, 2009 for the full release of 0.0.14a.
