Armadillo

Badlands and savanna biomes.

1–3XP (when killed by a player or tamed wolf)

Rolls up if it's hurt, confronted by an undead mob, or players who are sprinting, mounted, or on a vehicle.
Drops an armadillo scute every 5 to 10 minutes.
Can also be brushed to drop one armadillo scute, using brush's durability.
Can be bred with spider eyes.

Changed

Name
Image
Changed

Spiders & cave spiders

Now run away from armadillos that are not rolled up.

Wolves

Received eight new variants, specific to the biome they are spawned in.
Now spawns in sparse jungle, savanna plateau, and wooded badlands.
Tamed wolves now have 40HP × 20 instead of 20HP × 10, and no longer takes half damage from most environmental sources.

Shulkers

Can no longer destroy armor stands.
