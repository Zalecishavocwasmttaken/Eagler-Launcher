13w25a is the fifteenth snapshot for Java Edition 1.6.1, released on June 17, 2013.

Changes
Mobs
General

Mobs named "Dinnerbone" or "Grumm" are now rendered upside down.
Items
Golden apples

Absorption time has been increased from 1 minute and 30 seconds to 2 minutes.

MC-15145 – Receiving very high amount of damage at once creates ghost mode in multiplayer.

MC-16003 – Possible to create a scoreboard objective without a name, but unable to use and/or delete it.
