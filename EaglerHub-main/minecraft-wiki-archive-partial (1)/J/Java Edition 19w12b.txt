19w12b is the twenty-eighth snapshot for Java Edition 1.14, released on March 21, 2019, which fixes some bugs.

Changes
Blocks
Glass

Mobs no longer suffocate in glass.
Mobs
Iron golems

Spawn chance has been slightly increased:
Now requires 5 villagers to meet instead of 10.
Villagers that are recently hurt by zombies have a higher chance of spawning an iron golem.
Villagers

No longer look at players in Spectator mode.
Fixes
22 issues fixed
From released versions before 1.14

MC-68687 – Entering or breaking Minecart briefly plays riding sound.
MC-70057 – Can't place redstone on sea lantern.
MC-117619 – The player's hitbox does not match his model when he sneaks.
MC-129750 – Non [a-z0-9_.-] character in one advancement causes ALL advancements to not load with no error message.
MC-135376 – being in water at y 0 makes screen too dark.
MC-136218 – Syntax help is offset to the right.
MC-136327 – Drowned never spawn in river biomes.
From the 1.14 development versions

MC-137784 – Vindicators sometimes get stuck in their attack animation.
MC-138179 – Crashes when enabling fullscreen (snapshot).

MC-140469 – Paths drop down when generating.
MC-141155 – Structure block integrity solely dependent on structure location, integrity seed is ignored.
MC-141484 – Entities stay on the global list even when the chunk unloaded.
MC-142703 – Joining world outside of spawn chunks whilst riding entity results in desync
MC-145731 – Turning in boats causes extremely annoying frame issue.
MC-145864 – Villagers see players in spectator mode.
MC-146166 – Internal Java error after opening barrel (ticking error).
MC-146218 – Stuttering screen when spectating someone.
