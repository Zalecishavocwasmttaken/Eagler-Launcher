The naturalist is a human scholar in the world of Minecraft and the in-universe writer of the Minecraft: Mobestiary. They have supposedly made it their life’s work to study Minecraft’s mobs and write their findings into an encyclopedia about all the creatures that have been discovered.

“As I sit here in my warm study, surrounded by my treasured books, my mind turns to the worlds beyond my windows.
