The Navy Cape (Japanese: ネイビーマント, Hepburn: Neibī Manto?) is a cape worn by Navy. It can be created using the Imagination Mod Power in Minecraft: The Manga.

Appearance
The Navy Cape is a plain cape, with a creeper face design in its center.

Functionality
When in the air, the Navy Cape allows its wearer some level of streering control. Navy used this alongside the Navy Cannon.

Crafting recipe

IngredientsCrafting recipe

Any Pressure Plate + String

History
When Nico and Navy prepared to fire the Navy Cannon on the Colossal Zombie, Navy suggested Nico craft the Navy Cape, and he agreed. He crafted it and placed it on Navy's back before launching him in the cannon, allowing Navy to use the cape to steer in midair and hit their target.
