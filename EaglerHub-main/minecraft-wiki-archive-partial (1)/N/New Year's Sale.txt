Soundscapes+, the free add-on that was included in the marketplace with the New Years Sale.
The New Years sale is a large Marketplace sale in Bedrock Edition that occurs at the end of every year. When the sale is active, players can enjoy up to 75% discount in the marketplace, meaning they can enjoy cheaper worlds, Add-ons, and more. They will need to spend less real money to buy Minecoins to purchase things in the marketplace.
