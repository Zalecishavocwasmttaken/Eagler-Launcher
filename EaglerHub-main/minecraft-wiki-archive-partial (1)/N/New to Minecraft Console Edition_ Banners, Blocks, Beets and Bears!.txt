Summary
During snowfall, Alex and the trapper stay inside an igloo, and Steve joins them, freezing a river with their boots along the way. Meanwhile outside a castle, a knight attempts to create the ideal banner, which the other dislikes. In a savanna village, Steve harvests beetroots from a farm and crafts a beetroot soup out of it inside their house. Back to the castle, the banner knight makes three failed attempts at making a good banner. Then, Steve travels across a cave where three players excavate a fossil, and goes through a Nether portal they decorated with red Nether bricks. In the Nether, the portal has a larger decoration with Nether wart blocks and a replica of a fossil. Outside the castle, the banner knight manages to create the perfect banner, hanging many of them on the walls.
