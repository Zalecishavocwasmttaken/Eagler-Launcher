Natural generation
Nether wart blocks and generate as part of huge crimson fungi in crimson forests.

Crafting recipe
Notes

Nether Wart

The Nether wart block cannot be crafted back into Nether wart.

Post generation
Main article: Huge crimson fungus § Growth
Using bone meal on a crimson fungus that is planted on crimson nylium has a 40% chance to grow into its huge equivalent, generating Nether wart blocks as part of it.

Usage
Nether wart blocks are not a compacted block as they cannot be crafted back into Nether wart.

Composting
Nether wart blocks can be fed into a composter with an 85% chance of leveling up the pile. Composting a Nether wart block instead of 9 Nether wart items is less efficient.

Mob spawning
Piglins, zombified piglins, and hoglins do not spawn on top of Nether wart blocks. Other Nether mobs such as skeletons and endermen are not prevented from spawning.
