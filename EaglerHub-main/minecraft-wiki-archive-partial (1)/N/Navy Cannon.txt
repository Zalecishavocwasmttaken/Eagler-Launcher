Navy Cannon (Japanese: ネイビーキャノン, Hepburn: Neibī Kyanon?) is a cannon designed to launch Navy. It can be created using the Imagination Mod Power in Minecraft: The Manga.

Appearance
The Navy Cannon is a short strip of flowing water with a dispenser. Navy sits at the end of the water stream, held in place by iron bars.

Functionality
The Navy Cannon has the ability to launch Navy for long-range explosions. It is functionally similar to a TNT cannon.

Crafting recipe

IngredientsCrafting recipe

TNT + Redstone Dust + Any Button+ Hopper + Dispenser + Stone + Navy

History
Wanting to participate in Skoll Village's potato festival, Nico created the Navy Cannon, thinking it would impress the judge. He aimed it at a nearby mountain and fired, but missed, launching Navy off into the distance.
In the air, Navy saw the Colossal Zombie approaching the village, and rushed back. The adventurers tried to defeat it, but found themselves outmatched by its size; however, Nico thought of using the Navy Cannon again, and he and Navy returned to the village. Loading the cannon, Nico fired on the zombie, allowing Navy to hit it with maximum power and kill it.
