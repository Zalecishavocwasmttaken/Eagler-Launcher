Nintendo Co., Ltd. is a Japanese multinational video game company headquartered in Kyoto. Nintendo is responsible for the creation and development of many video game franchises such as the Super Mario and The Legend of Zelda franchises.

In the Minecraft franchise
The Super Mario Mash-Up Pack is exclusively available for Wii U,  Nintendo Switch, and New Nintendo 3DS editions, as well as for Bedrock Edition on Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2 ​[upcoming]
Minecraft was released for the following consoles made by Nintendo: Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2,[upcoming] Nintendo 3DS, and Wii U.
Minecraft: Story Mode, Minecraft Dungeons, and Minecraft Legends were also released for Nintendo consoles, with Story Mode - Season Two, Dungeons, and Legends being playable on Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2, and the first season of Story Mode being playable on the Wii U, Nintendo Switch, and Nintendo Switch 2.
External references
The Challenger Pack 7 DLC for Super Smash Bros. Ultimate, a game published by Nintendo, adds Steve,  Alex, Zombie and Enderman as playable fighters, as well as several skins from the Legacy Skin Pack.
History
On September 23rd 1889, Fusajiro Yamauchi created a company called Nintendo to make Hanafuda cards. Later in 1977, Hiroshi Yamautchi turned Nintendo into a video game company.

On December 17th 2015, Nintendo teamed up with Mojang and 4J Studios to create the Wii U Edition of Minecraft.
On May 18, 2016, Wii U Edition Patch 5 released, which introduced the Super Mario Mash-Up Pack.
On May 12th 2017, Nintendo, Mojang and 4J Studios created the Nintendo Switch Edition.
On August 22nd 2017, Minecraft: Story Mode - Season One released on Nintendo Switch.
On September 13th 2017, Nintendo, Mojang and Other Ocean Interactive created the New Nintendo 3DS Edition of Minecraft, 2 months after the discontinuation of the New Nintendo 3DS.
On June 21st 2018, Nintendo Switch Edition was replaced with Bedrock Edition.
On November 6th 2018, Minecraft: Story Mode - Season Two released on Nintendo Switch.
On June 9th 2019, Minecraft Dungeons was announced to be released on Nintendo Switch.
On May 26th 2020, Minecraft Dungeons released on Nintendo Switch.
On October 13th 2020, Nintendo introduced the Challenge Pack 7 DLC to Super Smash Bros. Ultimate, which added Steve,  Alex, Zombie and Enderman as playable fighters, as well as several skins from the Legacy Skin Pack.
On June 12th 2022, Minecraft Legends was announced and was stated to be released on Nintendo Switch.
On April 18th 2023 Minecraft Legends released on Nintendo Switch.
On March 21st 2026, Minecraft Dungeons II ​[upcoming] was announced and was stated to be released on Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2.
On June 9th 2026, a native Nintendo Switch 2 version of Minecraft was announced.
On August 5th 2026, the Nintendo Switch 2 version of Minecraft was scheduled to release on October 27th 2026.
On August 7th 2026, Mojang Studios started sending selected players a notification that would give selected players access to a closed beta of Minecraft for Nintendo Switch 2
On September 29th 2026, Minecraft Dungeons II ​[upcoming] is scheduled to be released on Nintendo Switch and Nintendo Switch 2.
On October 27th 2026, Minecraft is scheduled to be released on Nintendo Switch 2 ​[upcoming].
