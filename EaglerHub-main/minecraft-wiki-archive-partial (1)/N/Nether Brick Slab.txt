A Nether brick slab is a decorative slab variant of Nether bricks that does not generate naturally and is used for building.

Usage

The different variant block states for the stone_slab and double_stone_slab IDs have been split up and merged into their own IDs.

Prior to The Flattening, these blocks' numeral IDs were 43 and 44.Pocket Edition Alphav0.12.1build 1
Nether brick slabs have the stone_slab ID, however their item form's ID is incorrectly registered as double_stone_slab.

The direct item form of double Nether brick slabs have the real_double_stone_slab ID.Bedrock Edition1.19.0Preview 1.19.0.27
