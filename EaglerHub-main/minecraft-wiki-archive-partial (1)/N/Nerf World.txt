Summary
Hub
The Hub is the main center. In the center of the hub, is a golden statue known as the "First Dart." The doors upon entrance to your left are the entrance to the practice range. The doors on the right lead into the blaster shop while the ones straight ahead lead into the arenas.

Practice Range
The practice range opens up to a bunch of moving targets and in front of them lies a nerf chicken microshot. On the left, lies the entrance to the obstacle course. The obstacle course consists of falling darts, swinging darts, targets, and platforms above foam pits.

Blaster Shop
In the blaster shop lies many blasters that you buy with Nerf bucks from winning levels. On the left wall, lies the microshots. On the right wall, lies blasters designed like weapons from Minecraft Dungeons. On the main wall, there are Dart packs and other the Ender Dragon blaster.
