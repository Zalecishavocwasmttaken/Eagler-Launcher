At the start of the book, Owari has the title "Taskmaster", which means she manages the Enderdome. In this role, she has little toleration for negligence.
At the meeting on the end island, she explains of the danger that will happen when humans complete the End portal. Later, after getting in an argument over the creation of the End, she is pacified with a potion of Weakness to make her embrace order.

Relationships
Kan
Kan dislikes Taskmaster Owari due to her abrasive manner in the Enderdome. Kan thinks the Enderdome is dangerous and violent, and that Taskmaster Owari is annoying. Owari returns the favor by dismissively telling Kan to get stronger every time he complains.
