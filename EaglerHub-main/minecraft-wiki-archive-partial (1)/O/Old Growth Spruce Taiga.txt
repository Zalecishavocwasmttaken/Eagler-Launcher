The old growth spruce taiga or redwood taiga mutated is a forested biome covered with tall 2×2 spruce trees that grow leaves all around the trunk. The ground is made of podzol and coarse dirt and covered by mossy cobblestone rocks.

Generation
Old growth spruce taigas generate at moderately low temperature, very high humidity and negative weirdness. They commonly border regular taigas and dark forests. They are often separated by rivers from old growth pine taigas, a variant with mega pine trees that only have some leaves at the top.
Old growth spruce taigas can generate in any type of terrain, near swamps and windswept forests, or near mountains with grove slopes and nearby pale gardens. Due to their very high humidity values, old growth spruce taigas are almost guaranteed to have lush caves generate beneath them.

Description
As the name implies, old growth spruce taigas include trees that have attained a great age, and therefore great height. These spruces grow all over the landscape, while most of them are tall with 2×2 trunks, regular pine and spruce trees can also generate.
Much of the grass blocks are replaced by large swaths of podzol and coarse dirt. Additionally, rocks made of mossy cobblestone frequently generate on the ground and dead bushes generate alongside the sweet berry bushes, ferns, and large ferns. Mushrooms are more common here than in most Overworld biomes.
Trail ruins may generate here, but villages and pillager outposts cannot. Chestnut wolves, foxes and rabbits‌[JE  only] may spawn here. The climate is cold and allows snowfall above layer Y=160±8, which is notably lower than in the old growth pine taiga.
Abandoned camps sometimes generate in old growth spruce taigas.​[upcoming: Third Drop 2026]
With Vibrant Visuals, old growth spruce taigas use a coolish humid volumetric fog setting, which slightly fades the distance in an orange tint. Old growth spruce taigas have coolish color grading which tints the environment with a colder blue color, and cold sunlight which appears slightly brighter at dusk and dawn.
