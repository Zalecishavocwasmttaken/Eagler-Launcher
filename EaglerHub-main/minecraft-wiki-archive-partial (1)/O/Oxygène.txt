Track description
Previews of tracks in Minecraft have been shortened to 30 seconds on this wiki in accordance with fair use rationale. "11" and "Magnetic Circuit" are exempt from this.

"Oxygène" is composed of a very short ambient piece consisting of distant, windy, chiptune-style synths and an echoed piano playing a mysterious musical flourish.
The album version of "Oxygène" continues into "Équinoxe".

All music files are in Ogg Vorbis.

Trivia
The name "Oxygène" is a reference to the song of the same name by Jean-Michel Jarre.
The first few notes of Minecraft can be heard at the beginning of the song.
