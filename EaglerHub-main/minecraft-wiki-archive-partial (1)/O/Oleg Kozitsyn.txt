Oleg Kozitsyn is a Technical Lead of the UI team at Mojang Studios.
He leads a team of engineers in C++ to search for a new interface structure for Minecraft. In 2017, he was a Senior Developer and was working on the Bedrock Edition of Minecraft since the version 1.2.9, for integration of the interface layout mechanism in Minecraft.
