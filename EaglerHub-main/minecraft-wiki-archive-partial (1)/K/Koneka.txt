Koneka was a character in the novel Minecraft: The End. She was an enderfrag, or a young enderman.

Koneka is generally cautious and reserved. On multiple occasions, she goes out of her way to question deviations from the status quo.

Biography

During training at the Enderdome, Koneka teleported to Mo, Kan, and Fin to ask the latter why he was not training in the Enderdome.
Later, Commander Kraj gave Koneka an iron sword with the Bane of Arthropods enchantment as part of training for the imminent war. She declines at first, noting that endermen traditionally use their fists in combat. She continues hesitating when asked to use the sword on her training partner Nif, resulting in her loss of the sword.
Koneka was killed by the Ender Dragon's breath while fighting Koal and Jess.
