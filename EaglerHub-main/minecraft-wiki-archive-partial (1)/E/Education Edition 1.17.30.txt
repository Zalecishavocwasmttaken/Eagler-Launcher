Bedrock Edition


1.17.30, the first release of the GOAT Update, is a major update to Education Edition, released on November 2, 2021. This update brought the Nether Update and Caves & Cliffs: Part I features to Education Edition, as well as Flipgrid and Microsoft Teams integrations.

Changes
General
Python

Content creators can add pre-made libraries of codes to lessons and if enabled, students can save their own custom code libraries for reusing code across a whole project.
Flipgrid and Microsoft Teams

Added Flipgrid and Microsoft Teams integration to Education Edition.
Library

Renamed folders for Starter Worlds, Build Plates, History & Culture, Equity & Inclusion, Literacy & Language.
Added a new folder called “Climate & Sustainability”.
Multiplayer

Now supports up to 40-players multi-player.
