Old colors



This section describes content that has been removed and was only present in earlier versions of Minecraft. 
Potion colors were changed shortly before the Trails & Tales update on Java Edition and Bedrock Edition. Legacy Console Edition and New Nintendo 3DS Edition still use old potion colors alongside older versions of Java Edition and Bedrock Edition.
