The Endermonster is an entity appearing in the Minecraft: Stonesword Saga series, with a major role in Crack in the Code! The Endermonster is one of the six splinters, representing the Evoker King's emotion of fear.

Appearance
The Endermonster resembles an Enderman. The Endermonster's eyes are a bright shade of red, as opposed to the purple of standard Enderman eyes.

Personality
The Endermonster represents the Evoker King's emotion of fear. The Endermonster is very shy, attacking anyone who looks into their eyes. The Endermonster never leaves their life of solitude in the forest, always attacking anyone nearby. The Endermonster accepts the obligation to be brave after a discussion with Theo.

Biography
Theo Grayson altered the code of the Evoker King, leading to the creation of the Endermonster as well as the other Splinters.
The Block Headz first find the Endermonster in a forest, where the group is attacked. After the encounter, Morgan Mercado begins referring to the entity as the "Endermonster."
One night, when Theo is tracking the Endermonster, he is attacked by the entity. Ash Kapoor defeats the Endermonster with water. The next day, the Block Headz use pumpkins to encounter the Endermonster. Theo talks with the Endermonster alone, prompting them to reveal their backstory. After Theo encourages the Endermonster to be brave, the latter transforms into one of the Evoker King's legs.
