[Short] life: Increments each tick when the projectile is not moving; resets to 0 if it moves. When it ticks to 1200, the projectile despawns.

[Byte] PierceLevel: The amount of remaining times this projectile can pierce through an entity.
[Byte] shake: Ticks until the projectile can be picked up by players. This is set to 7 when the projectile initially hits a block.
