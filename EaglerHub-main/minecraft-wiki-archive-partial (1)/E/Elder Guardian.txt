An elder guardian is a large hostile mob and a variant of the guardian. It is the largest and strongest aquatic mob. It attacks the same way as a normal guardian, and it also applies Mining Fatigue to players in a large radius around itself. They are only found in ocean monuments; three of them generate in each one.

Spawning
Three elder guardians spawn naturally during the generation of each ocean monument:

One in the top room of the monument.
One in each of the two wings of the monument.
They are spawned with the ocean monument and thus they do not respawn.

Behavior
Elder guardians do not swim around as much as normal guardians, and they do not swim away when approached by a player they are targeting, unlike regular guardians. Like normal guardians, elder guardians attempt to attack the player, squid, glow squid, and axolotls.
The elder guardian's eye follows and stares at any nearby players and always looks directly at its target. The eye still follows a player under the effects of Invisibility or in Spectator mode, though the elder guardian does not attack.
Elder guardians are considered aquatic mobs, and thus are affected by the Impaling enchantment.‌[Java Edition  only]​[until: JE Combat Tests]
The elder guardian has three methods of attacking, including firing its laser, inflicting Mining Fatigue, and a defensive thorn-like attack.
The elder guardian pathfinds toward an ocean monument if it is not within one.
In Bedrock Edition, elder guardians always return to their spawn point, whether they spawned naturally or from a spawn egg.
An elder guardian attacks axolotls that are not playing dead. Otherwise, an elder guardian does not attack mobs that attack it, although the attacking mob takes damage from the elder guardian's natural spikes defense.
The elder guardian, like a normal guardian, does not take suffocation damage when out of the water for a prolonged period of time.

Elder guardians cannot ride boats.Cursing player in Java Edition.
Laser
The laser takes 3 seconds to charge, doing no damage and allowing the player to move away in the meantime. As it charges, it changes from purple to bright yellow. Once charged, the beam flashes green, abruptly disappears, and deals 8HP damage plus another 4HP magic damage on Normal difficulty. The magic damage is increased by another 2HP in Hard difficulty after its usual scaling factor of 1.5x, meaning an elder guardian can deal up to 8HP magic damage on Hard difficulty with a single beam attack. The elder guardian swims around for a few seconds before firing again. The beam cannot be dodged and has a maximum range of approximately 14 blocks. Once the player is out of range, if the beam is obstructed by solid blocks, or if the player has a shield, the guardian's beam disengages from the player and deals no damage.

Inflicting Mining Fatigue
In Java Edition, once every 60 seconds, an elder guardian afflicts Mining Fatigue III to every player in Survival Mode within a 50 block radius. Players on the same team as the guardian, and players that already have at least 60 seconds of mining fatigue III or higher, are unaffected. In Bedrock Edition, the elder guardian inflicts any unafflicted player within range immediately.

When afflicted, the player sees a ghostly image of the elder guardian and hears a bell-like sound. The effect decreases a player's attack speed by 30% and decreases mining speed even more for 5 minutes. The attack targets players through blocks, even underground, and a potion of Invisibility offers no defense. This is not considered an attack in regards to the Thorns enchantment.Cursing player in Bedrock Edition.
The image of the elder guardian is a particle effect that can be recreated by issuing the command /particle minecraft:elder_guardian.‌[Java Edition  only]

Spikes defense
An elder guardian deals 2HP (3HP on Hard mode) damage every time it is hit while its spikes are extended, similar to the Thorns enchantment on armor. If cornered by a player or axolotl, the elder guardian usually extends its spikes and fires at point-blank range.

Trivia
The elder guardian's status as a boss or simply a powerful mob has been historically ambiguous. While it lacks important characteristics of a boss, chiefly a health bar, it has been grouped with boss mobs and addressed as one by Mojang before. The elder guardian was also marketed as a boss in Pocket Edition, having been introduced in the Boss Update. Jeb later stated that it is intended as a miniboss opposed to a full boss which is why it does not have a health bar.
The elder guardian's eye is the "head" part of the mob's model, giving the illusion of a dynamic eye.
According to Jeb, elder guardians were introduced to encourage players to solve the temple maze rather than dig through it easily. He acknowledges there are ways around it, but players would still have to solve the challenge in a different way.
Also according to Jeb, the "jumpscare" by an elder guardian is unintentional; instead, he added it to provide a visual cue as to why players get slowed down by Mining Fatigue.
In Bedrock Edition, elder guardians make their underwater death sounds if killed on land.
The elder guardian drops a sponge to guarantee that a player can get at least three from an ocean monument without a sponge room.
Despite being classified as only a monster, the elder guardian was referred to as an "animal of the deep" in a Minecraft video.
