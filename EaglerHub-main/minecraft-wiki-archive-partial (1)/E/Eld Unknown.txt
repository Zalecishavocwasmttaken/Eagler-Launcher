Track description

Previews of tracks in Minecraft have been shortened to 30 seconds on this wiki in accordance with fair use rationale. "11" and "Magnetic Circuit" are exempt from this.

The song starts with a piano and bass, adding string chords as it goes, then takes on a calm mood, after which a synthesizer and strings carry the melody along with a sort of marimba, which it ends with.
