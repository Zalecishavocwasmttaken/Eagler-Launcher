Description
This biome generates as part of the outer islands of the End, forming the hill-tops in the center of each island. This is the only biome where chorus trees naturally generate. End cities also generate here.

History
Java Edition1.915w31a
Added infinitely generating outer islands, separated from the central island by a gulf of about 1000 blocks.1.1318w06a
The outer islands of the End biome are now divided up into four separate biomes: The End - Floating Islands, The End - Medium island, The End - High island, and The End - Barren island.18w19a
"The End - High island" has been renamed to "End Highlands."
