1.4.0 (Windows) or 1.4.1 (macOS) is a major update to Education Edition, released for Windows and macOS on June 12, 2018, and for iOS on September 6, 2018, which adds most of the Update Aquatic features equating to Update Aquatic (Phase 1) for Bedrock Edition 1.4.0. It is the first version to be released on iOS devices.
