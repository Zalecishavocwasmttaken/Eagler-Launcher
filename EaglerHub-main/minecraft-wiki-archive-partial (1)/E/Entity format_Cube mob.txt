[Int] Size: The size of the cube mob with 0 as the smallest, 1 as the second smallest, and so on. Values that are greater than 126 get clamped to 126. When summoning the mob or resetting its attributes, this value also determines the mob's attribute bases for max_health, movement_speed, and attack_damage (except sulfur cubes which have no attack damage).
[Byte] wasOnGround: 1 or 0 (true/false) - true if the mob is touching the ground. This is a an exact copy of the [Byte] onGround tag.
