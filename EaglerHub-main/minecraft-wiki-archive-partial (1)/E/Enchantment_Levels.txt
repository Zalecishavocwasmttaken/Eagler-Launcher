Please note that these are modified enchantment levels, not normal ones. This was taken directly from the data, do not modify this list. 
These are not the levels you need to pay for an enchantment. Note also that this is not just the base enchanting level provided by bookshelves; you must also take into account the modification of levels that occur in step one.

These are the base cost levels of the vanilla enchantments, used when calculating which enchantments to choose. In Java Edition, the minimum and maximum values are controlled by the min_cost and max_cost fields of the enchantment definition and can be modified with a data pack.
