Obtaining
It is automatically obtained and appears in the hotbar when the player starts up a world. It also appears in the hotbar after the player dies.

Usage
The Build item can be used to command certain living blocks to go to the targeted location and turn into normal blocks. It is the only way to build.

Issues
Issues relating to "Build (item)" are not maintained on the bug tracker because it is an April Fools' joke, and is therefore not in the newest stable version or snapshot. Issues reported there are closed as "Invalid".
