Dolphin

No longer spawns in cold oceans or deep cold oceans.

Drowned

Now has a 50% chance to spawn as a zombie nautilus jockey, if it doesn't spawn in rivers.
15% of baby drowned that spawn can now find a mob to mount, like baby zombies and baby husks.
Baby drowned can now drop items upon death.

Horse, donkey, mule, camel, llama, trader llama

No longer sinks in water while being ridden.

Husk

Can now spawn as a rider of a camel husk jockey.
Can now spawn with iron spears.
Can now pick up and use spears.

Piglin

Can now spawn with golden spears.
Can now pick up and use spears.
Now consider golden spears, and golden nautilus armor as one of the items they like.

Pufferfish

Can now spawn in lukewarm oceans and deep lukewarm oceans.

Zombie

Can now spawn as a rider of a zombie horseman.
Can now occasionally spawn with iron spears.
Can now pick up and use spears.

Zombie horse

Now spawns naturally with an adult zombie rider holding an iron spear.
Now counts towards the monsters mob cap.
Has an updated texture.
Now has 25HP × 12.5 health points (12.5 hearts) of health.
Spawns in darkness, and burns in sunlight like other undead mobs.
Drops 2-3 rotten flesh.
Can be healed and tempted using red mushrooms.
Can be tamed in the same fashion as regular horses, and can then be mounted and controlled by a player.
Tamed zombie horses are persistent and do not despawn.
Can be equipped with a saddle and horse armor.
Horse armor protects zombie horses from sunlight damage.
Has variable movement speed and jump strength, determined when they spawn.

Zombie villager

Baby zombie villagers can no longer mount mobs like baby zombies and baby husks.
Can now pick up and use spears.

Zombified piglin

Can now spawn with golden spears.
Can now pick up and use spears.
