1.17.30 (beta 1.17.20.20), ? Current

Changed

The terrain generation breakdown beyond X/Z ±2,812,333.

Areas that are normally high enough to generate jagged peaks or frozen peaks instead generate monolith-like structures that extend all the way to about Y=256. The exact effects on the terrain differ between 32-bit and 64-bit builds of the game.In Beta 1.18.0.20 or newer, the terrain breaks down due to inadvertently introduced alongside as a result of the terrain generation rewriting process. In an unkown version​[more information needed], the monolith generation was changed from sometimes having lava lakes stretching down to often meet an ancient city.
