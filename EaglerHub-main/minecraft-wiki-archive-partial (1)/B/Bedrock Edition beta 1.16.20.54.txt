Beta 1.16.20.54 is the fourth and final beta version for Bedrock Edition 1.16.20, released on July 22, 2020, which fixes bugs.

Additions
General
Added a prompt messages for older devices to notify the discontinuation of Gear VR, Windows 10 Mobile, Android devices with less than 768MB of RAM, iOS devices running iOS 10 or below, and video cards that only support DirectX 10.1 or below.
Changes
Mobs
Piglins and Piglin Brutes

The attack animation of unarmed piglins and piglin brutes has been changed.
Vindicators

The attack animation of unarmed vindicators has been changed.
Fixes
Performance and stability

Fixed a crash that could occur when playing an emote and suspending the game (MCPE-73235).
General

Tweaked arm animation for brutes, piglins, and vindicators that have their weapons removed (MCPE-83581).
Emotes can no longer be equipped before unlocking them (MCPE-84810).
Custom skins now work properly on multiplayer (MCPE-48207).
