Battle & Beasts 2 Skin Pack is a skin pack which, like the Battle & Beasts Skin Pack, contains 45 skins themed around wild animals and warriors of different nations. It is included in the Wii U and Nintendo Switch editions by default.

Baby Ghast

A juvenile ghast, with shrunken proportions, such as thin tentacles.

This was the first Minecraft skin with a completely transparent body and lower torso.
Similar baby ghasts appear in Minecraft Dungeons and 15 Year Journey, with different designs.
Predates the ghastling mob by eleven years.

Siamese Cat

A humanoid siamese cat.

Rabbit

A humanoid rabbit, noticeably more realistic than Minecraft rabbits.

Predates the rabbit mob by a year.
