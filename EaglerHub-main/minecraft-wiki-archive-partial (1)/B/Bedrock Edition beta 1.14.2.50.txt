Beta 1.14.2.50 is the first beta version for Bedrock Edition 1.14.20, released on December 18, 2019, which fixes bugs. This is the final beta released in 2019.

Contents

1 Fixes
2 Trivia
3 References
4 Navigation

Fixes
Crashes

Fixed several crashes that could occur during gameplay.
General

Classic skins in skin packs are now saved when reloading the game. (MCPE-55278)
Gameplay

Projectiles can now be shot through the gap between two honey blocks.
Tridents no longer bounce continuously on the sides of honey blocks. (MCPE-53626)
Players no longer bounce when being pushed and pulled by honey blocks and pistons. (MCPE-53815)
Mobs

Bees no longer fly in and out of a beehive or nest that is on fire. (MCPE-53872)
Map makers and add-on creators

Fixed a bug that was preventing players from being teleported and rotated smoothly.
The sensing component (used for ranged attacks) now calculates the view direction like it used to, making it so mobs can see their target on the y-axis.
Fixed an issue that could sometimes prevent behavior pack animations from working on Android. (MCPE-56117)
Fixed the command order for riding custom entities on a Marketplace map.
Trivia
This is the last Bedrock Edition beta to be released in 2019 as well as the 2010s decade.
Beta 1.14.2.50 was released on the latest calendar date out of all beta builds, on December 18.
