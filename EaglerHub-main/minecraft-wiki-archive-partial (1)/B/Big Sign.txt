A big sign is a three block wide version of a sign. It was obtainable in MinecraftEdu by placing a sign and selecting the Big sign option in the GUI.

2 History
3 Gallery
4 See also

Usage
The big sign functioned effectively the same as a regular sign, however the model was stretched to the width of three blocks. The big sign allowed for 50 chars to be included per line, due to the increased width.

History
MinecraftEdu?
Added big sign. Each line holds 50 characters.[verify]
