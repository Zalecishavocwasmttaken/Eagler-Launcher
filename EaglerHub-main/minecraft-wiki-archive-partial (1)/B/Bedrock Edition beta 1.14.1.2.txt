Beta 1.14.1.2 is the fourth beta version for Bedrock Edition 1.14.1, released on December 4, 2019, which fixes bugs.

Contents

1 Fixes
2 Trivia
3 References
4 Navigation

Fixes
Fixed several crashes that occurred during gameplay.
Increased performance when loading skins on featured servers.
Bees now die after attacking. (MCPE-53860)
Fixed empty hands being invisible in first-person perspective.
Fixed placeholder text appearing on the resource packs and behavior packs screens.
Trivia
This development version, the fourth for 1.14.1, was released before 1.14.0 had even been fully released.
