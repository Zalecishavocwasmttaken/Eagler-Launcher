Beta 26.30.32 (ChromeOS, Android) or Preview 26.30.32 (Windows, iOS, iPadOS, Xbox, PlayStation) is the seventh and final beta/Preview for Bedrock Edition 26.30, released on May 26, 2026 for most devices, and on May 29, 2026 for iOS, which fixes bugs.

Changes
Blocks
Potent sulfur

Erupting geysers can now propel boats and falling blocks.
Mobs
Sulfur cube

Sulfur cubes using the "slow bouncy" archetype are now correctly kicked back based on the damage dealt.
World generation
Sulfur caves

Now blend correctly with neighboring pre-generated chunks.
Technical
Entity Filters

The redstone_strength_at_position entity filter now correctly evaluates to 0 when no signal is present and to the redstone strength when a redstone wire is pointing at the evaluated position.
Features

The only_check_intersection_for_motion_blocking_blocks field in the block_intersection constraint of minecraft:structure_template_feature is now optional.
Fixes
8 issues fixed
From the 26.30 development versions

MCPE-238275 – Sulfur cubes in buckets preserve momentum and other unwanted data.
MCPE-238463 – Inaccurate translation for "Damage Tilt" setting (RU).
MCPE-238476 – Redstone dust pointing into centered sulfur cube with tnt does not quite ignite it.
MCPE-238953 – Hot sulfur cube archetype does not match the regular archetype.
MCPE-238957 – Old growth taigas no longer play music in Survival mode.
Other

Fixed a bug where sulfur cubes would sink or not be able to move after ejecting its block.
Fixed corrupted visuals on mobs when using Vibrant Visuals with PBR resource packs that include heightmap textures for actors.
Fixed a crash that could occur when a player takes too long to join local multiplayer on Xbox.
