Key art
Builders & Biomes is a free skin pack, that consists of "six skins and three variants" from the board game Minecraft: Builders & Biomes. A ZIP file containing the skins for Java Edition is available for download on Minecraft.net.

Skins

Name

Render

Description

Notes

Buff Butcher

A dark skinned builder with lavender hair, a navy shirt, boots with large silver buckles, and many tools.

Buff Butcher Alternate

A light skinned version of the regular Buff Butcher.

Barn Builder

A tan skinned person wearing a green shirt, blue overalls, and black gloves. Their hair is black, with a pencil on their left ear.

Homestead Healer

A noticeably older or grumpier figure, with a lower than normal face, large forehead, and long rose white hair dropping over a blue and gold robe.

Bee-friender

A person wearing armor themed around bees.

Bee-friender Alternate

A dark skinned version of the above.

Ranch Ranger

A dark skinned person wearing a yellow headband, and a yellow and green uniform.

Pig Whisperer

A light skinned person wearing a mask of a pig, on top of a red tunic holding a carrot.

Pig Whisperer Alternate

A dark skinned version of the above.
