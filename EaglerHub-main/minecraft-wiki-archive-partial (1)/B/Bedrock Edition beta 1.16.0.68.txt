Beta 1.16.0.68 is the eighteenth and final beta version for Bedrock Edition 1.16.0, released on June 17, 2020, which fixes more bugs with account sign-in and multiplayer.

Contents

Fixes
Fixed several issues with account sign in and multiplayer.
Trivia
This beta and Bedrock Edition Preview 1.19.0.68 currently have the highest minor version numbers.
This beta is also the farthest development version a version has received in Bedrock Edition.
