Obtaining
Farming
Beetroots are obtained from harvesting a fully-grown beetroot crop, which drops 1 beetroot in Java Edition or 1 to 2 beetroots in Bedrock Edition and 1 to 4 beetroot seeds (2 5⁄7 per crop harvested on average). If a crop is harvested before it is fully grown, it just drops one seed. Harvesting with a Fortune-enchanted tool increases the number of seeds dropped but does not increase the yield of beetroots.

Usage
Food
See also: Hunger management

A beetroot can be eaten to restore 1 () hunger and 1.2 hunger saturation. Six beetroots can be crafted into beetroot soup, which restores six times the hunger and saturation of a single beetroot.

Breeding
Beetroots can be used to breed and attract pigs.
Villagers can pick up beetroot items to become willing, allowing them to breed. Villagers need 12 beetroots before they can breed.

Composting
Placing a beetroot into a composter has a 65% chance of raising the compost level by 1.

Trivia
Jeb has stated that beetroots were originally added to Pocket Edition as a developer on the team "thought it would be a nice thing to have." When they were added, though, they served as a more reliable source of rose red dye. Rose red's only other source, red mushrooms, generated uncommonly in the Old worlds of the time or could be spawned, with tedium, using a Nether reactor.
