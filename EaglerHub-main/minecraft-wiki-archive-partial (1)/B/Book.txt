A book is an item that can be used as a crafting material or to store enchantments in a form that can be applied to any valid item using an anvil.

Crafting

Ingredients
Crafting recipe

Paper +Leather

Grindstones
Disenchanting an enchanted book at a grindstone yields a normal book and a small amount of experience (unless the enchanted book has Curse of Binding or Curse of Vanishing on it).

Villager gifts
In Java Edition, librarian villagers throw books at players under the Hero of the Village effect.
