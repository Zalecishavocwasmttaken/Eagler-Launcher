A butcher is a villager that trades meats and some other food items. Butchers wear a red headband and have a white apron.

Spawning
An unemployed villager turns into a butcher when an unclaimed smoker is nearby.

Natural generation
The workstation of butchers, the smoker, generates in butcher shops in villages.

Drops
In Java Edition, a butcher can drop various items by throwing them as a gift towards a nearby player with the Hero of the Village effect. The gift is randomly selected from the following list:
