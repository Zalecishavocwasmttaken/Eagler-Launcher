26.32 is a hotfix to Bedrock Edition released on June 25, 2026, for most devices, and on June 27, 2026 for Nintendo Switch, which fixes bugs.

Contents

1 Fixes
2 References
3 Navigation

Fixes
14 issues fixed
MCPE-140943 – Top of a candle on a cake in water is not displayed correctly
MCPE-163482 – Uncontrolled mobs can cut off leashes if a player will sit on them
MCPE-185209 – "Chiseled" used instead of "Chiselled" in the Tuff and Tuff brick blocks in EN (UK)
MCPE-223722 – Fire texture in third-person view appears as a pink and black rectangle in official mash-up pack worlds
MCPE-224609 – Player model in the Dressing Room is displayed incorrectly for a second when unequipping body features
MCPE-235303 – Golden Horse Armor is still using the old name "Gold Horse Armour" in English (UK) language
MCPE-238295 – "Player Waypoints" in the game settings displays the key name
MCPE-239565 – Can’t enter worlds that have multiplayer disabled using skins from some packs, or if the skin is not loaded
MCPE-239693 – FPS dropping after the new update
MCPE-239719 – When a resource pack contains subpacks, sounds that are not located within a subpack and have "stream" set to true will not play correctly
MCPE-239753 – Writing in a book and quill and closing it doesn't allow you to interact with anything in pre-26.30 worlds.
Other

Fixed custom blocks rendering a stray solid face that could bleed through textures when a model face was hidden by setting one of its UV size dimensions to zero.
Fixed several crashes that could occur during gameplay.
Players with multiplayer restricted skins can now enter worlds with multiplayer disabled.
