Beta 26.40.31 (ChromeOS, Android) or Preview 26.40.31 (Windows, iOS, iPadOS, Xbox, PlayStation) is the sixth and final beta/Preview for Bedrock Edition 26.40, released on July 12, 2026, for iOS and iPadOS, and on July 14, 2026, for all remaining devices, which fixes bugs.

Fixes
5 issues fixed
From released versions before 26.40

MCPE-239513 – Game launch time increases significantly when the skin_packs folder contains a large number of files.
MCPE-239742 – Vibrant Visuals is no longer supported on Samsung devices with One UI 8.5 or above installed.
From the 26.40 development versions

MCPE-239756 – Potion tipped arrows bounce off iron golems and ravagers after dealing damage instead of breaking.
Other

Fixed several crashes that could occur during gameplay.
Fixed a bug where WinHTTP would not honor a 3 second timeout when probing for NetherNet under certain conditions.
