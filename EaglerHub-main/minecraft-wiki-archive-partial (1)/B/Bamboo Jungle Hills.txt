The bamboo jungle hills biome is a hills variant of the bamboo jungle biome that no longer generates as of Caves & Cliffs: Part II, and was removed in Java Edition.

Description
Old
The bamboo jungle hills was similar to the bamboo jungle, though with steeper terrain like the regular jungle hills. Large amounts of bamboo covered the landscape, and patches of podzol replaced most grass blocks. Naturally-generated jungle trees were always large variants, and pandas spawned here, like in the bamboo jungle. Jungle pyramids also generated here in Java Edition.

Current
In Bedrock Edition, bamboo jungle hills in older worlds still received updates. When generated through single biome or behavior packs, they can also generate new blocks.
They appear similar to the current bamboo jungle, spawning warm animal variants.
With Vibrant Visuals, bamboo jungle hills use a strong humid volumetric fog setting, which fades the distance in an orange tint. Bamboo jungle hills use warmish and color grading, which makes the biome look more humid and warm. The night sky is slightly brighter and moonlight colored more vibrant blue.

Mobs
In Java Edition, bamboo jungle hills used the same mob spawning chances as jungle hills for hostile and ambient categories, and had different chances for passive categories. In Bedrock Edition, bamboo jungle hills use the same mob spawning chances as bamboo jungles.
