Parrot

Jungle

Feather (1–2)

Passive creatures can be tamed using seeds.
Has the ability to fly and occasionally rest on the ground.
If there is music playing from a jukebox nearby, they will dance accordingly.
Will dance even if they are on the player's shoulders.
They will die after eating a cookie.

Changed

Name
Image
Changed

All mobs

Now avoids cactus, fire and lava.
Adjusted the color it flashes when attacked.
Increased stride stability when moving up stairs while riding certain creatures.

Mooshroom

Now only generates above mycelium.

Witch

A potion of fire resistance will be drunk when standing on top of a magma block.
There is now an 8.5% chance of dropping the potion it was taking when killed. Before this, they always dropped potions.

Skeleton, stray and zombie pigman

Now has a chance to spawn with enchanted tools and weapons.

Ender dragon

Corresponding particles will now appear when stopping using dragon breath attack.

Sheep

Shorn sheep no longer drop wool when killed.

Adult polar bear

Panic now appears when on fire.

Villager

Peasant villagers now sell 5-7 apples for 1 emerald.
Observe new offers from villagers more quickly after trading is unlocked.

Horse

Eating or drinking something while riding a horse no longer slows the player down.
Updated appearance.
No longer opens its mouth when dropping a player or taking damage.

Donkey

Updated appearance.
No longer opens its mouth when dropping a player or taking damage.

Mule

Updated appearance.

Skeleton horse

Can now be saddled.

Snow golem

Snow golem's face is now only visible when clipped.
Lingering water bottle now deals damage to it.

Ghast

Ghast fireball activates Nether portal when it hits it.

Blaze and enderman

Lingering water bottle now deals damage to it.

Cow

Players will no longer drink the milk directly after milking the cow.

Changed

Name
Image
Changed

Item frame

From items to entities. ​[more information needed]

Brewing stand

Blaze powder is now required to power brewing stands.

Beacon

Light beams will now change color when passing through stained glass or stained glass panes.
The color of the light beam will only use the color of the highest layer of stained glass and will not be mixed.

Ice

Is now transparent.

Stained glass and stained glass pane

Now have their own textures and can be obtained through normal means.
Changed the inventory icons of glass pane and stained glass pane.
Single glass pane or stained glass pane are now columnar.

Observer

Updated the texture of observer so that the observer will now flash red when outputting a redstone signal.
Now able to detect updates to more blocks.
Increased the strength of the redstone pulse output by the observer, and no longer has two pulses when the piston expands and retracts once.

Bricks, nether bricks and clay block

Renamed to better distinguish it from other items.

Iron bars

Individual iron railings are now columnar.
Changed the inventory icon of iron railings.

Bed

A message will appear when trying to sleep too far from bed.
A dyed bed can no longer be dyed with the same dye.
Text chat with controller and touch screen can now be opened while sleeping.

Trapdoor

Trapdoors will open directly when placed next to an active signal source.

Sandstone stairs

Sandstone stairs now have a cracked texture at the bottom.

Mycelium

Mycelium no longer turns to dirt when a giant mushroom is on top of it.

Fence

[verify]

Blocks can now be placed on top of fences with leads attached to them.

Shulker boxes

When crafting a shulker box, it outputs an undyed variant instead of purple, resulting in 17 colors.

Piston

Can now push arrows.

Diorite, andesite and granite

Can no longer be used to replace stone crafting recipes.

Monster spawner

Only 4 monsters can be spawned at a time now.

Emerald ore

Can no longer be mined with a stone pickaxe.

Sign

No longer flammable.

Hay bale

Reduced damage from falling on hay bales.

Changed

Name
Image
Changed

All items

Items can now pass upward through solid blocks, allowing for a 3×3 item lift.

Bucket

Only one iron barrel can now be placed in the furnace at a time.

Map

The player's head will appear on the map.
Locator maps now track the owner's location regardless of their dimension.
Empty maps can now be combined with a compass in an anvil to create a locator map.
Renaming maps and increasing map size in Anvil now only costs experience levels.
Maps and locator maps can now be obtained via /give.

Leash

Right click to release the leash.
There is now a sound when placing leashes.

Gameplay
Changed

Name
Changed

Creative mode

Pressing A in the creative mode inventory will place an item into inventory instead of a group. Pressing Y will drop the entire group; pressing X will clear the shortcut bar in creative mode.

Inventory

3 new buttons for classic UI: "Inventory only", "Inventory and recipe book" and "Recipe book only".
Add a switch to the recipe book to switch to full-screen creative mode inventory.

Spawn

Increased the spawn rate of friendly mobs, making them easier to spot.
