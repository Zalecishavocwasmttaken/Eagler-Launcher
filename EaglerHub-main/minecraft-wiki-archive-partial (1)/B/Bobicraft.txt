Rodrigo Narváez Gómez, better known by his username Bobicraft, is a Mexican YouTuber known for his Minecraft content.

In the Minecraft franchise
In 2021, Bobicraft made a debut on the pre-show of that year's Minecraft Live, showcasing some clips of his community events.
In 2023, he was one of the selected content creators by Xbox to try Minecraft Legends on their offices, becoming the first Latino American to play it for the first time.
In 2024, he inaugurated the pre-show of that year's Minecraft Live, appearing at the beginning to answer personal Minecraft-related questions.
In 2025, he attended the green carpet for A Minecraft Movie. He also voiced General Chungus in the Latin American Spanish dub of the film.
In the same year, he was one of the two hosts for Minecraft LIVE – March 2025's pre-show, alongside fellow YouTuber GeminiTay.
In 2026, Bobicraft was one of the selected content creators by Mojang to try a demo version of Minecraft Dungeons II during TwitchCon Europe 2026.
He was chosen as the new Minecraft ambassador for Minecraft Experience.
