Crafting recipes
Cocoa beans
See also: Dyes

In Pocket Edition v0.8.0 alpha build 3, cocoa beans were added with their own exclusive recipe. Since v0.9.0 build 1, cocoa beans were made able to be obtained with cocoa pods. In the v0.16.0 official release, cocoa beans' crafting recipe was removed.
The recipe was removed because cocoa beans could be obtained from cocoa pods. It was reported by a user in the issue tracker and was confirmed as an unintended feature.

Ingredients
Crafting recipe

Ink Sac +Red Dye +Yellow DyeorInk Sac +Orange Dye

Enchanted golden apple
See also: Enchanted Golden Apple

The enchanted golden apple recipe was removed in 1.2 beta 1.2.0.2.
However, enchanted golden apples may still be obtained in other ways.

Ingredients
Crafting recipe

Block of Gold +Apple

Bed
See also: Bed

The recipe of crafting the red bed with any wool color was removed in Pocket Edition 1.1.0 with the addition of multiple colored beds. This recipe was removed because prior to 1.1.0, only red beds existed in the game, and now it is required to have matching red wool to craft a red bed.

Ingredients
Crafting recipe

Any Wool +Any Planks

Old Stonecutter
See also: Stonecutter (old)

In v0.13.0 build 1, the crafting recipe for the old stonecutter was removed, but it is only available through add-ons.
In Bedrock Edition beta 1.10.0.3, the old stonecutter was not removed but functionally replaced with the new stonecutter.

Ingredients
Crafting recipe

Cobblestone

Nether reactor core
The nether reactor core's recipe was made no longer available and it no longer appears in the creative inventory. It will drop its ingredients when broken.

Ingredients
Crafting recipe

Iron Ingot +Diamond

Smooth stone slab
In older versions, the smooth stone slab (called Stone Slab then) could be crafted using any stone. However, the recipe changed to use stone only in 1.2.0, and in 1.9.0, the recipe changed again so it requires smooth stone.

Ingredients
Crafting recipe

Any Stone

Stone bricks
In older versions, stone bricks could be crafted using cobblestone, andesite, granite, diorite, and their polished variants. However, in 1.2.0, stone bricks were changed to be crafted with stone only.

Ingredients
Crafting recipe

Any Stone

Redstone repeater
In older versions, redstone repeaters could be crafted using cobblestone, andesite, granite, diorite, and their polished variants. However, in 1.2.0, the recipes changed to use only stone.

Crafting recipe

Redstone comparator
In older versions, redstone comparators could be crafted using cobblestone, andesite, granite, diorite, and their polished variants. However, in 1.2.0, the recipes changed to use only stone.

Copper Horn
Main article: Copper Horn
The Copper Horn was an item used to play sound, which had 10 variations; each of them could play 3 different sounds when looking up, crouching, or doing neither of those, respectively. In total, there were 30 different sounds that could be played using copper horns.
In beta 1.19.0.24, the Copper Horn was removed. Mojang in the Minecraft Feedback's Knowledge Base said "the Copper Horn was a fun experiment that didn't quite live up to our design goals, so we decided to remove it".

Ingredients
Crafting recipe

Copper Ingot +Goat Horn

String
