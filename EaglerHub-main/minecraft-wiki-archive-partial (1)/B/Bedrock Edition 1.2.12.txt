New Nintendo 3DS Edition


1.2.12 is a version of Bedrock Edition released only for Nintendo Switch on June 21, 2018. This was the first version of Bedrock Edition for Nintendo Switch, and is the version contained on the physical cartridge release. The internal build date of this version is April 6, 2018, over two months before its release.
The changes between this version and 1.2.11 are unknown.
