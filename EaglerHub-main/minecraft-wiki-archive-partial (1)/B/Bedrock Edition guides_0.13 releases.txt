Items
New

Name
Image
Obtaining
Uses

Rabbit hide

Adult rabbits will drop 0-1 rabbit hide when killed.

Can be used to craft leather.

Raw rabbit

Adult rabbits will drop 0-1 raw rabbit when killed.

Press and hold the Use key in the hotkey bar to eat raw rabbit. Doing so will restore 3 () and 1.8 hunger saturation.
Raw rabbit meat can be used to breed or heal tamed wolves, guide them to move around, and also reduce the growth timer of baby wolves by 10%.
Raw rabbit meat can be used to cook cooked rabbit meat.

Cooked rabbit

Adult rabbits will drop 0-1 cooked rabbit when killed while on fire.

Name
Ingredients
Smelting recipe

Cooked Rabbit

Raw Rabbit +Any fuel

0.35

Press and hold the Use key in the hotkey bar to eat cooked rabbit. Doing so will restore 5 () and 6 hunger saturation.
Cooked rabbit meat can be used to breed or heal tamed wolves, guide them to move around, and also reduce the growth timer of baby wolves by 10%.
Cooked rabbit meat can be used to craft rabbit meat stew.

Rabbit stew

Ingredients
Crafting recipe

Cooked Rabbit +Carrot +Baked Potato +Red Mushroom or Brown Mushroom +Bowl

Press and hold the Use key in the hotkey bar to eat rabbit stew. Doing so will restore 10 () and 12 hunger saturation.
After eating the rabbit stew, it will turn into a bowl.
Can be eaten by wolves to restore 10HP HP.
