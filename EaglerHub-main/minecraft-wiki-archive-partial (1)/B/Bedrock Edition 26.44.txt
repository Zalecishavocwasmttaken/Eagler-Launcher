26.44 is a hotfix to Bedrock Edition released on August 14, 2026, for most devices, and on August 18, 2026, for Nintendo Switch, which fixes bugs.

Fixes
7 issues fixed
MCPE-240476 – Vibrant Visuals can no longer be enabled in worlds with the Education features toggle enabled.
MCPE-240527 – Arrows shot downwards get stuck in the player’s own hitbox.
MCPE-241324 – After 26.40 update, game closes itself entirely when using the back button of phone.
MCPE-241415 – Quick moving items in the hotbar while in Creative mode will remove the items entirely.
MCPE-241436 – Non-operator players receive self-selection expansion when using commands.
MCPE-241505 – Attempting to join old worlds fails and gives "Block" error code every time, even in singleplayer.
MCPE-241528 – minecraft:overworld minecraft:dimension_bounds doesn’t work since 1.26.40.
