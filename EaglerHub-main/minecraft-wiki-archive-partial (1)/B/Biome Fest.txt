Track description
Previews of tracks in Minecraft have been shortened to 30 seconds on this wiki in accordance with fair use rationale. "11" and "Magnetic Circuit" are exempt from this.

"Biome Fest" begins with a dreamlike choir of synthesized voices fading in, which makes way to an electronic song with bells and tribal percussion in the background. It ends with the same choir that it begins with, fading out.

Quotes
““Biome Fest” is a song that I created when Minecraft Alpha was done, but I felt it had no place anywhere in the game, until the creative mode was sort of reintroduced to the game. It’s one of my favourite songs I’ve ever created. I do love minimalism and achieving tones with just very few notes, and I think this song does that extremely well.„ — C418 on c418.org
Trivia
This track was previously used in a video by Notch to demonstrate biomes.
It is also one of the only two tracks on Minecraft - Volume Beta to feature a description of some kind, linking to the video made by Notch.
A remixed version of "Biome Fest" by C418 was released as "Biome Party" for the album 148.
