Breath of the Nautilus is a status effect that is granted while riding a nautilus or zombie nautilus which prevents the rider's oxygen bar from depleting.

Effect
The effect prevents an entity from drowning underwater by freezing their oxygen bar. If the entity's oxygen bar is already empty, the effect prevents them from taking drowning damage.

Immune mobs
Withers and ender dragons are immune to Breath of the Nautilus.
All mobs immune to drowning are unaffected by Breath of the Nautilus.
