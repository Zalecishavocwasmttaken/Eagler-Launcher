v1.4.0 (released as v26.30) is a release for the Bedrock Editor released in Preview on May 26, 2026, and in retail on June 16, 2026.

Changes
Disabled the "Export World" button on the Edit Project screen. Worlds can be exported by going to "File" → "Export" and selecting "Shareable World" from the dropdown.
