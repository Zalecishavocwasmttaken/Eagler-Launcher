A birch forest is a lush biome filled with birch trees and wildflowers that usually generates near other woodland biomes.

Generation
Birch forest biomes are slightly rarer than regular forest biomes. They generate in medium temperature, between regular forests and dark forests in terms of humidity. They can also border jungles and taigas, as well as oceans or temperate mountains.
Regular birch forests are most commonly separated from old growth birch forests by rivers. Lush caves may generate under a birch forest because of its slightly high humidity, unless the birch forest is far from the ocean.

Description
Birch forests feature birch trees across the landscape that generate as densely as trees in regular forests. Unlike regular forest biomes, oak trees and wolves do not spawn here.
Between the birches, scattered bushes, rose bushes, lilacs, peonies, and lilies of the valley can be found, while wildflowers commonly cover the ground in patches. Bee nests housing 2–3 bees spawn on 0.2%‌[JE  only] or 0.035%‌[BE  only] of birches.
Abandoned camps sometimes generate in birch forests.​[upcoming: Third Drop 2026]
