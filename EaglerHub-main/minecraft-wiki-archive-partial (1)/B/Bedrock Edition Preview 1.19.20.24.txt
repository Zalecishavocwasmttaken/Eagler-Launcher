Beta 1.19.20.24 (Android) or Preview 1.19.20.24 (Windows, iOS, iPadOS, Xbox) is the fourth and the final beta/Preview for Bedrock Edition 1.19.20, released on July 27, 2022, which fixed some bugs.

Contents

1 Fixes
2 References
3 Navigation

Fixes
Blocks

Campfires can once again be stacked correctly (MCPE-159398)
Graphical

Reverted the fix for MCPE-105487, which can cause the textures to turn pink after playing for long periods of time on a server.
Fixed an issue with RTX on Windows where point lights were unintentionally accumulated for emissive blocks (MCPE-159485, MCPE-159488)
Items

Fixed a bug where pistons sometimes did not drop the piston item when broken by the arm (MCPE-158314)
Fixed a bug where bows could fail to shoot an arrow the first time they were used, this also affected crossbows and throwing tridents (MCPE-159467)
Performance and Stability

The game no longer crashes when browsing the Marketplace
Fixed a crash related to spawn eggs (MCPE-159302)
