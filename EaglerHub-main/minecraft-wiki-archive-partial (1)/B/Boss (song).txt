"Boss" is a song composed by C418 that plays during the ender dragon fight.
Unlike other songs by C418 in the Minecraft soundtrack, this was neither part of any soundtrack albums nor released as a single.

Track description
Previews of tracks in Minecraft have been shortened to 30 seconds on this wiki in accordance with fair use rationale. "11" and "Magnetic Circuit" are exempt from this.

"Boss" is largely based on modified versions of some themes from Minecraft - Volume Alpha. The theme is a dark ambient piece with an ominous air of desolation.
Throughout the track, extremely distorted/remixed versions of parts of the tracks "Cat", "Haggstrom" and "Subwoofer Lullaby" can be heard while ambient background noises gradually increase in intensity.

Raw music files
In Java Edition, the music files can be found in .minecraft/assets/objects.
In Bedrock Edition and Minecraft Education, it can be found in:

Mobile: com.mojang/resource_packs/music/vanilla_music/sounds/music/game/end/
Windows: Content\data\resource_packs\vanilla_music\sounds\music\game\end
All music files are in Ogg Vorbis.
