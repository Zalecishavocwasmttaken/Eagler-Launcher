Skins

Name

Render

Description

Notes

Rancid Anne

A woman who is half-human, and half-zombie.

Free to use without purchasing the pack.

Ol' Diggy

An undead miner with pale skin and tattered clothes.

Free to use without purchasing the pack.

Bedlam

A monstrous, humanoid red bed.

This skin was originally called "The Sham" before being renamed at an unknown date.

Crisp

A person seemingly caught in an explosion, covered in soot.

The Enderall

An enderman-like figure with humanoid proportions; save for their jaw, which is inhumanly unhinged.

Farlander

A glitched, distorted person with parts of their body visibly floating.

The Headless Haunter

A take on the Headless Horseman.

The Lapis Lady

A wraith-like figure wearing a lapis-dyed dress.

Lumbering Jack

A lumberjack with an axe lodged in his back.

The Arisen Rose

A spirit covered in roses.

The Sea-Swallowed Captain

A drowned-like pirate captain.

The Blocksquatch

A take on Bigfoot or the Yeti.

The Unshorn

A sheep-like creature, with pale skin, red eyes, and an exposed ribcage.

Silksnatcher

A spidery person with stoney skin, wearing a dress made out of cobwebs.

Cropsy

A scarecrow, with hay for arms and a melon head, featuring a villager nose.

The Well Wisher

Resembles Sadako Yamamura from the horror film franchise The Ring, with a well ornament on her head.
