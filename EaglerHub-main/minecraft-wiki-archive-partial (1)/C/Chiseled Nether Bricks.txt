Obtaining
Breaking
Chiseled Nether bricks can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Usage
Chiseled nether bricks are a decorative block. The block has the face of a wither skeleton engraved on it. See also Easter eggs#Mobs on chiseled blocks.
