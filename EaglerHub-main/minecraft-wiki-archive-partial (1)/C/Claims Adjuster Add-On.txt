The Claims Adjuster Add-On is an official Marketplace add-on developed by Minecraft. Released alongside the add-on was the Ice Cube Claims Adjuster, used to promote the release.

Additions
Mobs
 Adjuster
Known as “Larry” in-game
Spawns near the player's spawn point, if no suitable block is to spawn on, he will spawn on a stone block
"Asks" the player certain questions from a dialogue database
Once dialogue is completed, it will give broken wooden tools, diamonds, the player's items before death, or summon a Claims Adjuster Golem
Spawns the Golem when killed
 Golem
Called "The System" in-game
Neutral
Spawns once completing the questions from the Adjuster
Attacks monsters (except creepers), and hoglins and zoglins (when not in Peaceful mode)
Drops the player's items before death when killed (or iron ingots when summoned with spawn egg), and sometimes other items
 Baby Golem
Called "The System" in-game
Smaller version of the Golem
Behaves similarly to the normal Golem
Only spawnable with commands
