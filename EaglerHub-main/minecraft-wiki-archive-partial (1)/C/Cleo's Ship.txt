Cleo's ship is the primary setting of the second volume of the Minecraft graphic series. It is a naval vessel that is the base of a group headed by Cleo and Aiden.

Location

The ship is docked on the corner of a remote stone island. An ocean monument is located nearby.

Architecture

The vessel has a maroon color scheme, roughly the color of mangrove wood. On deck are some anonymous barrels alongside two large black sails. At least five cannons are on one side of the ship, which are activated by a lever and launch TNT.

History
The ship was built as a base for Cleo and Aiden at an unknown period of time earlier in the story, luring an elder guardian out of a nearby ocean monument.
The Gang first find the ship from a map leading to the island nearby the ship, discovered at the then-ransacked Skeleton Cove. The Gang discover Cleo's group has been holding Tyler's friends from gaming club hostage. Attempts to resolve the situation peacefully dissolve into battle, which concludes with both groups uniting to defeat the now-loose Elder Guardian, which was released when Evan accidentally fired the TNT cannons, destroying the wall that kept the Elder Guardian in. The operation is a success, and the denizens of the ship ally with The Gang.
