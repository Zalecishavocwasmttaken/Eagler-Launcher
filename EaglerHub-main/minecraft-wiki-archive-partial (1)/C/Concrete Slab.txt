Concrete slabs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Usage
 For information about the placement mechanics of all slabs, see Slab § Usage.

Concrete slabs come in sixteen colors, which are white, light gray, gray, black, brown, red, orange, yellow, lime, green, cyan, light blue, blue, purple, magenta, and pink.
