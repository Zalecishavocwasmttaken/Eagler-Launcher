This page describes content that has been removed and was only present in earlier versions of Minecraft. 
The Bedrock Edition Creative inventory was overhauled completely in Bedrock Edition 1.2.0 in beta 1.2.0.2. The Java Edition Creative inventory received major modifications after The Flattening in 17w47a and was overhauled completely in 1.19.3 in 22w42a.

This page documents the historical layouts of the Creative inventory in Java Edition prior to Java Edition 1.19.3, and pre-The Flattening in 17w47a; as well as in Bedrock Edition prior to the introduction of the recipe book in Bedrock Edition beta 1.2.0.2. In the case of Bedrock Edition, the introduction of the recipe book in the Better Together Update is the only significant major overhaul to the layout of the Creative inventory since the introduction of the tabbed categories on that edition, even to this current day.

Java Edition Creative inventory before 1.13
Prior to The Flattening, the order of blocks and items in the Creative inventory on Java Edition was fixed directly based on numerical block and item IDs. This meant that the layout and order of items in the Creative inventory remained fixed since the introduction of the tabbed categories for blocks and items in Creative in 1.3.1 in 12w21b. After The Flattening in 17w47a, the layout of blocks and items could be shuffled around, meaning the arrangement of items in the Creative inventory could be, and was changed, after 17w47a.

The introduction of the Better Together Update's Bedrock Edition, and the addition of the recipe book in Bedrock Edition 1.2.0, was the only major significant overhaul to the Creative inventory on Bedrock Edition. This remains the only major overhaul to the Creative inventory on Bedrock Edition even to this day.

Legacy Console Edition
The Legacy Console Edition has never had any major sweeping changes to its Creative inventory. All historical changes to the Creative inventory on Legacy Console can be, and are described in full, at Creative inventory/History#Legacy Console Edition. As the whole edition of the game was discontinued in late 2019, the Creative inventory for this edition is found at Legacy Console Edition exclusive features#Creative inventory.

New Nintendo 3DS Edition
The tabs of the Creative inventory on the New Nintendo 3DS Edition had remained identical since its initial release. As the New Nintendo 3DS Edition of the game was discontinued in 2019, the Creative inventory for this edition can be found at New Nintendo 3DS Edition exclusive features#Creative inventory.
