Cameron Manning (born February 22, 2004), better known by his username camman18, is an American YouTuber and Twitch streamer known for his short-form videos about Minecraft, specifically about Java snapshot releases, lesser-known game mechanics and the rarity of the game's different features. He was one of the special guests at Minecraft Live – March 2026's The Deep Dig aftershow.

Minecraft franchise
In 2025, Manning uploaded the video "I Interviewed Mojang...", where he interviewed Anna Lundgren, the senior product manager of Mojang Studios. In the interview, Manning asked Lundgren questions about their game philosophy, such as feature rarity, the line between realism and fantasy in game design, difficulty balancing for casual and complex players, and balancing quantity over quality in updates. Manning also asked about the nautilus and whether it was planned during or after the addition of the nautilus shell,[verify] and about the possibilty of faster minecarts.

Events
Minecraft Experience: Villager Rescue
In late 2024, Manning was invited as a VIP in the opening of the Minecraft Experience: Villager Rescue event in Plano, Texas.

Minecraft Live – March 2026
In 2026, Manning was invited to be a special guest at Minecraft Live – March 2026, as revealed by a TikTok posted by Minecraft revealing all the guests.
He had previously made a video introducing the golden dandelion during snapshot releases of Tiny Takeover, where he says "Then we got the Golden Dandelion, which is a golden dandelion". The quote became a meme across social media, primarily TikTok. During the show, Vu Bui asked Manning about the meme.
In the aftershow, Manning played the Chaos Cubed game drop with Vu Bui, Agnes, and Jeb, playing mini-games using the sulfur cube.
