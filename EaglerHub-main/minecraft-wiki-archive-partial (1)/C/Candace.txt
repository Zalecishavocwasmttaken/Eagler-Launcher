Biography
Some time ago, Candace joined a group chat with the rest of the Gang.
Candace returned with Grace from the Nether with the final wither skeleton skull. She journeyed with the rest of the party to the stronghold, where she killed the Ender dragon along with Grace. She happily accepts an offer from Grace to go on a date.
For much of the start of the second session, Candace is busy with soccer practice, but she eventually joins, taking a diplomatic stance in the confrontation with Cleo.
Candace spends some time reconciling with Grace, who disassociated with the group for some time. Eventually, Grace meets up with Candace at the latter's house.
