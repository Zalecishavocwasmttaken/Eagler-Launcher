Obtaining
Breaking
Concrete stairs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Usage
 For information about the placement mechanics of all stairs, see Stairs § Usage.

Concrete stairs come in sixteen colors, which are white, light gray, gray, black, brown, red, orange, yellow, lime, green, cyan, light blue, blue, purple, magenta, and pink.
