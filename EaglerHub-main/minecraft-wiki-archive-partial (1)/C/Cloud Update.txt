The Cloud Update is a major update to Minecraft Education released on July 23, 2024 as Minecraft Education 1.21.03. It adds the ability to backup worlds to OneDrive, as well as introducing new features from the Bats and Pots, Armored Paws, and Tricky Trials updates.

Changes
Items
General

Chemistry items like balloons, compounds, and glow sticks are now available in the Creative inventory.
General
The "Manage Account" button has been moved to the bottom right of the home screen.
Further revisions
1.21.05 was released to add support to cloud world backup to OneDrive in macOS and iOS, and fix some bugs.
1.21.06 was released to improve coding performance.
1.21.10 was released to fix some bugs and remove the option to disable "Only Allow Trusted Skins".
