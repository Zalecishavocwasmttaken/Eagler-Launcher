2009
April 2009
29 - Infiniminer is released, a game by Zachtronics Industries (creators of the critically acclaimed 2011 puzzle game SpaceChem), whose concept mainly inspired Notch for Minecraft. It was regularly updated and became open-source less than a month later.
May 2009 - Birth of Minecraft
10 - Development on "Cave Game" started using RubyDung, an abandoned game project of Notch, and Infiniminer as bases for the code.
13 - Java: First video of "Cave Game", Block placement, first Mobs (simply named Mob), physics created (Cave game tech test).
14 - Java: "Cave Game" renamed to "Minecraft: Order of the Stone" after a suggestion by RinkuHero from The Independent Games Wiki on an IRC with Notch, "Minecraft" being an analogy to the famous real-time strategy game StarCraft (Pre-Classic).
16 - Java:  Early private testing, among TIGSource members (Classic 0.0.2a-0.0.9a).
17 - First release of the game (Classic 0.0.11a). Game name shortened to simply "Minecraft".
21 - Accounts can now be registered.
24 - An official Minecraft Forum is opened.
June 2009
1 - Java: First Multiplayer test (Classic 0.0.15a (Multiplayer Test 1)). Notch starts his new job at Jalbum.
7 - Java: Multiplayer released (Java Edition Classic 0.0.16a).
14 - Java: Custom Skins released (Classic 0.0.18a).
August 2009
4 - Java: First elements of Survival shown.
18 - Java: Introduction of Health shown.
September 2009
1 - Beginning of SURVIVAL TEST (Classic 0.24_SURVIVAL_TEST).
October 2009
9 - Passwords can now be changed.
December 2009
2 - Minecraft 4k released.
4 - Minecraft 4k contest is over, discontinuing it.
22 - Hayden Scott-Baron becomes the Minecraft Artist.
23 - Minecraft for Java passes to Indev development stage (Indev 0.31 20091223-0040) (which requires a 9.95 € Premium Account to be accessed), introduction of Torches and dynamic lighting.

January 2010

29 - Java: Introduction of the Crafting system (Indev 0.31-20100129-1447).

27 - Minecraft for Java passes to Infdev development stage, introduction of infinite maps.

17 - Minecraft passes 20,000 sales.

25 - Java: Seecret Friday Update 2 released. Dungeons introduced.
30 - Minecraft for Java passes to Alpha development stage (Java Edition Alpha v1.0.0), it is now possible to play on a client window.

30 - Java: Seecret Friday Update 7 (Alpha v1.0.14) released. Chickens and Music Discs introduced.

30 - Original Herobrine livestream is created.
31 - MinecraftCon, a spontaneous gathering of over 50 fans after a call by Notch, is held in Bellevue, Washington.
September 2010
2 - Notch returns from the trip to the U.S.
10 - Java: Seecret Friday update 9 (Java Edition Alpha v1.1.0) released. Compasses introduced.
13 - Daniel "Kappische" Kaplan joins the Mojang team.
18 - Java: The Seecret Saturday update (Java Edition Alpha v1.1.1) released. Sneaking introduced. Minecraft technically becomes free-to-play for the weekend, due to the login server not working correctly.
23 - Minecraft Classic is available again on Minecraft.net.
October 2010

24 - Notch names the default player character "Steve".
30 - Java: Java Edition Alpha v1.2.0 "Halloween Update" released. Nether introduced.

20 - Minecraft for Java passes to Beta development stage (Java Edition Beta 1.0), price raised from 10.00 € to 15.00 €.
22 - Java: Java Edition Beta 1.1 released.
