Chris Nordgren is a virtual effects artist and 3D artist at Mojang Studios, who worked on Minecraft Dungeons. He had also developed and published Everhood and Everhood 2 alongside Jordi Roca, as Foreign Gnomes. He is currently working on the upcoming Everhood Hunters.
