A dark skinned person wearing an orange, blue, and white robot costume.

A vampiric figure wearing a black coat and red vest, with tall red eyes.

A pale skinned figure wearing a mask of a werewolf, a green jacket, white undershirt, ripped pants, and furry gloves and boots.
