Chaos Cubed is a collection of skins promoting Chaos Cubed. The pack consists of three skins, all of them were based on a character created to promote a series of Character Creator items, like its original design.

Brawler Skin

A light skinned character wearing a TNT shirt and wearing a red football helmet.

Simplified versions of both the Brawler Jersey and Helmet, made to fit the limitations of skins.

Builder Skin

A dark skinned character wearing a green vest with a golden pickaxe and wearing a construction hard hat.

Simplified versions of both the Builder Jersey and Helmet, made to fit the limitations of skins.

Rancher Skin

A light skinned character wearing a Sulfur Cube shirt, while also wearing a Sulfur Cube Head.

Simplified versions of both the Rancher Jersey and the Sulfur Cube Head, made to fit the limitations of skins.
