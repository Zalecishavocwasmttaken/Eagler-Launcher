Smelting
Copper ingots can be obtained by smelting raw copper in a furnace or blast furnace, as well as the ore itself if mined using Silk Touch.

Mob loot
See also: Tutorial:Drowned farming

When killed by a player or a tamed wolf, a drowned has a 11% (11⁄100) chance of dropping a copper ingot. With the Looting enchantment, the chance can be increased to 13% (13⁄100) with Looting I, 15% (3⁄20) with Looting II, and 17% (17⁄100) with Looting III. Copper golems also drop 1-3 copper ingots when killed, which is unaffected by Looting in Bedrock Edition.
