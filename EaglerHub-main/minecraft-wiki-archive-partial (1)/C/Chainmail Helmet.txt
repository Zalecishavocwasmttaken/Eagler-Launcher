A chainmail helmet is an uncommon variant of helmet that cannot be crafted, and is only obtainable through other means such as trading. It is the fourth-highest tier of helmet, having the same armor points and durability as an iron helmet but with higher enchantability.

Obtaining
Mob loot
Zombies, husks, zombie villagers‌[Java Edition  only], skeletons, strays, bogged, and parched have a small chance to spawn wearing armor, which may include a chainmail helmet. There is a 8.5% chance (9.5% with Looting I, 10.5% with Looting II and 11.5% with Looting III) for the mob to drop the helmet they are wearing upon death unless they are spawned from an ominous trial spawner‌[Java Edition  only]. The dropped helmet is usually badly damaged, and may be enchanted with enchantment levels 5-19.

Chainmail Helmet

A chainmail helmet can be repaired in an anvil by adding iron ingots, with each ingot restoring 25% the helmet's maximum durability, rounded down. Two chainmail helmets can also be combined in an anvil. Both methods preserve the helmet's enchantments.

Armor trimming
It is possible to upgrade armor with trims. This requires a smithing template, a chainmail helmet, and an ingot or crystal (iron ingot, copper ingot, gold ingot, netherite ingot, emerald, redstone dust, lapis lazuli, amethyst shard, nether quartz, diamond, or resin brick).
These trims have no effect on the gameplay or strength of the armor.
