Cartoon Network is a television network created to air and promote exclusively animated series, such as Dexter's Laboratory, The Powerpuff Girls, Edd, Edd n' Eddy, Codename: Kids Next Door, Ben 10, Adventure Time, Regular Show and The Amazing World of Gumball. Several properties made by the network have collaborated with Mojang Studios.

Adventure Time is a children's television show which follows Finn and Jake in the Land of Ooo.
In 2017, a mash-up pack themed around the show was released. MINECON Earth 2017 showcased a non-canon Adventure Time episode with lots of Minecraft elements called "Diamonds and Lemons".
