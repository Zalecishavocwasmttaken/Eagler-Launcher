A cobblestone wall is a decorative wall variant of cobblestone that generates in Overworld structures and is used for building.

Breaking
Cobblestone walls can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Natural generation
Cobblestone walls generate in woodland mansions. They also generate in pillager outpost towers, abandoned camps​[upcoming: Third Drop 2026], and in some houses in plains, taiga, and snowy tundra villages. They can also generate in snowy taiga villages in Bedrock Edition.
