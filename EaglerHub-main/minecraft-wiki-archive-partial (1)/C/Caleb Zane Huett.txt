Caleb Zane Huett is an American author, known for writing novels aimed at young audiences, such as Top Elf and Buster. Outside of writing, he is known for creating the tabletop RPG Triangle Agency.

In the Minecraft franchise
In 2024, Huett took over the Minecraft: Woodsword Chronicles series from Nick Eliopulos, becoming the lead writer of the Minecraft: Ironsword Academy. This line would run up to 2026, concluding with Last Exit to the End!
In 2026, he helmed the second novel of the Minecraft Dungeons series, The Rift.

Trivia
His favorite part of Minecraft are the axolotls.
