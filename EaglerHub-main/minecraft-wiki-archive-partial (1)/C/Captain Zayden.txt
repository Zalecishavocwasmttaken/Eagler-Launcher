Biography



Spoiler warning! This section contains detailed information about Minecraft: The Dragon that may spoil your enjoyment of it. Read at your own risk!

A few years ago, Mayor Maxine recruited Zayden from another town, where he led a legion of volunteers to battle illagers.
In the first raid, he tells Zetta, Rift, and Rayne what to do, later giving the former an enchanted bow. In a later raid, he organizes the use of the TNT cannons before calling for a retreat as defeat seemed inevitable. At the end of the book, he uses a shovel given to him by Zetta to carve out a smile in a lump of coal as an apology to the Ender Dragon.
