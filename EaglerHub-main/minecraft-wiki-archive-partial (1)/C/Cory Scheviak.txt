Cory 'Cojomax99' Scheviak is a member of the Minecraft: Java Edition and Minecraft Dungeons development teams at Mojang Studios. He first announced that he was joining the Mojang team on May 11, 2018. Most of what he does with the Mojang team is fixing bugs. He is currently actively developing the Tropicraft mod for Minecraft: Java Edition. He started playing Minecraft in October 2010, and began developing mods in 2011.

Trivia
He designed the original Bad Omen mechanic, where after defeating a pillager captain, the player would trigger a raid the instant they entered a village. This was later changed to the current mechanic, where the player can use an ominous bottle to have full control over when a raid starts.
