Crack in the Code! is the first installment of the Minecraft: Stonesword Saga series, authored by Nick Eliopulos. The book continues the story from where the Minecraft: Woodsword Chronicles series left off.

Synopsis
“Based on the most popular video game of all time, this all-new chapter book series takes a group of intrepid Minecraft players deeper into the game than ever before. Someone–or something–has turned the Evoker King to stone. And now a new player, Theo, has joined the team on their quest to return their former enemy to normal. Theo’s has coding skills that could come in handy, but does he have what it takes to be part of the team, or will his meddling put a crack in the game code that none of them will survive? „ — Description
