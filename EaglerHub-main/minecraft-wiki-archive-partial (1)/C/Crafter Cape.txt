The Crafter Cape is a cape that was given to TwitchCon 2026 attendees.

Appearance
The cape's design depicts an iron pickaxe laying on a crafting table background. Apart from the pickaxe's orientation, it has the same design as the Builder Cape.

Distribution
This cape could be obtained at the in-person TwitchCon 2026 event in Rotterdam, which took place on May 30–31, 2026.
Guests received an inventory ribbon that could be attached to their TwitchCon badge during badge pickup. They then had to participate in either Minecraft content sessions or arena activities to receive emerald stickers. Once guests had collected three emerald stickers, they could take the inventory ribbon to the Cape Trader booth to receive a code card. This code could be redeemed to obtain the cape.
Various content creators invited to TwitchCon to playtest new features were given additional codes that were given away to their viewers.

Trivia
This is the first cape to be exclusive to a convention since the MINECON 2016 Cape.
The cape's design resembles the teaser poster for A Minecraft Movie Squared.
Although the redemption period was stated to end on June 30, 2026, this deadline has not been enforced, and codes can still be redeemed. This is consistent with the fact that Mojang has typically not enforced their stated cape code redemption deadlines.
