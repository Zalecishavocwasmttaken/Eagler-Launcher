Camps & Clubs is a minor update for Education Edition. Development for this update started with beta Education Edition 1.14.60. This update expands eligibility to camps, after-school programs, homeschools, nonprofits, and other organizations. Education Edition 1.14.70 was released on May 26, 2021.
