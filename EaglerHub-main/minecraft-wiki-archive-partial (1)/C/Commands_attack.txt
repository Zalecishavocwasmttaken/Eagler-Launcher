Let the agent attack in the direction specified.

2 Arguments
3 Result

Syntax
attack <direction: string>
Arguments
direction: string

Specifies the direction of Agent to attack.
Must be one of:
forward to attack forward.
back to attack backward.
left to attack leftward.
right to attack rightward.
up to attack upward.
down to attack downward.
Result
Always success, Agent attacks in the specified direction by 8HP attack damage.
