A cartographer is a villager that trades various maps, compasses, as well as banners and banner patterns. Cartographers wear a golden monocle and an empty map pack.

Spawning
An unemployed villager turns into a cartographer when an unclaimed cartography table is nearby.

Natural generation
The workstation of cartographers, the cartography table, generates in cartographer houses in villages.

Drops
In Java Edition, a cartographer can drop various items by throwing them as a gift towards a nearby player with the Hero of the Village effect. The gift is randomly selected from the following list:
