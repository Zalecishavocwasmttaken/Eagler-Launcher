Carl Manneh was a co-founder and former Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of Mojang Studios until he left the company on November 6, 2014.
Carl Manneh befriended Notch when Notch himself used to work at jAlbum , Unlike Notch's old workplace, MidasPlayer.com, Carl actively encouraged Notch to work on Minecraft.

Trivia
Carl Manneh has played golf with Barack Obama, former president of the United States of America. He described Obama as an "incredible person."
Before Mojang, Carl Manneh worked for jAlbum, where Markus Persson also used to work.
