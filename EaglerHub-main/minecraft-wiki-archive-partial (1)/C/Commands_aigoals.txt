This page describes content that has been removed from Minecraft. 
This feature was present in earlier versions of Minecraft, but has since been removed.



This feature is exclusive to Bedrock Edition.

Toggles the display of AI and pathfinding info of mobs.

Contents

1 Showed information
2 Syntax
3 Result
4 History
5 Gallery
6 Navigation

Showed information
When the drawing of AI goals is enabled, it shows information above every mob's head.

Resource location of the mob's identifier (e.g.: minecraft:cow)
Health and maximum health of the mob (e.g.: Health: 10/10)
Speed of the mob (e.g.: Speed: 0,250000)
Active AI goals of the mob (e.g.: RandomLookAround)
Syntax
aigoals

Result
Always successful.

History
Bedrock Edition1.2.13release
Accidentally added developer commands to iOS, including /aigoals.1.2.14
Removed all developer commands.
