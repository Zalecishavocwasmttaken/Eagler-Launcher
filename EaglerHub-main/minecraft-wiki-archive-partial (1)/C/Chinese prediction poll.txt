The Chinese prediction poll was a popularity poll held in China, teasing the announcement at MINECON Earth 2018 of a Chinese "national treasure" coming to Minecraft. The poll's result did not affect the development of Minecraft.

Results
The available options were the giant panda, Chinese alligator, golden monkey, white-lipped deer, and river dolphin, with the giant panda eventually winning the poll upon its conclusion on September 21, 2018. It was then announced at MINECON Earth 2018, eight days later, as part of the Village & Pillage update.
