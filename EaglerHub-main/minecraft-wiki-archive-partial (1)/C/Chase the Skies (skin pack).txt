Chase the Skies is a collection of skins promoting Chase the Skies. The pack consists of two skins, one based off a character created to promote a series of Character Creator items, the other an original design.

Ghast Riding Swimmer

A swimmer wearing cyan with a bit of dark cyan swimsuit, with warm skin, light yellow hair, goggles, and a bright smile. Its clothes, a swimsuit with happy ghast with goggles floaters on both arms, with harness floatie the waist and sandals.

A simplified version of Ghast Rider Floatie, made to fit the limitations of skins.

Happy Ghast Pilot

A pilot wearing a brown flight jacket with a hook on both arms, and gloves with white fur along with a white scarf, brown helmet and goggles. Its other clothes, black pants with gold trimmings resembling a happy ghast equipped with the black harness and dark brown boots.

Another simplified version of Happy Ghast Pilot Gear, made to fit the limitations of skins. Underneath the mask, there are no facial features at all.
