Obtaining
Breaking
Chiseled stone bricks can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Infested blocks
Chiseled stone bricks can also be obtained by mining its respective infested block with Silk Touch.

Natural generation
Chiseled stone bricks can generate as part of ocean ruins and ruined portals. In addition, three chiseled stone bricks generate as part of each jungle pyramid. They also generate in igloo basements, though some may be infested.
