Cobalt is a 2D action platformer developed by Oxeye Game Studio with the assistance of Mojang and Microsoft Game Studios, and published by Mojang. Originally launching in Alpha on December 15, 2011, Cobalt was released on February 2, 2016, on Steam, Xbox 360, and Xbox One. To cross-promote with Minecraft and to support the launch of Cobalt's spinoff, Cobalt W.A.S.D., a new tileset based on the Minecraft world ("Blockworld") was added to the Cobalt level editor as part of a free update.

About
Cobalt is a 2D Action-Adventure game focused on its fast physics-based movement, versatile and high-powered weaponry, time dilation where the game speed around the player slows to improve reaction times when facing lethal danger, and a combat roll which enables the player to aim their weapons at any angle as well as deflect oncoming fire with the top of their head. The game is split into seven core game modes: "Deathmatch", "Team Strike", "Plug Slam", "Survival", "Speed Challenges", "Combat Challenges", and a campaign "Story Mode".

Minecraft skin
Agent Cobalt, the player character of Cobalt's story mode, is a skin released as part of Skin Pack 2 for Legacy Console Edition. The skin is free to use without purchasing the pack on Xbox editions, but requires purchase to be used in the PlayStation editions. The skin pack was re-released for Bedrock Edition as Skin Pack 2 - Classic and is free to use without purchasing the pack. The Agent Cobalt skin is depicted as having a full Metalface explorer hull and a charged hard shieldbelt from the game equipped.

Blockworld tileset
The "Blockworld" tileset was released as part of update v254 for Cobalt on November 28, 2017, just ahead of the release of Cobalt W.A.S.D. It features 108 tiles based on overworld assets from the pre–Texture Update default resources. In v257, a bug was fixed which prevented the "Blockworld" textures from working in the Autodesk Stingray builds of Cobalt and Cobalt W.A.S.D.
