A cut sandstone slab is a decorative slab variant of cut sandstone that does not generate naturally and is used for building.

Obtaining
Breaking
Cut sandstone slabs can be mined using any pickaxe. If mined without a pickaxe, they drop nothing.

Cut sandstone slabs have the stone_slab4 ID, however their item form's ID is incorrectly registered as double_stone_slab4.

The direct item form of double cut sandstone slabs have the real_double_stone_slab4 ID.1.19.0Preview 1.19.0.27
The IDs of cut sandstone slabs and double cut sandstone slabs have been changed to stone_block_slab4 and double_stone_block_slab4 respectively.1.20.30Preview 1.20.20.20
