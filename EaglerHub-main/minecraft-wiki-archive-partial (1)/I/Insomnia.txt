Insomnia is a mechanic in Minecraft that allows phantoms to spawn around players that have not entered a bed or died for three in-game days (1 hour or 72000 ticks). In Java Edition, this value is specifically defined by the "Since last rest" statistic. It can be disabled with the command /gamerule spawn_phantoms false in Java Edition or /gamerule doinsomnia false in Bedrock Edition.
Insomnia may refer to:

Phantoms – phantoms may only spawn when a player has insomnia.
Sleeping – sleeping or entering a bed are methods to reset the insomnia counter.
/gamerule spawn_phantoms‌[Java Edition  only] or /gamerule doinsomnia‌[Bedrock Edition  only] – a game rule that enables or disables insomnia, and thus, phantom spawning.
