Generated loot
Infinite water sources could be found in starting house chests in stacks of 99 in Indev 0.31 20100124-2310, and stacks of 100 in Indev 0.31 20100128-2200. Prior to this, they were added to the player's inventory by default in a stack of 5.

Usage
As infinite water sources are full solid cubes, placed gears render on their sides.
The infinite water source has many visual and functional differences from normal water:

It is a full, solid cube block with full block collision.
It is completely opaque.
All sides use the same still water texture.
Creating liquids
Immediately after placement, the infinite water source creates source blocks of water on each of its four orthogonal sides. If these created sources are removed, they get regenerated after enough time.

Sponges
Infinite water sources are deleted by sponges.

The infinite water source has been removed. As the ID is now empty, it now crashes the game with a NullPointerException if a chunk containing said block is loaded or an inventory containing one opened.20100625-1917
