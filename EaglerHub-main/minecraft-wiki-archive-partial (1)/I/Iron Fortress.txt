The Iron Fortress is a location in Minecraft: The Haven Trials, on the Anarchia server. It is the home base of Declan and his group. The base is named after the ubiquity of iron in its architecture, as well as the difficulty of breaking into it.

Geography

The Iron Fortress is "built into the side of a stone hill". The base is not far from an abandoned village used as a landmark by players of the server, and a dirt path leads from the base to the mountains where the third trial is located.

Architecture

In the near vicinity of the fortress are oak trees for decoration and a number of basic farms. The base has four entrances, each guarded by a pack of four wolves. Horses are also present near a base, presumably due to intervention by the Ocury.
The base comprises mostly iron. In the middle of the base is a large lounge-like area. Further away is Declan's room, which is adorned with an armor stand, a fireplace, and the heads of several killed players.

The base was presumably built some time ago and with assistance from the Ocury; the base was developed to the point of having such advanced equipment as clocks, potions, and automated sugar cane farms, along with some materials exclusive to the administrators, such as diamonds.
The protagonists went into the base at one point and stole some of its equipment, all without the residents noticing.
