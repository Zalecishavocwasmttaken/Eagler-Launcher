Summary
The video begins with the sound of birds and chicks chirping. There is a group of chicks in the grass. It then cuts to a shed with a copper golem and allay outside of it. After another cut, it goes to a wolf laying down in a grassy field. It then cuts to the wolf walking (followed by the chicks) with the large sentance "8 PLAYERS" above them. It then cuts to a wooden house where a Minecraft character steps outside and then it goes back to the real world. The person does some stretching and is revealed to be Tubbo. Tubbo opens a map and it goes back to the Minecraft world as Tubbo in Minecraft is walking with the words "8 COACHES" below his feet. Minecraft Tubbo then enters the Nether. Real world Tubbo continues to walk through a park. Real world Tubbo jumps out from behind a bush in front of the words "6 HOURS". Nether Tubbo meanwhile, is getting bombed by an angry ghast. Real world Tubbo gets shot at by some pillagers and appears standing under the Ender dragon. While that was happening, Minecraft Tubbo made it to the sea as well with real world Tubbo. They both go get into there boats and sail off into the sea as the logo appears above them.
