Description
The Chicken Jockey Lego set is referencing the popular Chicken Jockey meme. Which originated from A Minecraft Movie.
The set features a baby zombie riding a chicken, along with a small build inspired by the Minecraft world. It was released as part of the LEGO Minecraft theme.
