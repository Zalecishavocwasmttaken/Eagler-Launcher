57Digital is a mobile application development company based in Sheffield, England. It is run by Jamie Chapman and Luke Dixon, two software engineers from Sheffield. The company is responsible for creating an official Minecraft companion guide, Minecraft Explorer, along with several other applications previously available for iOS devices. Following this, they went on to become a partner on the Marketplace. and created several official collaborations.
