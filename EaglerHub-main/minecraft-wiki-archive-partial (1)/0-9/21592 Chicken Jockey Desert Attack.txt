The "21592 Chicken Jockey Desert Attack" is a Lego Minecraft set, released on June 1, 2026. The set comes with various BigFigs of various mobs, including a husk chicken jockey, an iron golem, a baby desert villager, a pale puppy, and a golden rabbit.
