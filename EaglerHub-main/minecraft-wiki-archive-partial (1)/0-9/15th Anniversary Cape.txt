The 15th Anniversary Cape is a cape that was given to users to celebrate the 15th anniversary of Minecraft alongside the Purple Heart Cape and the Follower's Cape.

Appearance
The cape features a light green checkered pattern with a creeper face. The back of the cape has a dark green checkered pattern with a light green "15" on the bottom.

Distribution
In order to get the cape, users had to go to Minecraft.net and claim it from a section on the 15th anniversary page. After doing so, it would appear in both Java Edition and Bedrock Edition.

History
May 17th, 2024

The section to claim the cape is removed from Minecraft.net.
