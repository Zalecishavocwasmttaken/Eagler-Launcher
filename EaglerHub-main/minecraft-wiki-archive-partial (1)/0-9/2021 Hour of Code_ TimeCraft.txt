Summary
An agent travels through time in an orb to year 1684 in England, where they find Issac Newton sitting under a tree reading a book. Spotting another tree full of falling apples, they carry him beneath the apple tree so an apple falls on his head, causing him to discover the three laws of motion. Once done, the agent teleports to the Moon during the Apollo 11 mission where Neil Armstrong gets stuck in his spaceship because of a rock mound blocking the door. In order to release him, the agent moves the spaceship away from the mound and Armstrong manages to come out. Then, the agent travels to 2520 BC during the construction of the Great Sphinx of Giza, using a pickaxe in order to remove the long nose from the sphinx. After that, the video continues on a shot of the map.
