The "21260 The Cherry Blossom Garden" is a Lego Minecraft that was released on June 1, 2024, featuring the first appearance of Sunny in the series.

Description
The set consists of Sunny with two cherry trees and a small player-built area made of fences and what appears to be birch planks. This location includes a decorated pot with the skull sherd, and a soul torch on top of a black object. A torchflower and a pitcher plant are planted in the ground by the trees beside a small stream. A sniffer, snifflet, and sniffer egg are also included, with the former having a mechanism allowing it to either sniff or dig up a mound containing what appears to be torchflower seeds. Two bees are included and are meant to be placed freely, along with a zombie.
