Summary
“Take the worlds of LEGO® and Minecraft® with you with this LEGO Adventure Box featuring full-color activities and a LEGO minifigure with accessory!Meet a LEGO® Minecraft® Armorsmith and follow him on his adventures through an amazing comic book-style story. This Adventure Box features unique LEGO Minecraft building activities to complete with your minifigure and accessories as well as mazes, puzzles, search and finds, and more! This LEGO Minecraft book makes a great gift and includes an Armorsmith minifigure and crafting table accessory!„ — Book description“Meet the Armorsmith. He’s a hardworking, adventurous smithy who spends his days crafting cool weapons and Armor.
Build your Armorsmith minifigure and kit him out with his sword and other accessories, before completing the puzzles, reading the comics and finding out all about his life in the LEGO® Minecraft® Universe!„ —
