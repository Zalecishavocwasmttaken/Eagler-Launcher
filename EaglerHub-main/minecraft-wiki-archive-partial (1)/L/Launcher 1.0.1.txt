1.0.1 is a launcher version released twice on June 26, 2013. No fixes or changes to this version were documented. The initial release was unsuccessful in enabling Yggdrasil. Due to login errors, the launcher was downgraded to 1.0. The second release, however, was successful in enabling Yggdrasil.
