LEGO Minecraft Ideas is a Minecraft book authored by Shari Last and Julia March, detailing several LEGO Minecraft building possibilities for children to create or be inspired by. It also includes a mini LEGO set.

Synopsis
“Use your LEGO collection to bring your Minecraft builds to life. Inspire budding builders to design a diamond sword, build a busy biome, make a micro mob, create a creeper house, and much more. Challenge family and friends to exciting games that combine the best bits of Minecraft and LEGO building. Comes with a diver minifigure and all the bricks needed to build an exclusive LEGO Minecraft mini model of an aquatic scene. Get ready to play!„
