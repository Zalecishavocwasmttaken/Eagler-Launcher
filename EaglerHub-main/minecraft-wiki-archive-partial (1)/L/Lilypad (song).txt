Track description
Previews of tracks in Minecraft have been shortened to 30 seconds on this wiki in accordance with fair use rationale. "11" and "Magnetic Circuit" are exempt from this.

This track features a similar but not exactly the same melody as some of Amos Roddy's previous game soundtracks he made, heard in Citizen Sleeper's Relink (Track 20) and Possible Futures (Track 23), and in Citizen Sleeper 2: Starward Vector's Imagined Futures (Track 34). It uses electronic instruments and an ambient composition style that resembles the Citizen Sleeper series' music.
