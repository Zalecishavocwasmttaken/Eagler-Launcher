This article documents an April Fools' Day joke. 
This feature was exclusively part of a joke version and is not currently planned for addition to regular Minecraft.

The Living Block Command is an entity only present in 26w14a. It acts as an indicator of what command you've given to a Living Block and where it needs to go to perform that task.
