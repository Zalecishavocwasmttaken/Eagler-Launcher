Additions
Items
 Benzoin Sapling
Found in jungles.
Can be placed.
Produces one benzoin when used when ready, then goes into a cooldown before another can be produced.
Can only be placed on dirt, rooted dirt, coarse dirt, and grass blocks.
Affected by gravity.
 Benzoin
Can be bought from merchants for one emerald
 Big Blue Bath Bomb
Crafted using seaweed, salt, lemon, and lavender
"Produces a peaceful liquid" when thrown.
Liquid puddle remains for 60 seconds.
Grants speed and water breathing to players.
Converts lava into obsidian.
Creates a "bubble shield" around players, making them immune to damage for 60 seconds.

 Guide Book
Crafted using a book and seaweed.
Contains explanations and recipes for the items included in the add-on.
Appears in the inventory upon starting the map.
 Intergalactic Bath Bomb
Crafted using mint, grapefruit, and cedarwood.
Creates "mesmerizing calm bubbles" when thrown.
Slows down mobs who touch the liquid, as well as creating anti-gravity.[needs testing]
Liquid puddle remains for 60 seconds..
Grants jump boost and slow falling to players.
Converts lava into obsidian.
 Lakes Bath Bomb

Liquid puddle remains for 60 seconds.
Grants resistance and health boost to players.
Converts lava into obsidian.
Attracts fish.[needs testing]
