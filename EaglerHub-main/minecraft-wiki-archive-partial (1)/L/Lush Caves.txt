The lush caves is a temperate cave biome with unique fauna and flora found underground below humid biomes, indicated above ground by azalea trees. Aquatic mobs such as glow squid and tropical fish can spawn here. It is also the only biome where axolotls spawn.

Generation
Lush caves generate underground at any altitude and temperature, underneath biomes with high humidity values (≥ 0.7). This makes them most commonly found beneath dark forests, jungles, old growth taigas, wooded badlands, and taigas within frozen biomes.
Less commonly, they generate under windswept forests, mangrove swamps, birch forests, other taigas, and snowy taigas. When these biomes generate far inland, however, dripstone caves may generate instead.
It is not possible for lush caves to generate beneath forests and grassland biomes like plains and savanna as they are not humid enough.
Lush caves can sometimes generate beneath oceans and deserts, whose generation is independent of humidity values.

Description
Azalea trees generate on any empty space above a lush cave, with roots consisting of rooted dirt and hanging roots that generate down until reaching the lush cave. This is most common at the surface, but they can sometimes generate inside caves if there is enough room.
Underground, moss covers the floors and ceilings, along with moss carpets, short grass, tall grass, and azalea bushes on the ground. On the ceiling, vines and cave vines with glow berries grow down and light up the caves, and spore blossoms grow from the ceiling and fill the caves with particles.
Shallow lakes lined with clay generate between the moss on the ground, filled with dripleaf plants. Tropical fish and axolotls are unique to these pools, whereas common cave mobs like bats and glow squid can also spawn. While hostile mobs can spawn throughout the biome, many areas have a light level greater than 0 due to the pervasive glow berries.
Stone and variants containing ores are sparsely exposed between the vegetation.
Asides from the regular underground structures that can generate in all biomes, woodland mansions rarely generate on the surface above lush caves in Bedrock Edition.
In Bedrock Edition, lush caves are arid biomes with no rain and dry grass colors, although this is barely noticable underground.
With Vibrant Visuals, lush caves biomes use a strong humid volumetric fog setting similar to jungles, which fades the distance in an orange tint. Lush caves have default atmospherics and lighting, and warmish color grading, which makes the biome look more humid and warm, while also notably increasing the color contrast.

Trivia
The Swedish painter John Bauer was one of the main inspirations behind the design and idea of the lush caves biome, specifically Bauer's 'Oskuldens Vandring' (Innocent's Journey) painting.
