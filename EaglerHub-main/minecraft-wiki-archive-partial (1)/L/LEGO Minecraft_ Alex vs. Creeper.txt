LEGO Minecraft: Alex vs. Creeper is an activity book themed after Lego Minecraft, revolving around a conflict between Alex and a creeper.

Summary
“Fantastic, creative LEGO® and Minecraft® adventures are always with you in this deluxe Activity Box featuring an Alex LEGO minifigure with a Creeper and accessories!Alex takes on an exploding Creeper, but can she survive even with her trusty sword and shield? Dive into the adventures with this LEGO® Minecraft® Activity Box Set that features minifigures of some of Minecraft’s bravest heroes and most hostile mobs who can battle it out again and again against the included full-color backdrop! An activity book and guidebook add to the fun—and make this Deluxe Novelty Box a great gift idea for LEGO and Minecraft fans.„
