A blaze is a brick-built figure from the LEGO Minecraft theme introduced in August 15, 2015.

Appearance
The blaze is mostly yellow, with a specially molded head and five blaze rods in the color. In addition to these pieces, two black knobbed 1×1 bricks are used to connect the blaze rods and the head, as well as a transparent stud to give the illusion of levitation.

Background
Blazes are consistently shown in the Nether, usually within Nether fortresses. The mob embodies heat, with a fiery temper and pyrokinetic abilities, enabling them to levitate several studs above ground.
