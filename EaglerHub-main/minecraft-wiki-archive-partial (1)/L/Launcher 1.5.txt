1.5 is a major update to the Minecraft Launcher released on July 28, 2014.

Contents

1 Changes
2 Navigation

Changes
"Development Console" tab renamed to "Launcher Log".
The player can now run multiple instances of Minecraft at once, provided they're on different users.
"Play" button renamed to "Already Playing..." if Minecraft is open while looking at launcher.
New tab called "Game Output <your-username>" appears after launching Minecraft.
