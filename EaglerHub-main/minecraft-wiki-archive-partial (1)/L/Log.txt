A log or stem is a naturally occurring block found in trees or huge fungi, primarily used as a building block and to create planks, a versatile crafting ingredient. There are currently nine types of logs: oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, mangrove, cherry, and pale oak; and two types of stems: crimson and warped. The upcoming third drop of 2026 introduces poplar as the tenth log type.

Natural generation
All types of logs generate naturally as part of trees, although fallen trees can only contain oak, spruce, birch, jungle, or poplar.​[upcoming: Third Drop 2026] Azalea trees are generated with oak logs. Additionally, one jungle log generates for each jungle bush. Stems generate naturally as part of huge fungi.

Post-generation
Logs and stems
Trees can be grown using saplings or azalea, and huge fungi can be grown using small Nether fungi.

Smelting ingredient

Name
Ingredients
Smelting recipe

Charcoal

Any Log +Any fuel

0.15

Stems cannot be smelted into charcoal.

Fuel
Logs, but not stems, can be used as a fuel in furnaces, smelting 1.5 items per block.

Stripping
Using an axe on a log or stem strips it, turning it into a stripped log or a stripped stem, which act the same as regular logs or stems.

Cocoa beans
Cocoa beans can be placed on the side of jungle logs to grow a new cocoa pod.
