Elizabeth "Lizzie" Batty, better known by her username LDShadowLady, is an English YouTuber known for her Minecraft playthroughs.

In the Minecraft franchise
In 2016, she performed as a fictionalized version of herself in Minecraft: Story Mode, taking part in a murder mystery storyline alongside several other YouTubers.
In 2021, she was one of several YouTubers selected to appear in the YouTube Creators Skin Pack, celebrating a trillion views of Minecraft content on YouTube. This same design was reused for her depiction in Caves & Cliffs Explorers map, where she appeared as an NPC.
In 2024, she joined MC Championship to participate in the Ender Cup, under the team the Aqua Axolotls. During the competition, she played alongside jojosolos, Shubble, and Aphmau.
In 2025, she made a cameo appearance in A Minecraft Movie alongside several other creators as Auction Attendees at an auction for Steve's belongings. Prior to being officially cast in the film, she posted about taking a trip to New Zealand.

Trivia
In A Portal to Mystery, she is not credited by her full name but instead as "LDSHADOWLADY as Herself", unlike other cast members.
