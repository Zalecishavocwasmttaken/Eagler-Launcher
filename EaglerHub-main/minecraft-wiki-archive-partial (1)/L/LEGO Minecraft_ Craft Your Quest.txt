LEGO Minecraft: Craft Your Quest is a LEGO Minecraft book about the character Ori’s adventure. It is a choose your own adventure book where you use the given pieces to create useful items and objects to help Ori. The book will be published on October 8, 2026 by the publisher "Buster Books".

Synopsis
“Embark on your very own LEGO® Minecraft™ adventure with this awesome set filled with LEGO pieces, stickers, puzzles and loads more.Build your exclusive Ori minifigure before making your way through an exciting choose-your-path journey. Read the comic story, complete the puzzles and decide what Ori should do next after each stage. With multiple paths to choose from, you can play again and again and discover a new story each time.Along the way are building challenges, so you can use your LEGO pieces to create useful objects and items that will assist Ori on his way. Help him practice his mining skills, defend him from hostile mobs, build a minecart and much more. There’s even a cute cat to build!As your adventure progresses, don’t forget to use your stickers on the cool inventory poster to keep track of the items you find along the way.Young fans of LEGO Minecraft will love this opportunity to bring their favourite characters to life through imaginative play, fun activities and engaging reading.„ — Book description
