Daniel "Kappische" Kaplan was Mojang's business developer from October 2010 to July 2017.

Biography
Kaplan was born and raised in Skövde, Sweden. He founded ludosity.com.  After leaving Mojang, he worked at Coffee Stain Studios.

Games:
