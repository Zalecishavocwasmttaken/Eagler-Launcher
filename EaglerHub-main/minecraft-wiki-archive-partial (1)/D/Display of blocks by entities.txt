Enderman/minecart appearences
From 14w04a to 14w21b certain blocks in minecarts would look quite strange, applying a series of textures to a cuboid model rather than using geometry accurate to the block itself. In 14w25a and 14w25b changes were made such that the block's actual model is used, although in the first of these two snapshots the missing model was used for many of these instead.
Blocks are only listed in this section if their pre-14w25a appearance visually differs from their placed block form.
