Obtaining
Harvesting
Dragon's breath can be obtained by using empty glass bottles on the ender dragon's breath attack while perched, or on the purple effect clouds caused by dragon fireballs.

Usage
Brewing ingredient
Dragon's breath may be added to any splash potion to upgrade it to a lingering potion, which can be used to create a lasting area effect cloud, or to craft tipped arrows.

Name
Ingredients
Brewing recipe

Lingering Potion

Dragon's Breath + Any Splash Potion

With two or more dragon's breath in the brewing stand, after the brewing process, a glass bottle drops as an entity. However, with one dragon's breath in the brewing stand, the glass bottle is consumed and cannot be retrieved.​[until: JE 26.3]
