Generation
Deep lukewarm oceans replace deep oceans in the moderately high temperature zones, bordering lukewarm oceans and mushroom fields. They are twice as deep as regular oceans, and border deep oceans near lower temperatures, or regular warm oceans that are twice as deep near higher temperatures.

Description
Since they are a deep ocean variant, they can generate ocean monuments and have more tall seagrass. In any other aspect, they are the same as regular lukewarm oceans, with a sand floor and warm fish.
With Vibrant Visuals, deep lukewarm oceans use a coolish humid volumetric fog setting, which slightly fades the distance in an orange tint.
