Dungeons Hero Skin Pack, also known as Minecraft Dungeons Hero Skin Pack, is a skin pack consisting of 13 skins of heroes from Minecraft Dungeons. At some point it was delisted, but relisted on August 27, 2026[verify] in a promo for Minecraft Dungeons II.
