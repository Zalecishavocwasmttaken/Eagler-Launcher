A displacement wand is an item only present in 25w14craftmine. Its description reads: "Jera Isaz Berkanan".

Obtaining
The displacement wand is only obtainable without commands by getting the upgrade "Enigma", but by default this upgrade is obfuscated. Killing an enderman makes this upgrade readable.

Usage
It has different properties from the normal version; instead of following a curved trajectory, it travels in a straight line when fired, and when the player is teleported, they take no damage.
