The dark oak is a type of tree with dark bark and wood, being a darker variant of oak. Dark oaks have thick 2×2 trunks that grow thicker near the top, and are found only in the dark forest biome.

Appearance
Dark oak trees generate with thick 2×2 trunks. Dirt blocks appear under the trunk if generated on a steep cliff. Dark oak trees nearly always generate with irregular logs connected to the trunk – these represent large branches. Covered by a wide canopy, many dark oaks together can increase darkness significantly.
Dark oak trees with trunks consisting of 1×1 dark oak logs, instead of 2×2, can be found in two types of rooms in woodland mansions: the tree chopping room, and the nature room. These are not generated elsewhere in the world, and cannot be grown from saplings.

Dark oaks with vines
Main article: Tree with Vines


This feature is exclusive to Bedrock Edition. 

Some dark oaks may be covered with vines on 6/7 of all logs of the main trunk, not the branches, but sometimes even replacing leaves. Dark oaks grown from saplings also have a chance to be covered with vines.

Generation
Dark oaks in a dark forest, together with some regular oaks
Dark oaks generate naturally only in the dark forest biome. A dark oak always generates with dirt under its trunk, even if it spawns partly or wholly over air or water blocks.
The ground surrounding naturally-generated dark oaks is covered by leaf litter.

Growing these trees requires four dark oak saplings arranged in a 2×2 grid; they do not grow if planted individually. Dark oak trees grow at a much faster rate than most other trees. They require a 3×3 column of unobstructed space at least 7 blocks above the northwestern sapling to grow (8 blocks including the sapling itself). Additionally, dark oak trees require 5×5 layers without obstruction for the top 3 layers of its final height. No horizontal clearance is needed at the base of the tree (saplings planted in a 2×2 hole 1 block deep still grow).
