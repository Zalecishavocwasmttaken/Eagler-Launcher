[Int] minecraft:damage: The number of uses consumed (not remaining) of the item's durability. Must be a non-negative integer, defaults to 0. If not present, the item cannot take damage. If this component is present on an item, its value is non-zero, the minecraft:max_damage is also present on the item, and the player hovering over the item has advanced tooltips on, then white text is added to the tooltip showing "Durability: <durability> / <max_damage>".
