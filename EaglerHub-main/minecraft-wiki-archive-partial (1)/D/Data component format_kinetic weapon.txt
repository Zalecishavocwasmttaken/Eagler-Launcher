Notes

 The damage dealt is calculated as floor(relative_speed * damage_multiplier), where relative_speed is the difference of speed vectors of the attacker and the target as projected onto the axis of the attacker's view vector. Any additional damage from enchantments or attribute modifiers is added after this calculation.
