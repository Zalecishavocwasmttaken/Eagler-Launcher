In the Minecraft franchise
During Java Edition Alpha he made several unofficial trailers such as "Minecraft Fan-Made Trailer", "Minecraft Fanmade Teaser", "Minecraft Fanmade Trailer v2" and "This Is Minecraft", among other videos.
These videos became very popular in the early Minecraft community, and Notch put "This Is Minecraft" on the front page of Minecraft.net for approximately a year between late 2010 and late 2011, making it the first official trailer for Minecraft. Vareide then went on to create the Official Minecraft Trailer.
Vareide continued to make more Minecraft videos on his channel until 2014. He then pivoted to making other videos elsewhere that were not Minecraft-related. His original channel was later renamed to "This channel is not active". In 2022, he would appear in an episode of Meet a Minecrafter, reflecting on his career.
