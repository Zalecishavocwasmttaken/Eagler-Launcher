Mob loot
Zombies, husks, zombie villagers‌[Java Edition  only], skeletons, strays, bogged, and parched have a chance to spawn wearing armor on Normal and Hard difficulty, with a 0.04% chance of it being diamond armor. If killed by a player, there is an 8.5% chance (9.5% with Looting I, 10.5% with Looting II and 11.5% with Looting III) for the mob to drop the armor they're wearing upon death. The dropped armor has a random durability and retains any enchantments it has.

Damage reduction
Main article: Armor § Damage reductionWearing armor reduces incoming damage according to two values: armor, and armor toughness. Each piece of armor provides a given armor and armor toughness value, and the values from each armor piece are added together when calculating damage reduction.
The strength of incoming damage also determines the damage reduction of worn armor, as armor does not protect in a linear fashion.
