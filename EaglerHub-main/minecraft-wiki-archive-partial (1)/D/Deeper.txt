Track description
Previews of tracks in Minecraft have been shortened to 30 seconds on this wiki in accordance with fair use rationale. "11" and "Magnetic Circuit" are exempt from this.

"Deeper" is a song written by Lena Raine to bring a creepy vibe to the deep dark, dripstone caves, or sulfur caves. It also samples the leitmotif of Infinite Amethyst. The song features a piano, later joined by very quick sequences of drum beats, along synth chords and pads.

Windows: Content\data\resource_packs\vanilla_music\sounds\music\game
All music files are in Ogg Vorbis.
